## 1. Verdict

1. **U=<y1> is canonically the fixed line of the Sp_{40}(Z_p)-action on K_+.**
   This is an exact representation-theoretic identification, conditional only on
   the Kondo 2026 Definition 3.2/Lemma 3.3 embedding diag(1,A). It does not need
   a choice of basis beyond that embedding.

2. **This fixed-line identification does NOT prove 1 in U or U=W^perp.**
   The fixed line is intrinsic as a line, but its relation to the unit 1 and to
   the trace pairing is extra integral data. Kondo 2025 Theorem E gives
   W=ker Tr as a functional and Kondo 2026 gives the Sp action; neither gives
   the trace pairing x,y->Tr(xy), Tr(y1), or the coordinate of 1 in U.

3. **Exact equivalence:** for the trace pairing,

       W^perp = Q_p 1,   U=W^perp  <=>  U=Q_p1  <=>  1 in U.

   The trace computation is exact; the premise 1 in U is not supplied.

4. **Exact missing reference/data:** there is no theorem in Kondo 2025/2026 or
   the frozen C9 corpus identifying U with Q_p1 or W^perp. The missing statement
   is an integral duality/normalization theorem, not a citation that can be
   extracted from the existing Kondo statements. Task-217 models show the
   recorded data admit both U=Q_p1 and U!=Q_p1.

## 2. U as the Sp fixed line

Kondo 2026 Definition 3.2, for odd d=2g+1, embeds Sp_{2g}(Z_p) in
GL_d(Q_p) by A -> diag(1,A) in the y-basis y1,...,y_d. Let

    K = U direct-sum W, U=<y1>_{Q_p}, W=<y2,...,y_d>_{Q_p}.       (2.1)

The action is:

    alpha_A(y1)=y1,  alpha_A|W = A (A in Sp_{2g}(Z_p)).           (2.2)

### Proposition

    U = Fix(Sp_{2g}(Z_p)) := {v in K : alpha_A(v)=v for all A}.   (2.3)

Proof.

- Every u in U is fixed by (2.2).
- Let v=u+w with u in U,w in W be fixed by all A. Since U is fixed,
  w is fixed by all A in Sp_{2g}(Z_p).
- For g>=1, -I in Sp_{2g}(Z_p) (p is odd and -I is symplectic). Then
  A=-I gives w=-w, hence 2w=0. Since 2 is invertible in Q_p, w=0.
- Thus v=u in U. QED.

This is exact and uses only the Kondo embedding plus -I in Sp. It is
independent of the y-basis choice, in the sense that the fixed subspace of a
given representation is canonical. In particular U is not an arbitrary
complement of W chosen by hand; it is the unique Sp-fixed line in the Kondo
action.

Note: this identification does not say U is the image of a subgroup of G_K.
U is a line in K_+=K, the additive reconstruction; the Sp action is on K_+.
The fixed-line description is the canonical group-theoretic description of U
available from the Kondo action.

## 3. The trace orthogonal complement

Let beta(x,y)=Tr_{K/Q_p}(xy). It is a nondegenerate symmetric Q_p-bilinear form.
Kondo 2025 Theorem E gives, for odd d in the tame totally ramified case,

    W=ker Tr=Q_p-span(pi,...,pi^40),  Tr(y1)!=0.                  (3.1)

For every w in W, beta(1,w)=Tr(w)=0; hence 1 in W^perp. Since dim W=40 and
beta is nondegenerate, dim W^perp=1, so

    W^perp = Q_p 1.                                                (3.2)

Therefore

    U=W^perp
      <=> Q_p y1 = Q_p 1
      <=> y1 in Q_p 1
      <=> 1 in U.                                                 (3.3)

This equivalence is exact. It does not prove its own premise: Kondo gives no
information that y1 lies in Q_p 1. The trace theorem gives only W=kerTr and
Tr(y1) nonzero, not the value of Tr(y1) or the line U.

## 4. Attempts from the reconstruction/mapping-class action

The following attempts fail:

1. **Mapping-class D fixed y1.** task-165 says D fixes x1 hence y1 in k_+.
   This gives a fixed vector in U, not 1 in U; D's action cannot distinguish
   U=Q_p1 from another line. The mapping-class source does not supply the
   integral coordinate of y1.

2. **Sp fixed line.** Proposition 2.3 identifies U as the fixed line, but the
   fixed line can be any complement to W. The trace line Q_p1 is a particular
   complement (W^perp); neither Kondo Lemma1.6/Def3.2/Lemma3.3 nor Kondo 2025
   Theorem E says the Sp-fixed line is the trace-orthogonal complement.

3. **Trace functional.** W=kerTr determines W and the line W^perp=Q_p1, but
   not U. Knowing W and Tr(y1) nonzero does not tell whether y1 is a multiple
   of 1; the trace functional is not the trace pairing and does not compare
   y1 with 1.

4. **F definition.** Kondo 2025 lines 363-366 define F via local reciprocity,
   uniformizer projection, and p-adic log; y1=F(x1) in J=m. This gives y1 in m
   but not y1 in Q_p1; y1=p is in m and spans Q_p1, while y1=p+pi is also in m
   and spans another line. No source value distinguishes them.

5. **Integral lattice.** U is not shown to be a Z_p-lattice line; y1 in J=m
   means y1 is integral but Q_p y1 is a Q_p-line, not a Z_p-line. The Q_p-line
   U can equal Q_p1 only if y1 is a nonzero Q_p multiple of 1.

No attempt proves 1 in U or U=W^perp from the Kondo reconstruction.

## 5. Exact missing reference/data

Kondo 2025/2026 contains no statement of the form

    U = Q_p 1,  or  1 in U,  or  U = (ker Tr)^perp under x,y->Tr(xy).

The exact missing datum is an **integral duality/normalization theorem**
identifying the Sp-fixed line (or the line of y1) with the trace-orthogonal
complement of W, equivalently with Q_p1. In the frozen corpus this is not
supplied by:

- Kondo 2025 Theorem D (generators/relations only);
- Kondo 2025 Theorem E/1.3 (W=kerTr functional only);
- Kondo 2026 Lemma1.6 (Q_p-linear/closed Phi only);
- Kondo 2026 Definition3.2/Lemma3.3 (diag(1,A) and Sp containment only);
- Kondo 2025 F definition (reciprocity/uniformizer/log, no trace pairing);
- the mapping-class/Dehn-twist construction (fixes y1, not its relation to 1).

The missing reference would have to prove one of the equivalent statements
(3.3). No such reference appears in the frozen source set; if one is added, it
must also explain how the trace pairing is recovered from the Q_p-linear
reconstruction, since Kondo does not include the ring multiplication.

## 8. Conclusion

Kondo's U=<y1> is canonically the fixed line of the Sp_{40}(Z_p)-action by the
exact representation argument (including -I in Sp). That is the strongest
canonical identification supported by the source. It does not prove 1 in U or
U=W^perp: the trace pairing gives W^perp=Q_p1, and U=W^perp is equivalent to
1 in U, which remains an extra integral-duality datum absent from Kondo and the
frozen corpus. Therefore H_A-full/B=O_K remains conditional on 1 in U; no
unconditional branch conclusion follows.
