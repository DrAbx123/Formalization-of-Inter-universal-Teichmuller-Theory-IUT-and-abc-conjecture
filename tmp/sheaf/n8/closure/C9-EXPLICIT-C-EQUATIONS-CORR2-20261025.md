## 2. Corrected local p relation q/pi/qhat/E[41]

Let q be the actual Tate parameter at p=12301. From E_j, v_p(Delta)=6, v_p(j)=-6, so v_p(q)=6 and q belongs to Q_p^× with |q|_p<1; write

    q = p^6 u,   u in 1 + p Z_p (indeed q/p^6 in 1+p^6 Z_p).        (2.1)

The 82nd-power map is an automorphism of the pro-p group 1+pZ_p (p=12301 does not divide 82). Let v in 1+pZ_p be the unique element with

    v^82 = q/p^6.                                                   (2.2)

Let pi be a uniformizer of the E[41]-local field K_v with pi^41 = p (the Kummer generator of the 41-tower). Define

    qhat := pi^3 v.                                                 (2.3)

Then

    qhat^82 = pi^(3*82) v^82 = (pi^41)^6 (q/p^6) = p^6 * q/p^6 = q,   (2.4)
    qhat^2 = pi^6 v^2 = q^(1/41).                                   (2.5)

The correct interpretation:
- qhat is an 82nd root of q. It lies in K_v, not in Q_p: qhat has valuation 3/41, while q has valuation 6.
- qhat^2=q^{1/41} is the 41st root of q used for the E[41]-torsion; it has valuation 6/41 and lies in K_v.
- K_v = F_v(pi) is the totally ramified degree-41 extension (e=41, f=1) generated by pi with pi^41=p; q=pi^246 v^82, and pi^6 v^2=q^{1/41}.
- The local q-order is b=3: qhat=pi^3 v with v a unit, so qhat has the same valuation and residue as pi^3; the order condition 41 does not divide b=3 holds. The unit v is determined by q and is not arbitrary; it does not change b or the C_v type.
- The previous sentence "q has an 82nd root in Q_p" is wrong if read as qhat in Q_p; the 82nd root exists only after adjoining pi (valuation 3/41). The previous "q^{1/41}=pi^{6/41}"/exponent confusion is replaced by (2.5): q^{1/41}=pi^6 v^2.

At other bad places (the M-primes), the local E[41]-extension has e=41, f=1 for the same Kummer reason; their q-parameter valuations are those of E_j at those primes (potential good additive; after F_sem they become good and are removed from Vbad). They do not contribute to the p b=3 data.
