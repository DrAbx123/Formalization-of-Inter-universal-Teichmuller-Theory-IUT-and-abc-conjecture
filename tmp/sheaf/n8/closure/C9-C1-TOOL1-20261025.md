## 1. What Tool I claims

C9-C1-NEW-TOOLS Tool I:

    C_s = VC(LS)  (small/log-shell container),
    C_l = VC(I)   (large container),
    C_l^Q = C_l tensor_Z Q,
    L : C_l^Q -> C_l^Q,  U |-> LGP*U,
    S^Q := L^{-1}(S_q) in C_l^Q,
    L_s : C_s -> C_l^Q,  S |-> LGP*S.

T1: Is the image L_s(C_s) dense in a neighborhood of [S_q] in C_l^Q?
T2: Is L_s continuous?

## 2. Verdict

Tool I is NOT well-defined from the source, and its density claim is false
under the natural compactness/continuity hypotheses.

Two independent problems:

(P1) There is no source-defined map C_s -> C_l^Q. C_s and C_l are different
     volume containers; the source does not provide a canonical inclusion or
     diagonal embedding of the small/log-shell container into the large
     container. L_s is therefore an undefined restriction; Tool I must
     postulate an embedding, which is an extra assumption.

(P2) Even after postulating an embedding, if C_s is compact and L_s is
     continuous into a Hausdorff C_l^Q, then L_s(C_s) is compact, hence
     closed. A closed proper subset is not dense. If S^Q is not in the image,
     then S_q is not in the image and there is an open neighborhood of S_q
     disjoint from L_s(C_s); density near [S_q] fails. If S^Q is in the image,
     one has an exact admissible/preimage S, so the approximation route is
     unnecessary; but that exact case is exactly what the source lacks.

Thus Tool I does not provide the approximation mechanism it proposes.

## 4. Continuity of L on C_l^Q

If one works entirely on C_l^Q, then

    L : C_l^Q -> C_l^Q,  U |-> LGP*U

is multiplication by the fixed element LGP. This is a linear endomorphism of
the Q-vector space C_l^Q.

- If LGP is nonzero (all components nonzero: q_uv^{j^2} != 0, 1 != 0), then L
  is a linear automorphism of C_l^Q.
- On a finite-dimensional Q-vector space with its standard topology, L is
  continuous; its inverse is also continuous.
- The constant Haar Jacobian statement is about the action on the large
  log-shell container, not about L_s:C_s->C_l^Q.

So continuity of L on C_l^Q is acceptable conditionally; continuity of L_s
requires a topology and an embedding of C_s, neither of which is in the
source.

## 5. Density failure

Assume the minimal hypotheses needed for the density question:

(H1) an embedding iota : C_s -> C_l^Q;
(H2) L_s = L circ iota is continuous;
(H3) C_s is compact;
(H4) C_l^Q is Hausdorff.

Then K := L_s(C_s) is compact, hence closed in C_l^Q. Since C_l^Q is a
Q-vector space of positive dimension, K is not all of C_l^Q. Therefore K is a
closed proper subset and is not dense.

If S_q = L(S^Q) is not in K, then S_q lies in the open complement of K; there
is a neighborhood of S_q disjoint from the image. Hence the image is not dense
near S_q.

If S_q is in K, then there exists S in C_s with L_s(S)=S_q, i.e. an exact
preimage in the small container. That is the exact solution the approximation
route was trying to avoid; in that case Tool I is not needed (and the exact
solution would still need admissibility of S for eta^anab).

Thus, under compactness/continuity, Tool I's density claim is refuted. If C_s
is not compact, then Tool III's compactness exactification has no basis; no
density proof is available.

## 6. Volume-class variant

Tool I is stated at the level of C_l^Q, but its use in the eta route is via
the volume class in R^ss. One might weaken density to:

    the set of volume classes [L_s(S_q)] for S in C_s is dense near [S_q].

If C_s is compact and the log-volume map vol : C_l^Q -> R is continuous, then
vol(L_s(C_s)) is compact, hence closed and bounded in R. It cannot be dense in
a neighborhood of a value not in it. If the volume value Q is not attained, a
closed bounded image misses an open interval; densities near Q fail. If Q is
attained, the exact S exists.

If vol is not continuous or C_s is not compact, no source theorem gives the
required density.

## 7. Exact missing definitions

Tool I lacks:

(D1) a source-defined embedding C_s -> C_l^Q (small/log-shell to large
     rationalized container);
(D2) a topology on C_s and C_l^Q compatible with the LGP action and Haar
     volume;
(D3) proof that C_s is compact in that topology (the noncompact small volume
     container \prod K_uv is not compact; only the log-shell version may be
     compact);
(D4) proof that L_s is continuous and that its image is not closed proper;
(D5) proof that S^Q=L^{-1}(S_q) lies in or is approximated by C_s;
(D6) the admissibility of S for eta^anab, separate from density.

None is in LANA 9.2 or IUT xi-d.

## 8. Conclusion

Tool I is not a valid source construction: L_s:C_s->C_l^Q is not defined
without an extra embedding, and under the natural compactness/continuity
hypotheses its image is compact closed, hence not dense near a point outside
it. The density claim is refuted in the compact Hausdorff setting; without
compactness, no proof is available.

Consequently Tool I does not establish an admissible approximation to S^Q or
the eta equality. C1 remains open.
