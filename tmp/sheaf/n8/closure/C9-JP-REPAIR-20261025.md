## 2. What a D-prime-strip isomorphism does give

An isomorphism of D-prime-strips gives an equivalence of the underlying
tempered/Galois categories. Via pi_1 reconstruction it induces an outer
automorphism of G_K (and label/cusp permutation data). It does not select:

  - a field automorphism K -> K;
  - a uniformizer pi or the lattice J=pi O_K;
  - an additive map J -> J.

JP is the missing statement:

    (JP) Every D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism occurring in full Ind1 acts on the
         local factor by an Aut_{Z_p}(J) lattice automorphism of J=pi O_K
         (not necessarily by O_K^times).

JP would suffice for L_B invariance; unit action is not needed and is false
(the symplectic H action on J_W is non-scalar). But JP is an extra naturality/
log-shell compatibility theorem, not a consequence of Def4.1/Def4.10/Prop4.11.

Why H_A-lite is insufficient: H_A-lite/task-196 proves J/I preservation only
for H=Phi^{-1}(Sp_{2g}(Z_p)). Full Ind1 is larger and includes arbitrary
capsule-full poly-isomorphisms. H_A-full for full Out/Ind1 is open; E30/E33
show the principal filtration is not preserved by full Out. The W subset J/E24
issue showed the additive integral lift is not automatic.
