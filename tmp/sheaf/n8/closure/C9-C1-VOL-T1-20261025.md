## 1. Verdict

H1 of the volume-scaling route is NOT a source theorem.

The claimed map

    T_1 : VC(LS) -> VC(I),   S |-> 1·S

is not defined by the LANA report. In LANA, the action of 1 in N acts by the
LGP element on the LARGE log-shell volume container VC(I), not on the
small/log-shell container VC(LS). The only source-defined map is

    VC(I) -> VC(I),   U |-> LGP · U,                            (1.1)

which is a measurable bijection with constant Haar Jacobian when LGP is
nonzero. It is not a map from VC(LS) to VC(I).

Consequences:

- G1 (T_1 measurable bijection with constant Jacobian from VC(LS) to VC(I)):
  REJECT as source; H1 is an extra assumption.
- The scaling route's S in VC(LS) has no source-defined image 1·S in VC(I);
  the volume comparison [T_1(S)] = [S_q] is not justified.
- G3 (admissibility of the volume-selected S for eta^anab) is also missing:
  arbitrary measurable S of prescribed volume need not be admissible in the
  sense of LANA 9.2.
- The route does not establish eta equality or C1; C1 remains open.

## 3. Is T_1 a measurable bijection with constant Jacobian?

### 3.1 On VC(I): conditional YES

If one defines

    T_1^I : VC(I) -> VC(I),   U |-> LGP · U,

then T_1^I is multiplication by the fixed nonzero element LGP of the
additive group VC(I). For any additive Haar measure, translation/dilation by a
fixed nonzero group element is a measurable bijection whose measure scaling is
the constant Haar modulus |LGP|. Thus

    vol(T_1^I(U)) = J · vol(U),   J = |LGP|,                    (3.1)

provided LGP is nonzero and the Haar measure is fixed. This is the standard
measure-scaling property of Haar measure.

But this is a map on VC(I), not on VC(LS).

### 3.2 On VC(LS): NO source definition

The route requires

    T_1 : VC(LS) -> VC(I),   S |-> 1·S.                          (3.2)

LANA does not define such a map. Problems:

(P1) VC(LS) and VC(I) are different containers (small/log-shell versus large
     log-shell tensor product). There is no canonical inclusion or
     measure-preserving identification VC(LS) -> VC(I) in the source.

(P2) The LGP action is defined on VC(I) by multiplication. Restricting it to
     VC(LS) would require an embedding of VC(LS) into VC(I), a choice not in
     LANA. Different embeddings give different images/volumes.

(P3) Even if one uses an embedding, the constant-Jacobian property on VC(I)
     does not automatically descend to a constant-Jacobian map from VC(LS):
     the Haar measure on VC(LS) and the induced measure from VC(I) need not
     agree. No source proves compatibility.

(P4) The source's "1·S" is the action of N on the LGP element after S is used
     as integral structure. It is not the set-theoretic multiplication of an
     arbitrary subset S of a measure space by a fixed scalar.

Therefore H1 is not a consequence of the LANA formulas; it is a new
measure-theoretic assumption. G1 REJECT as source.

## 4. Admissibility of S (G3)

Even if one postulates T_1, the route chooses S by volume alone. LANA 9.2
requires:

- S subset VC(LS_uv), the log-shell/small container;
- [1·S] adelic and measurable in R^ss;
- compatibility with the anabelian N-action and the fixed BPS/Hodge-theater
  isomorphism (subject to Ind1/Ind2).

A measurable subset of prescribed volume need not satisfy these conditions.
The volume-scaling file's G3 acknowledges this but does not solve it.

Thus even under H1, the eta equality would require an additional admissibility
theorem for the volume-selected S. G3 REJECT as proved.

## 5. Lemma B and volume existence (G2)

Lemma B assumes VC(LS) has measurable subsets of every volume in
(0, vol_total) and that the measure is non-atomic. LANA defines Haar measures
on volume containers, but does not state this intermediate-value/non-atomic
property for VC(LS), nor a normalization of vol_total. The existence of
subsets of every prescribed volume is a standard consequence for non-atomic
finite measures if the measure is non-atomic and finite; however:

- the route does not prove non-atomicity/finiteness for VC(LS);
- the measure normalization and the relation to vol(S_q) are not specified.

G2 is plausible but not source-proved.

## 6. G4 and pilot classes

Assuming T_1 and S with vol(T_1(S))=V_q, one gets [T_1(S)]=[S_q] in R^ss.
But:

- the equality is only at the level of volume classes, not at the level of
  admissible integral structures or eta^anab definitions;
- q-pilot target pilot is [S_q] in the large container; T_1(S) is in the image
  of the assumed map, whose container/codomain are not source-defined;
- the scaling J and V_q are only defined up to normalization; the file does not
  fix a common normalization.

G4 is conditional on H1/G2/G3 and is not a source statement.

## 7. Exact audit conclusion

G1: REJECT. T_1 is not a source map VC(LS)->VC(I). The source action is on
    VC(I) and is a measurable bijection with constant Haar Jacobian there.
G2: NOT PROVED. Non-atomicity/finiteness and existence of subsets of every
    volume in VC(LS) are extra measure-theoretic assumptions.
G3: REJECT. Volume selection does not imply LANA admissibility/N-action
    compatibility.
G4: CONDITIONAL. Volume class equality is not eta equality.
G5/Bridge: not addressed by the scaling route; C1 remains open.

## 8. What would make the route valid

A valid volume-scaling route would need:

(V1) a source-defined measurable map T_1 : VC(LS) -> VC(I) (or a canonical
     embedding VC(LS) -> VC(I)) compatible with the LGP/N-action;
(V2) proof that T_1 has constant Haar Jacobian J and that the measures on
     source and target are normalized compatibly;
(V3) a construction of S in the LANA eta domain (not merely measurable) with
     vol(S)=V_q/J;
(V4) proof that [T_1(S)]=[S_q] in the correct R^ss quotient and that the
     resulting eta^anab_S is defined and pointed-linear;
(V5) the bridge from eta equality to an actual output D0 with logVol(D0)=Q.

None of V1-V5 is in the source or in the reviewed file. The scaling route
therefore does not establish C1.

## 9. Conclusion

The 1-in-N LGP map is a source action on the large log-shell volume container
VC(I), where it is (conditionally) a measurable bijection with constant Haar
Jacobian. It is NOT a source-defined map T_1:VC(LS)->VC(I). The volume-scaling
route's H1 is an extra assumption, and its volume-selected S is not shown to
be admissible for eta^anab_S. G1/G3 are rejected; C1 remains open.
