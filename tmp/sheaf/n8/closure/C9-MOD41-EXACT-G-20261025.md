## 0. Result

1. The subgroup generated by the two corrected Frobenius classes at l=37
   and l=127 is exactly the determinant preimage of the order-10 subgroup
   of F41^× generated by 37 and 4. It contains SL2(F41) and has order
   68,880 * 10 = 688,800; it is not GL2.
2. Adding the exact Frobenius class at l=7, whose determinant 7 has order
   40 in F41^×, makes the determinant image full. Since the two classes
   already force SL2(F41) subset G, the full image over Q is

       G = GL2(F_41),   |G| = 2,755,200,   transitive.

3. Over F=Q(i,E[6],F_sem): F/Q is unramified at 41, so F cap Q(zeta_41)=Q
   and the determinant over F is all of F41^×. Conditional on [F:Q] being
   2,3-smooth, the same SL2-transfer applies, hence

       H = image(G_F) = GL2(F_41),   |H| = 2,755,200.

If [F:Q] has a prime 5 or 7, the SL2-transfer is not justified; the
determinant over F is still full, but H could be a proper determinant-full
subgroup. The semistabilization degree remains conditional.

## 3. Exact subgroup generated by the two classes

The determinants of these classes are 37 and 4 in F41^×. Exact orders:

    ord_41(37)=5,   ord_41(4)=10.

Thus the determinant subgroup D0=<37,4> has order lcm(5,10)=10; 4 already
has order 10, so D0 is cyclic of order 10.

Let G0 be the subgroup generated by any representatives of the two
Frobenius classes. By (2.1), SL2(F41) subset G0. Its determinant image is
D0. Therefore

    G0 = det^{-1}(D0) = {g in GL2(F41): det(g) in D0},
    |G0| = |SL2(F41)| * |D0| = 68,880 * 10 = 688,800.        (3.1)

This is independent of the chosen representatives: a subgroup containing
SL2 and having determinant image D0 is exactly the preimage of D0. In
particular the two displayed classes alone do NOT generate GL2.

## 4. Full determinant from the l=7 class

l=7 is a good prime for E_j: D=1-1728p^6 is nonzero mod7 and the
discriminant numerator 2,985,984 is nonzero mod7. The exact class at l=7:

    #E(F_7)=11,  a_7=-3,  t_7=38,  d_7=7 mod41.

The determinant of Frobenius at l is d_l=l mod41 (Weil pairing/standard
Galois determinant; exact and independent of point counting). Since

    ord_41(7)=40,

the determinant image contains an element of order 40, hence is all of
F41^×. Combined with SL2(F41) subset G, this gives

    G = GL2(F_41),   |G| = 2,755,200.                        (4.1)

Equivalently, for any h in GL2(F41), choose g in G with det(g)=det(h);
then g^{-1}h is in SL2 subset G, so h is in G. The image is transitive on
nonzero vectors because it contains SL2.

The old task-205 determinant claim used only l=37/127, whose determinant
subgroup has order 10; the l=7 class repairs the determinant step. The
old numerical values a37=-6/a127=12 and ord(37)=40 were still wrong and
remain corrected in §1/§3.

## 5. Exact H=image(G_F)

Let H=image(G_F) and let d=[F:Q]. Since F/Q is Galois, H is normal in G and
[G:H] divides d.

### 5.1 Determinant over F is full

F=Q(i,E[6],F_sem). We check that F/Q is unramified at 41:

- E_j has good reduction at 41: D mod41 = -5 and 2,985,984 mod41 = 36,
  both nonzero; so the E[6] splitting field is unramified at 41.
- Q(i) is ramified only at 2.
- F_sem is the semistabilization at the bad primes 2,3,p=12301 and the
  D-primes; 41 is not among them.

Thus F/Q is unramified at 41. Q(zeta_41)/Q is ramified only at 41. Their
intersection F cap Q(zeta_41) is unramified at 41 (inside F) and at every
p != 41 (inside Q(zeta_41)), hence unramified everywhere over Q; by
Minkowski it is Q. Therefore the cyclotomic character

    chi_41: G_Q -> F41^×

is still surjective on G_F, so

    det(H)=F41^×.                                           (5.1)

This uses only the good reduction at 41 and the standard ramification of
Q(zeta_41); no U/B normalization.

### 5.2 SL2 in H and exact H

Assume [F:Q] is 2,3-smooth (the E_j completion's semistabilization degree
input; exact degree not computed here).

If SL2(F41) were not contained in H, then K=H cap SL2(F41) would be a proper
normal subgroup of SL2(F41). Since PSL2(F41) is simple, K is 1 or {+-I}.
The quotient SL2(F41)/K embeds in G/H, so [G:H] is divisible by 68,880 or
34,440. Both contain the primes 5,7,41, while [G:H] divides the
2,3-smooth d=[F:Q]; contradiction. Hence SL2(F41) subset H.

With (5.1), det(H)=F41^× and SL2(F41) subset H. The same argument as in
§4 gives

    H = GL2(F_41),   |H| = 2,755,200,                       (5.2)

conditional only on [F:Q] being 2,3-smooth. Without that degree condition,
(5.1) still holds but H may be a proper determinant-full subgroup.
