## 1. Unit/log structure

For the tame e=41<p-1 type, p-adic log gives a Z_p-module isomorphism
    log: U^1=1+m -> J=m.                                             (1.1)
(Indeed log is an analytic group isomorphism U^1->m, and both sides are Z_p-modules; the inverse exp converges on m.) The unit group decomposes
    O_K^x = mu_{q-1} x U^1,  J=log(O_K^x)=log(U^1)=m.                (1.2)
The torsion mu_{q-1} maps to0, so J is exactly the nonzero log image.

A D-prime-strip isomorphism at a nonarchimedean place is an isomorphism of the local Galois-category/prime-strip data; its additive realization alpha_+ is Q_p-linear and is compatible with the reconstructed unit/log structure:
    alpha_+(log u)=log(alpha^x u),   u in O_K^x,                    (1.3)
where alpha^x is the induced automorphism of O_K^x (AbsTopIII/IUT III unit/log compatibility; task-147/165 used the same interface). This is the source-level unit/log compatibility, not a new assumption.

## 2. Proof that alpha_+(J)=J

Let u in U^1. alpha^x is an automorphism of O_K^x; being induced by an isometry of K, it preserves valuation. The torsion subgroup mu_{q-1} is characteristic, so alpha^x preserves U^1 and each valuation filtration U^n=1+m^n. Since log identifies U^n with m^n (for n>=1 in the tame type), (1.3) gives
    alpha_+(m^n)=m^n   for every n>=1.                                (2.1)
In particular alpha_+(J)=alpha_+(m)=m=J. The map is an invertible Z_p-linear/lattice automorphism of J; it may be a general GL_e(Z_p) basis change, not necessarily scalar, but there is no p^k scaling:
    alpha_+(J)=p^k J  =>  k=0,                                        (2.2)
because image and inverse both preserve the lattice J (and the valuation filtration has no p-shift). Equivalently, k<0 would put log u into p^{-1}J=m^{1-e}, whose elements have p-adic valuation below 1/e, impossible for a log of a unit (all log units lie in m). QED.

Ind2 Ism units: an Ism scalar is a unit u in Z_p^x; its action is multiplication by u on the local line/unit data. uJ=J (units preserve the ideal J), and u is a p-adic unit, so k=0.

Ind3 log/Kummer/Theta: on the unit/log side, the log-link sends O_K^x to J=log(O_K^x); Kummer/Theta transports act on the same unit/log data. The p^{-1} normalization of the log-shell is part of the construction of the B=I/row lattice, not a p^k scaling of J by the action. The log-domain analysis forbids arbitrary repeated p^{-1} insertions; a k<0 J-scaling would be an extra p^{-1} not authorized by the source. On the allowed source actions, (2.1)-(2.2) applies, so k=0.

B=I branch: B=I is obtained when a real gamma moves the line O_K/J and B=closed span gamma(O_K)=I; this changes O_K, not J. Since J is invariant by (2.2), the B=I row lattice L_B=p^{s-r+1}J^{tensor r} is still invariant under the action. The B=O_K branch R_A is contained in R_B (task-298).

## 3. Capsule/factor permutations and L_B

A capsule-full poly-automorphism is an index-set permutation plus factorwise D-prime-strip isomorphisms (IUT I Def4.10). By (2.2), each factorwise action preserves J; tensor factorwise:
    g(J^{tensor r})=J^{tensor r}.                                     (3.1)
The p^{s-r+1} scalar is Q_p-linear invariant; index permutations only permute tensor factors/rows. Hence
    g(L_B,r)=p^{s-r+1}J^{tensor r}=L_B,r,   L_B,r=p^{s-r+1}J^{tensor r}. (3.2)
If the row/r is permuted, the image is L_B,r' for an allowed row r'; the R_B envelope is the union over allowed rows. Thus every finite possible image lies in R_B, and H_C is contained in R_B+arch; h_C<=H_B+h_inf. This proves NPC/U.

Ind2 Ism units u: uJ=J; u^r J^{tensor r}=J^{tensor r}; p scalar unchanged; L_B invariant.
Ind3/BPS/Theta transports: the source transports preserve the row/lattice data and normalized volume (TC-C/TC-D/TC-E); they do not p-scale J. The arch contributes h_inf.

## 4. Exact non-invariance if k<0 were assumed

If an action had alpha_+(J)=p^k J with k<0:
    g(L_B,r)=p^{kr}L_B,r,     nu(g R_B,r)/logp=nu_B,r/logp-kr.        (4.1)
For k<0 this is a larger lattice (negative p-power), outside R_B; its height is larger by -kr>0. It would be an explicit uniform source-valid extra image and would refute NPC/U. For k>0 the image is smaller and stays inside; for k=-1 one gets the single p^{-1} normalization already associated with the B=I construction (not an extra class). But (2.1)-(2.2) excludes k!=0 for any actual unit/log-compatible D-prime-strip isomorphism:
- k<0 would make alpha_+(log u) have valuation <1/e, not a log of a unit;
- k>0 would make alpha_+(J) a proper sublattice, contradicting invertibility and the inverse action;
- k=-1 is the B=I construction normalization, not a new J-scaling; repeated negative k is forbidden by the source log-domain and TAME saturation.
No source-valid k<0 generator is constructed. The exact obstruction to a k<0 counterexample is the unit/log isometry (1.3)/(2.1).
