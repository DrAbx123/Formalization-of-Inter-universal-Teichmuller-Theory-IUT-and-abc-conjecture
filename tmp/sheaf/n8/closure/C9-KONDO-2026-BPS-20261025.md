## 1. What the paper actually proves

From the abstract/introduction and Sections 1-3:
- Main theorem: for p and a nontrivial finite Galois extension k/Q_p (with sqrt(-1) in k if p=2), the Lubin-Tate representation of G_k is not Aut-intrinsically Hodge-Tate; the set of outer automorphisms making it Hodge-Tate lies in a closed subgroup of Out(G_k) of Haar measure zero.
- Lemma 1.6: the mono-anabelian reconstruction action Out(G_k)->Aut_{Q_p}(k_+(G)) is continuous and closed.
- Theorem 1.2/Lemma 1.3: Jannsen-Wingberg-type generators, the y-basis of k_+.
- Definition 3.2/Lemma 3.3: embedded Sp_{2g}(Z_p) in the image via the mapping-class construction; dim image lower bound.
- Theorem 2.6 proof: unit/log compatibility alpha_+ log=log alpha^times.
- Section 3: mapping class groups/Dehn twists and the proof of the main theorem.

These are local finite-place statements. Even the Sp containment is a local subgroup statement; it is not a global BPS construction and does not give the exact global mod41 image by itself (task-231 used additional classes).
