## 1. What Kondo 2026 actually proves

### 1.1 Mono-anabelian reconstruction (Theorem 1.2 and following)

From a group G of MLF-type, Kondo 2026 recalls functorial group-theoretic
algorithms that construct:

    P(G) subset I(G) subset G,
    O^times(G) subset k^times(G) subset G^ab,
    k_+(G),

and natural homomorphisms

    Aut(G) -> Aut(k^times(G)),  Aut(G) -> Aut(k_+(G)),
    Out(G) -> Aut(k^times(G)),  Out(G) -> Aut(k_+(G)).           (1.1)

For alpha in Out(G), write alpha_+ : k_+(G) -> k_+(G) for its image; alpha_+
is a Q_p(G)-linear automorphism (source lines 567-586, 682).

Theorem 1.2 supplies a presentation of G by generators
sigma, tau, x_0, ..., x_{d(G)} and relations. For i=1,...,d(G), y_i in
k_+(G) is the image of x_i under the composite

    P(G) hookrightarrow I(G) -> O^times(G) -> k_+(G),             (1.2)

where the last arrow is the p-adic logarithm. The y_i form a Q_p(G)-basis of
k_+(G) (source lines 679-688 and following).

### 1.2 Main theorem

The paper's main theorem: for a finite extension k/Q_p with nontrivial finite
Galois k/Q_p (and sqrt(-1) in k if p=2), the Lubin-Tate representation
rho_{k,pi}:G_k -> O_k^times subset Aut_Qp(k_+) has the property that, for
almost all outer automorphisms alpha of G_k, rho_{k,pi} composed with a
representative of alpha is NOT Hodge-Tate. The exceptional set is contained in
a closed subgroup of Out(G_k) of Haar measure zero. In particular rho_{k,pi} is
not Aut-intrinsically Hodge-Tate.

This is a theorem about **Hodge-Tate-ness of the Lubin-Tate character**, not
about H_A-full, J=pi O_K preservation, trace coordinates, or the global
target/BPS row exhaustion.

## 3. JP: J-preservation not proved

JP (from C9-NPC-VERIFY) is: for every actual D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism /
full Ind1 action, the induced alpha_+ preserves

    J = pi O_K = log(O_K^times).                                 (3.1)

Kondo 2026 gives the **functoriality** (1.1): every alpha in Out(G) induces
alpha_+ in Aut_Qp(k_+(G)) and alpha^times in Aut(k^times(G)). This is a
necessary structural input. But it does not prove

    alpha_+(J)=J.                                                (3.2)

Reasons:

1. The reconstruction constructs k_+(G) as a Q_p-vector space and alpha_+ as
   an arbitrary Q_p-linear automorphism; it does not identify the image of the
   logarithm O^times(G)->k_+(G) with a canonical subset J that alpha_+
   preserves. The log arrow (1.2) is used to define y_i, not proved
   alpha_+-equivariant.
2. Kondo 2026's negative main theorem shows that for almost all alpha in
   Out(G_k), the composite Lubin-Tate representation is not Hodge-Tate. Thus
   arbitrary outer automorphisms do not preserve the Lubin-Tate character/
   Hodge-Tate structure. J-preservation is weaker than character preservation,
   but the paper gives no theorem deriving (3.2) for the exceptional/symplectic
   or for arbitrary alpha.
3. JP is formulated for full Ind1 D^dag-prime-strip isomorphisms, not only for
   alpha in Out(G_K) induced by group automorphisms. Kondo 2026 is a local
   Aut(G)-reconstruction paper; it does not construct the IUT D^dag/Ind1
   actions or prove that their induced outer actions preserve J.

Therefore JP remains a **conditional missing theorem**, not a Kondo 2026
consequence. The functorial alpha_+ is available, but (3.2) is not.
