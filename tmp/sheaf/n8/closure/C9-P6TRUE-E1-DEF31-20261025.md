## 3. Exact obstruction

Theorem 3.1. E1 is not derivable from the frozen Def3.1/target/BPS data.

Proof/obstruction. Every clause of Def3.1 is satisfied at the source-skeleton
level by (1.1), including the only arithmetic conditions that could exclude
b=273: F-degree prime to41, q-order 273 prime to41, residue characteristics
not41, SL2 mod41 image, and stable reduction after F_sem. The target/BPS
data have no b-bound and A is b-independent. The remaining missing objects
(explicit C_F/C_K/V/epsilon equations, exact F_sem degree, full BPS/Theta
gluing, TC-A..H/TC-S) are **missing data**, not a proof of inadmissibility.
A missing construction does not imply non-existence. Therefore no E1 proof
follows. QED.

Equivalently, E1 is an **additional source theorem** of the form
`b <= B_0` with B_0<273, or a direct showing that no complete C with
b=273 exists; neither is in the frozen corpus.
