## 2. What a BPS label actually contains

The BPS abbreviation refers to the IUT II F^{⊩▶×μ}-prime-strip data. In the
frozen source it includes at least:

(a) the etale-unit part: decomposition groups, tempered fundamental groups and
    coverings, compact/Kummer structures, labels and conjugacy data;
(b) the value part: pilot degree/value groups and the value profile;
(c) mono-theta environments and the theta/Gaussian monoids
    Gamma_{r,v} = Delta(U_v) · xi_{r,v}^N;
(d) symmetrizing/conjugate synchronization isomorphisms and the zero-label
    splitting;
(e) Kummer classes of theta units and their restriction to evaluation points;
(f) cyclotomic rigidifications, roots of unity, and the BPS gluing to the
    Theta-pilot 0-column.

None of (a)-(f) is a function of the elliptic curve E_546 alone. IUT II
Cor.3.5-3.6 combines these data from an actual realization; the curve gives
the underlying local/global field and q, not the theta/Gaussian environment.

At good places the generator part is trivial, but the BPS label still records
decomposition groups, coverings, and integral/zero-label structures. E_546 does

## 4. Non-determination theorem

Theorem 4.1. Let D be the E_546 source data: the curve E_546/Q, its local
Tate parameter at p, the formal F/K/C_K quotient, and the mod-41 skeleton.
There exist at least two admissible BPS label/gluing structures over D that
agree on every E_546-determined datum but differ in the theta/Gaussian value
profile and its root normalization. No source theorem selects one.

Proof. The local theta value (3.1) depends on a choice of a with a^{82}=q
(equivalently a^{2l}=q) and on the source root unit zeta. If a is replaced by
zeta_82 a for a nontrivial 82nd root of unity zeta_82, then a^{j^2} is
multiplied by zeta_82^{j^2}; the resulting g_{r,v,j} are another admissible
value profile. The same curve E_546, the same q, and the same local I_546/
b=273 data are unchanged.

The BPS label/gluing also requires the choice of evaluation normalization and
zero-label splitting. These choices can be made compatibly in two different
ways (change the root and adjust the splitting); both satisfy the IUT II
Cor.3.5 value-profile and zero-label axioms. They are the standard root/cyclotome
ambiguities of the theta value formula, not arbitrary data introduced by this
file. The global gluing to the Theta-pilot 0-column records these choices, so
the two structures have different BPS labels.

Since E_546 does not distinguish the two choices, no function from D to a
unique global BPS label/gluing exists. QED.

Remark 4.2. The theorem is an underdetermination statement, not a no-go for
the existence of some BPS structure. A complete C with a specified BPS choice
could exist; E_546 alone cannot construct it canonically. The task's "no
arbitrary BPS data" forbids choosing one of the two structures by hand.

## 5. Exact obstruction

The global BPS/F^{⊩▶×μ} construction fails from E_546 because:

(O1) E_546 supplies the curve and local q, not the global mono-theta
     environment, theta/Gaussian monoids, evaluation points, or Kummer
     classes;
(O2) the theta value formula has root/cyclotome and zero-label choices that
     E_546 does not fix; at least two admissible local profiles exist;
(O3) the global BPS gluing/symmetrizing isomorphisms across places are not
     determined by local q or by E[41];
(O4) the target Theta-pilot 0-column and its possible-image set are missing;
     no BPS label can be glued to a nonexistent target column.

Consequently the b=273 complete-C BPS object is not constructed; the b=273
upper-side question is not repaired.

## 6. What would be needed

A genuine construction would require:

1. an actual realization with specified theta/Gaussian monoids, evaluation
   data, root/cyclotome normalizations, and zero-label splitting at every
   relevant place;
2. symmetrizing isomorphisms and BPS gluing to the Theta-pilot 0-column;
3. compatibility with the global Ind1/Ind2/Ind3 and target container;
4. an actual complete C with target-column/BPS data.

These are exactly the missing items in E2/TC; no source functor from E_546
supplies them.

## 7. Conclusion

The E_546 b=273 skeleton does not determine a global BPS/F^{⊩▶×μ}-prime-strip
label or its gluing to the Theta-pilot 0-column. The theta/Gaussian value
profile has root/cyclotome/zero-label ambiguities, and the global environment
is not a function of the curve. At least two admissible structures agree on all
E_546 data but differ in BPS labels; no source theorem selects one.

The exact obstruction is O1-O4. No arbitrary BPS data was chosen; the two
structures are the source-allowed root/cyclotome ambiguities, used only to show
non-determination. The b=273 global BPS object and the upper-side repair are
not obtained.
