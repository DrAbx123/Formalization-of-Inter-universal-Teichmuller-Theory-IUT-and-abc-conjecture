# C9 C1 domain correction: admissible S with [1*S]=[S_q]

Goal: audit D2/D3: construct or refute existence of an admissible S subset VC(I) with
    [1*S] = [S_q] in R^ss,
where 1 in N acts by LGP multiplication. Try S=LGP^{-1}S_q and volume scaling; state exact obstruction.

Inputs: C9-C1-DOMAIN-CORRECTION-20261025.md; C9-C1-VOL-T1-20261025.md; C9-C1-VOL-ADM-20261025.md; C9-C1-LS-ADM-20261025.md; LANA report snapshot C9-STRICT-DEFICIT-LANA-SNAPSHOT-20261024.tex, LGP lines 1598-1613, main goal lines 2502-2523.

## 0. Verdict

D2 is NOT constructed and NOT refuted. The two proposed constructions fail at the source level:

(A) S = LGP^{-1}S_q: LGP is an element of VC(O) defined by the theta/LGP values; it is not a unit of VC(O) and multiplication by LGP on VC(I) is not known to be invertible. The inverse LGP^{-1} is not source-defined as an element/operation on VC(I); LGP^{-1}S_q may leave VC(I), fail adelic measurability, and fail the integral/admissibility condition. No source construction of an admissible S this way.

(B) Volume scaling: choose S with vol(LGP*S)=vol(S_q). This requires a source-defined LGP action with constant Haar Jacobian J and an admissible S of prescribed volume; neither is proved. Moreover, volume equality gives only [LGP*S]=[S_q] as volume classes; it does not give admissibility of S or the eta^anab_S pointed-linear-isomorphism property. Volume-chosen S is not automatically admissible (C9-C1-VOL-ADM).

The exact obstruction is the combination:
- LGP is not known to be invertible on VC(I); no source inverse;
- T_1(U)=LGP*U is module multiplication, not a translation by an additive group element; constant Haar Jacobian is not established;
- admissibility/integral structure is not preserved by arbitrary scaling or by volume selection;
- [1*S]=[S_q] is only a class equality, not eta^anab_S existence or pointed isomorphism.
D2/D3 therefore remain open; no admissible S is constructed.

## 2. D2 exact condition

D2:
    exists S subset VC(I) such that
      (a) S is admissible (adelic measurable, integral/N-compatible, 1*S in R^ss);

Only (b) is a volume-class equality; (a) and D3 are construction conditions. A source construction of S must supply all three.

## 3. Attempt A: S=LGP^{-1}S_q

### 3.1 What would be needed

For S=LGP^{-1}S_q to be exact:
- LGP must be invertible in the relevant structure: multiplication by LGP on VC(I) must be a bijection, and LGP^{-1} must act on S_q within VC(I);
- S=LGP^{-1}S_q must be adelic measurable, finite positive volume, and an admissible integral structure;
- 1*S=LGP*(LGP^{-1}S_q)=S_q exactly, so [1*S]=[S_q].
If all hold, D2(b) is exact and avoids volume scaling.

### 3.2 Why it fails at source level

(a) LGP is not a unit of VC(O). LGP is the LGP element of the large volume container (LANA lines 1598-1613):
    LGP = (LGP_v), LGP_v = (LGP_{S_{j+1},v})_{j in J},
    LGP_{S_{j+1},v} = tensor(1) tensor q_v^{j^2} (bad v), = tensor(1) tensor 1 (good v).
Its local components are theta values q_v^{j^2}, which are nonunits at Vbad (positive p-adic valuation for q_v small). Therefore LGP is not a unit of VC(O), and multiplication by LGP is not a module automorphism of VC(O) or VC(I). It is injective in the ambient K-vector space but not surjective onto VC(I).

(b) LGP^{-1} is not source-defined as an operation on VC(I). The inverse of multiplication by LGP exists only on the ambient Q-vector space/VC(K); applying it to S_q may produce a subset outside the compact log-shell container VC(I). There is no source theorem that LGP^{-1}S_q lies in VC(I), is adelic measurable, or has finite positive volume. The construction would require an inverse which LANA does not provide.

(c) Admissibility is not inherited. Even if LGP^{-1}S_q is a subset of VC(I), it is defined from the target q-pilot class S_q, not from an integral structure; it is not proved to be VC(O)-compatible/N-compatible/admissible. The condition S admissible is independent of the exact identity LGP*S=S_q.

(d) Circularity. S is defined by inverting the very LGP action whose compatibility with the q-pilot is the content of C1. Using S=LGP^{-1}S_q assumes the q-pilot is in the image of LGP, which is not proved.

Verdict A: REJECT as source construction. S=LGP^{-1}S_q is not defined in VC(I), not admissible, and circular. No exact S is obtained.

## 4. Attempt B: volume scaling

### 4.1 What would be needed

Assume:
- T_1: VC(I)->VC(I), U |-> LGP*U is a measurable map with constant Haar Jacobian J>0 on its domain;
- admissible S of every positive volume exist in VC(I);
then choose S with vol(S)=vol(S_q)/J, getting vol(T_1(S))=vol(S_q), hence [T_1(S)]=[S_q] in R^ss.

### 4.2 Why it fails at source level

(a) T_1 is module multiplication, not translation. VC(I) is an additive compact Z_p-module; U |-> LGP*U is multiplication by the fixed module element LGP, not translation U |-> U+LGP. The Haar scaling of a general module endomorphism is not a constant Jacobian on VC(I) unless LGP acts as a unit/scalar with a well-defined Haar modulus. The source does not state that LGP is a unit or that T_1 is a measurable bijection with constant Jacobian. Task-441's conditional formula is not a source theorem for this module action.

(b) Even if T_1 were measurable, it is not known to be surjective/bijective on VC(I); LGP is nonunit, so the image is a proper subset. The class [T_1(S)] is defined only if T_1(S) is adelic measurable; for arbitrary S, the image may fail adelic measurability. This is exactly the ADM-NOGO obstruction.

(c) Volume scaling does not preserve admissibility. An admissible integral structure scaled by a real parameter (or by the inverse of J) need not remain VC(O)-compatible/N-stable; C9-C1-VOL-ADM rejects volume-only selection as insufficient. The existence of an admissible S of prescribed volume is not a source theorem.

(d) Class equality is not eta equality. Even if [T_1(S)]=[S_q], this is only a volume-class equality in R^ss; eta^anab_S requires S admissible and the anabelian pointed linear isomorphism; D3 is separate. Lemma A cannot be applied without eta^anab_S defined and pointed.

Verdict B: volume scaling gives at best a measurable set of the right volume class, not an admissible S with [1*S]=[S_q] and eta^anab_S defined. REJECT as source construction.

## 5. D3: pointed linear isomorphism

LANA says the anabelian construction gives an isomorphism eta^anab_S of pointed real lines after fixing a suitable S; it does not assert this for arbitrary S or for a volume-chosen S. For the attempted S:
- S admissibility/integrality is not proved;
- [1*S]=[S_q] is at most a volume class, and the target pilot [1*S] may not equal the q-pilot class as needed by Lemma A;
- nonzero pilot/finite positive volume is not proved for S or 1*S;
- the construction's Ind1/Ind2 dependence and the pointed-line structure are not checked.
Thus D3 is not proved. Even a successful D2 would need D3 and the output bridge D4.

## 6. Existence/refutation status

D2 existence is not refuted in the abstract: there might exist some admissible S satisfying [1*S]=[S_q]. But no source construction is available:
- S=LGP^{-1}S_q fails because LGP is nonunit and LGP^{-1} is not a source operation on VC(I);
- volume scaling fails because constant Jacobian/admissible S of prescribed volume are unproved, and class equality is insufficient;
- no other construction of an admissible S with the required class is supplied by LANA or IUT III.
Therefore D2 is OPEN, with the exact obstruction above. D3 is OPEN. C1 is not obtained.

## 8. Conclusion

D2/D3 are not closed. The two natural attempts fail:
- S=LGP^{-1}S_q requires an inverse of the nonunit LGP on VC(I), which is not source-defined; the result may leave VC(I) and is not shown admissible.
- Volume scaling gives only a volume-class match [LGP*S]=[S_q]; it does not prove S admissible, does not prove T_1 has constant Haar Jacobian, and does not prove eta^anab_S is a pointed linear isomorphism.
The exact obstruction is the non-invertibility/non-unit nature of LGP, the absence of a source inverse or constant-Jacobian action, and the insufficiency of volume class equality for admissibility and D3. No arbitrary S, no mixed/E3, no Lean.
