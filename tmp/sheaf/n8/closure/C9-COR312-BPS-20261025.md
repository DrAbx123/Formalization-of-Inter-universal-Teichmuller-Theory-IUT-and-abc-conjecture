## 0. Source statement

Cor.3.12 (lines 10152-10180): in the situation of Theorem3.11, define

    -|log(Theta)| := the procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume (j-average)
                       of the holomorphic hull of the union of possible images of a
                       Theta-pilot object in the multiradial representation, subject
                       to (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3);
    -|log(q)|     := the corresponding q-pilot log-volume, not subject to Ind1/2/3.

Then -|log(Theta)| is finite and

    -|log(Theta)| >= -|log(q)|.                                       (0.1)

In C9 notation h_C=-|log(Theta)| and Q_C=-|log(q)|; (0.1) is exactly P6, h_C>=Q_C.

## 1. Exact source schema A1-A3

The proof of Cor.3.12 is a tautological construction-computation in three source-level components. It does not require constructing an explicit complete C, an E_546-specific BPS representative, or a numerical theta-pilot region.

### A1. q-pilot input/representation

SOURCE: Definition3.8(i) (q-pilot and Theta-pilot objects) and Cor.3.12 Step (xi-a), lines 10571-10618. The proof fixes one Theta-times-mu LGP-link

    0,0 HT -> 1,0 HT,                                                 (A1.1)

a full poly-isomorphism of F^⊩▶×mu-prime-strips whose value-group portion sends the Theta-pilot object at (0,0) to the q-pilot object at (1,0). The q-pilot at (1,0) is the input representation on the codomain side; its log-volume is the fixed value -|log(q)|.

STATUS: source construction/definition. No explicit C or numerical q is needed for the logical schema; the situation of Theorem3.11 supplies the objects.

### A2. determinant/log-volume comparison

SOURCE: Cor.3.12 Steps (xi-c),(xi-d), lines 10618-10690. The multiradial algorithm yields output possibilities in the 1-column, linked by F^⊩▶-prime-strip isomorphisms to the q-pilot representation via the 1-column log-Kummer correspondence and expressible in the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure (IPL/SHE). Formation of the holomorphic hull expresses the output as localizations of arithmetic vector bundles over local rings O_{K_v}; these are rank>1, while the q-pilot is a line bundle in global realified Frobenioids. One therefore takes a suitable positive tensor power of the determinant of the rank>1 localizations and applies the j-normalized log-volume. This yields sets

    R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} subset R,    -|log(q)| in R,                  (A2.1)

and the comparison of the normalized log-volumes on the two sides.

STATUS: source proof. The determinant/log-volume comparison is a construction-computation, not an equality of explicit regions; no numerical determinant constant is introduced.

### A3. fixed q-pilot value implies the inequality

SOURCE: Cor.3.12 Steps (xi-e),(xi-f), lines 10690-10715, and Step (xi-g)/Fig.3.8. SHE fixes the q-pilot log-volume at -|log(q)| and says the q-pilot value is one of the possible output log-volumes. Therefore

    -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|},                                 (A3.1)
    hence  -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|.                                 (A3.2)

STATUS: source semantic/logical deduction. It is tautological once A1-A2 are fixed: the q-pilot value is in the output comparison set by construction.

## 2. P6-true theorem (universal, no explicit C needed)

**Theorem (Cor.3.12 main line).** For every admissible complete initial Theta-data in the situation of Theorem3.11, with the procession-normalized log-volumes of Cor.3.12,

    h_C >= Q_C.                                                        (2.1)

Equivalently P6 holds universally on the source main line. No explicit complete C, E_546-specific BPS, or numerical theta-pilot representative is required for the theorem; A1-A3 are source-level properties of the given data.

**Proof.** A1 supplies the q-pilot representation at (1,0). A2 supplies the determinant/log-volume comparison via the 1-column multiradial output and hull. A3 supplies the fixed q-pilot value inclusion. Then (A3.2) is (2.1). This is exactly Cor.3.12 Steps (i),(x),(xi-a)-(xi-f) (source lines 10248-10715). QED.

## 4. Exact source gaps / non-claims

1. A1-A2 are source-level construction properties (IPL/SHE/APT/HIS), not an explicit set-theoretic map from q-pilot to Theta-pilot and not a numerical determinant equality. This is a formalization gap, not an extra assumption of the theorem.
2. The source explicitly permits forgetting theta functions/values (HIS) and treats the multiradial algorithm by its properties; a black-box-free numerical algorithm is not supplied by the text. The logical proof of Cor.3.12 does not require one.
3. The theorem is universal over admissible Theorem3.11 data; it does not construct an explicit E_546 b=273 complete C or a BPS representative. That construction is a separate question and is not needed for P6-true.
4. Cor.3.12 gives h_C>=Q_C. Therefore any claimed target saturation upper U273 that would give h_C<=max(H_A,H_B)+h_inf for a b=273 complete C is incompatible with Cor.3.12; such a C cannot be a counterexample on the source main line. E1 is not needed.
