## 1. Theorem

**Theorem 1.1 (E2 is b-independent).** The truth of E2 for the b=273 profile
is equivalent to the truth of E2 for the same complete C with its b=3 profile;
b=273 contributes no new CFTH/TC/arch failure. Consequently:

(a) If CFTH/TC/arch hold for the target C, they hold for the b=273 profile;
(b) If they fail for b=273, they fail for a b-independent reason (the same C,
    donor, target container, or arch normalization fails);
(c) E2 cannot be established from the b=273 local data alone.

In particular, the b=273 no-go is conditional only on the global CFTH/TC/arch
inputs, not on any b=273-specific defect.
