## 2. Correction of the symplectic donor

The corrected donor is the one assembled in C9-SYMPLECTIC-SOURCE-20261025.md.
The original Section 2 of C9-TAME-LOG-DOMAIN-20261025.md must not be copied.
Five precise corrections:

C1. rho is not a homomorphism.
Kondo 2025 constructs rho: Mod(S minus P) -> Out(G_K) as a set-theoretic map.
Remark 2.18 does not assert that suitable lifts make it a group homomorphism.
Therefore one cannot write rho(Mod) = Sp, cannot choose a homomorphic section
Sp -> Out(G_K), and cannot multiply chosen lifts to get symplectic matrix
products. The correct closed subgroup is generated by the image of rho, and the
actual symplectic source is the closed preimage

    H = Phi^{-1}(Sp_{2g}(Z_p)) subset Out(G_K),
    Phi(H) = Sp_{2g}(Z_p).                                      (2H)

This H may be larger than the abstract Sp. The statement "the image of rho is
Sp" is false as stated; the correct statement is the containment
Sp_{2g}(Z_p) subset Phi(Out(G_K)) from Kondo 2026 Lemma 3.3.

C2. Boundary-word-preserving free-group substitutions are not automatically
actual Galois automorphisms.
The cited papers do not prove that every free-group automorphism preserving the
Jannsen-Wingberg boundary word lies in the image of the mapping class group Mod
or in Out(G_K). The actual source is the mapping-class/Dehn-twist construction
rho of Kondo 2025 Section 2. A single substitution such as
x_2 |-> x_2 x_3, x_3 |-> x_3 illustrates a Dehn twist preserving [x_2,x_3],
but it does not prove all Sp generators arise from such simple substitutions.
The full Sp donor must cite Kondo 2026 Lemma 3.3 / Kondo 2025 Section 2.

C3. Basis alignment is an inferred convention.
The trace theorem (Kondo 2025 Theorem 1.3) is stated for the 2025
Jannsen-Wingberg trace basis y_1,...,y_d. The symplectic embedding (Kondo 2026
Definition 3.2 and Lemma 3.3) is stated for the 2026 basis. The two papers use
different normal forms for the JW relation (the 2026 odd-d relation has the
extra factor (prod ... tau)^{varpi t/(p-1)}). The identification of the bases,
or the statement that the trace-adapted line is the same line U fixed by the
2026 diag(1,A) subgroup, is an explicit compatibility assumption, not a quoted
cross-paper theorem.

C4. Trace target.
Kondo 2025 Theorem 1.3 gives the trace kernel for Tr_{k/k^{(d=1)}}, not always
for Tr_{k/Q_p}. In the tame totally ramified C9 local type e=ell, f=1, one has
k^{(d=1)} = Q_p, so the formula becomes Tr_{K/Q_p}. It must not be transported
to extensions with nontrivial residue degree without the corresponding
k^{(d=1)}.

inferred consequences.

## 3. What full Ind1 contains and what it does not

For fixed n, Ind1 is the automorphism group of the procession
Prc(n,circ D^dag_T), not the finite label translation of the finite model.

At level j an automorphism is a capsule-full poly-automorphism; it includes:

1. permutations of the capsule index set;
2. arbitrary isomorphisms of the constituent D^dag-prime-strips;
3. independent choices at every level j, because the procession arrows are the
   full collections and no fixed flag is specified.

Full Ind1 is larger than the symplectic subgroup H. The symplectic subgroup is
useful for producing a nonconstant local orbit; it is not the whole Ind1 group.
A proof of invariance under full Ind1 is not a proof of invariance under H, and
vice versa. The input package uses the symplectic subgroup to produce selected
branches; that is legitimate only after the corrected H donor and its
naturality input are supplied.

Ind1, Ind2, Ind3 are distinct:
- Ind1: procession automorphisms;
- Ind2: independent Ism/order-2 actions on direct summands;
- Ind3: upper semi-compatible log-link indeterminacy.
The target hull H_C is subject to all three. A proof that handles Ind1 alone
does not imply target containment.

## 4. Common terminal T, beta, F, and their corrected source status

The input package fixes a core/gluing representative eta and a terminal
procession representative Phi with packet transport

    T = kappa_1^{-1} o Q(Phi) o kappa_0.                         (T)

It chooses a second representative Psi from field-copy isomorphisms f_{j,i,v}
and sets

    beta = Phi^{-1} o Psi,
    F = T o beta_+ = kappa_1^{-1} o Q(Psi) o kappa_0.            (F)

This construction does not depend on the flawed boundary-word argument, but it
does depend on the corrected H donor whenever beta or Psi is claimed to come
from actual Aut(G_K) elements. The following source conditions are required.

SD1. Phi and Psi must be actual procession isomorphisms of the same source and
target processions, with core representative eta. Their components f_{j,i,v}
must be isomorphisms of D^dag-prime-strips preserving the log-shell, the
mono-analytic integral structure, and the Kummer labels. A field-copy
isomorphism alone is not enough.

SD2. beta must be an element of the full source Ind1 group, not merely a change
of terminal representative. If Phi and Psi do not share the same source and
target processions, beta may be only a poly-morphism or a morphism between
different containers.

SD3. If beta arises from the symplectic donor, it must come from H =
Phi^{-1}(Sp), not from rho(Mod) as a homomorphic image. The actual lift is a
mapping-class/Dehn-twist representative; no multiplicative choice of lifts is
available unless the basis-alignment and naturality conditions are supplied.

SD4. F = T beta is a source-to-target packet transport. It is not automatically
a target automorphism. If T is the full multiradial algorithm transport, then
T beta is the algorithm applied to the beta-twisted source and its output is a
possible target image. If T is only the packet-level transport of (T), then F is
only packet-level and target membership is not automatic.

## 5. Audit of (11)-(14) after the donor correction

The input package states:

    T o beta_+ = F                                               (11)
    R_{beta gamma}
      = T(beta_+ gamma_+(A(S)))
      = F(gamma_+(A(S)))                                        (12)
    F(Hull(union_gamma gamma_+(A(S)))) subset H_C                 (13)
    nu(H_{C,j,p})/log p >= -s_j - 1/e                            (14)

with n = s e + t.

### 5.1 Equation (11)

Equation (11) is the definition/composition (F) once T, beta, and the Kummer
representatives are fixed. It is not an immediate consequence of Proposition
1.2(vi) and Proposition 3.2(i) alone. Those results give poly-isomorphisms and
Ism-orbits. A canonical single equality requires:

- chosen representatives of all Kummer/poly-isomorphism orbits;
- a proof that T is compatible with the chosen beta and F;
- an Ism/Ind2 treatment. An Ism scalar in Z_p^x leaves the integer lattice
  unchanged but can move the target hull/log-volume branch; this must be shown
  invariant or tracked.

The donor correction adds: if beta is realized through the symplectic source,
it must be realized through H = Phi^{-1}(Sp) and a mapping-class lift, with the
basis-alignment and naturality inputs. The invalid boundary-word construction of
C9-TAME Section 2 cannot be used here.

### 5.2 Equation (12)

If (11) holds as an equality on the source packet, and if beta gamma is in the
full source Ind1 group, then (12) follows by composition. Required conditions:

- beta in Ind1_src, not just a change of terminal representative;
- gamma allowed and beta gamma in the same full Ind1 group;
- A(S) is the actual source region of the Theta-pilot object whose possible
  images define H_C, not an arbitrary admissible integral input;
- T(beta gamma(A(S))) is an allowed target possible image in the same target
  container: same n, row j, vQ, Theta-gluing, target column, normalization.

If the source beta comes from the flawed symplectic construction, (12) must be
re-derived with the corrected H donor; the algebraic identity alone does not
repair the source.

### 5.3 Equation (13)

This is the decisive target-container step. A Q_p-algebra isomorphism F does
not by itself imply

    F(Hull(L)) subset H_C

for an arbitrary fixed target hull H_C. Two routes are available.

Route A: H_C is Ind1-saturated and includes all T(beta gamma)(A(S)) branches
for the full source Ind1 orbit. Then each F(gamma_+(A(S))) is one of the images
defining H_C, and since F is additive and H_C is a holomorphic hull closed under
addition, Z_p-scaling, and limits, F(Hull(...)) is contained in H_C. This is the
cleanest route; it requires an actual proof that the target possible-image
family is saturated under the target conjugates of the full source Ind1 group,
with the same T and the same core/gluing.

Route B: F is an allowed target automorphism (target Ind1/Ind2/Ind3) acting on
H_C, so F(H_C) subset H_C. But F as defined by (F) is a source-to-target
transport. To use Route B one must prove F = T beta T^{-1} with T invertible on
the target ambient and beta a target Ind1 element, or prove directly that the
target action implements the same conjugation.

The input package's sentence "because F is a ring isomorphism" is insufficient.
It silently assumes Route A or Route B. Neither is proved.

### 5.4 Equation (14)

Even with (13), (14) needs a numerical volume comparison:

    nu(H_C) >= nu(F(Hull(...))).

This requires F to preserve the target normalized log-volume, or to have a
known determinant factor. A Q_p-algebra isomorphism is not automatically
measure-preserving. The target log-volume is procession-normalized and
mono-analytic; it involves degree factors, tensor powers, global tuple weights,
and normalization of the target column. The input package does not prove:

- F(O_{E_r}) = O_target (or a controlled integrality inclusion);
- F is continuous and Z_p-linear on the relevant hull;
- nu_target(F(E)) = nu_source(E) for the actual regions used, with any scalar
  factor tracked.

Without this, (13) is a set inclusion, not the lower bound (14). The donor
correction does not affect this target-volume gap; it is independent.

## 6. Target-container conditions TC-A to TC-H

The following are the precise missing conditions. They are to be supplied, not
assumed, and must not be replaced by an arbitrary enlargement of H_C.

TC-A (source procession). Phi and Psi are actual isomorphisms of the same
source/target D^dag-processions, with core representative eta; components are
D^dag-prime-strip isomorphisms preserving log-shell, integral structures,
Kummer labels; beta is in full source Ind1.

TC-B (target saturation). Either H_C is the hull of the full union of target
possible images T(beta gamma)(A(S)) for the full source Ind1 orbit, or F is an
allowed target automorphism of H_C. A ring isomorphism alone is not enough.

TC-C (volume). F preserves the target integral closure/hull and the normalized
log-volume, or its positive determinant factor is computed and tracked.

TC-D (row/place). F preserves the row j, the place vQ, and the bad/good profile
used in the lower bound. If F permutes capsule constituents, the target H_C
must be the corresponding full Ind1 union; a fixed component H_{C,j,vQ} need
not contain the permuted image.

TC-E (Theta/gluing). F preserves the fixed Theta-pilot object, its
Theta-times-mu gluing, and the q/BPS/target-column class. Core/gluing eta
preservation alone does not prove this for a high-capsule field-copy
isomorphism.

TC-F (log branch/Ind3). F lies on the allowed log-Kummer branch and is
compatible with the upper semi-compatible log-link indeterminacy Ind3. A field
copy construction is not a log-link construction.

TC-G (n and infinity). Ind1 in Theorem 3.11(i) is attached to a fixed n. The
permutation symmetries n -> n' are separate. If H_C is quantified over n, or
the target column is shifted, the construction must be extended. The +infinity
branch of Corollary 3.12 and the archimedean places are not covered by the
finite all-bad formulas (14)-(15).

TC-H (Ism/Ind2). F may differ by an Ism or order-2 target indeterminacy. The
integer lattice can be unchanged while the target hull/log-volume branch moves;
this must be checked.

TC-S (corrected symplectic donor). Any use of beta/gamma from the symplectic
source must use H = Phi^{-1}(Sp_{2g}(Z_p)), the mapping-class/Dehn-twist actual
source, the trace basis alignment assumption, the trace target
Tr_{K/K^{(d=1)}} (with K^{(d=1)}=Q_p only in the tame totally ramified case),
the low-ramification input J=m, and the standard-lattice lemma. The original
boundary-word construction and the notation "G|W=Sp" as an image of rho are
withdrawn.
