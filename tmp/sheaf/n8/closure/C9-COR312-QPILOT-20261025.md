## 1. A1: q-pilot input and positivity

Source loci: Definition 3.8(i) (in the Introduction and §3), Cor3.12
statement, Step (xi-a).

(A1.1) The q-pilot object is the object obtained by considering the
arithmetic divisor determined by the zero locus of the elements `q_v` at
v in Vbad in a version/realiﬁcation of the global Frobenioid F^⊛_mod.

(A1.2) Its log-volume is denoted

    -|log(q)| in R,   so |log(q)|>0.                         (1.1)

The source states that `|log(q)|>0` is easily computed from the q-parameters
q_v of E_F at v in Vbad (Definition 3.1(b)); Vbad is nonempty.

(A1.3) In Step (xi-a), a q-pilot object at (1,0) is represented via the
holomorphic log-shells; the Θ×μ LGP-link from (0,0) to (1,0) is a gluing
isomorphism matching the Θ-pilot object at (0,0) to the q-pilot object at
(1,0). The q-pilot log-volume is defined in the multiradial representation
at (1,0) and is **not** regarded as subject to (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3).

Thus A1 is a source definition/construction, not a black-box assumption.
Positivity is a sign normalization: q-pilot is a negative log-volume
(a zero-locus divisor at the bad places), and |log q| is its positive
absolute value.

## 2. A2: determinant/log-volume comparison

Source loci: Steps (xi-c), (xi-d); Remark 3.9.5(vii)-(ix); Prop 3.9.

(A2.1) The algorithm of Theorem 3.11, followed by the holomorphic hull,
yields possible output regions in `1,°U` inside `1,°U_Q =
{IQ(S^±_{j+1}; 1,°D^⊣_{vQ})}`. These output possibilities are:

- linked, via isomorphisms of F^⊩▶-prime-strips, to the representation of
  the q-pilot object at (1,0) on `1,°U_Q` (IPL);
- expressible relative to the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure
  (SHE).

(A2.2) After passing to the holomorphic hull `1,°U`, the output data are
expressed as localizations of arithmetic vector bundles over local rings
O_{K_v}. These localizations have rank>1, whereas the q-pilot object is a
global arithmetic line bundle. The source compares them by:

    taking a suitable positive tensor power of the determinant of the
    rank>1 localizations [Remark 3.9.5(vii), Ob3-Ob4],
    applying the [suitably j-normalized] log-volume to the regions
    1,°U and to the region arising from the q-pilot representation
    on 1,°U_Q [Remark 3.9.5(vii)-(ix)],
    obtaining completely comparable objects:
        R_{<= -|log Θ|} := {λ in R : λ <= -|log Θ|} subset R,
        -|log q| in R.                                      (2.1)

(A2.3) The source notes this comparison is based on Proposition 3.9(iii)'s
interpretation of global arithmetic degrees of global realified Frobenioids
as log-volumes, and on the 1-column log-Kummer correspondence rectifying
the vertical unit/log-shell shift.

A2 establishes that both sides are read by the **same normalized log-volume
functional** on comparable arithmetic line bundles (after determinant power).
It does not produce an explicit numerical determinant-norm equality or an
explicit region map; that is a source-level gap (§4).

## 3. A3: fixed q-pilot value and the inclusion

Source loci: Steps (xi-e), (xi-f).

(A3.1) The relation between the pilot-object log-volume -|log q| and the
input F^⊩▶×μ-prime-strip is precisely the relation prescribed by the
1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure. `SHE` (expressibility relative to
that structure) amounts to:

    expressibility via operations valid/executable even subject to the
    condition that the pilot-object log-volume associated to the input
    F^⊩▶×μ-prime-strip [linked to the possible output F^⊩▶-prime-strips]
    equal the fixed value -|log q| in R.                     (3.1)

(A3.2) Step (xi-e) concludes: the multiradial algorithm, followed by
holomorphic hull and log-volume, yields possible output log-volumes
`R_{<= -|log Θ|}` linked via F^⊩▶-prime-strip isomorphisms to the fixed
pilot-object log-volume `-|log q|`.

(A3.3) Step (xi-f): the construction of `R_{<= -|log Θ|}` is subject to the
condition that it constitutes a construction of the pilot-object log-volume
of the input data, namely `-|log q|`. Therefore:

    -|log q| in R_{<= -|log Θ|},   hence   -|log q| <= -|log Θ| in R. (3.2)

The source calls this formal; it is the semantic/tautological inclusion: the
q-pilot value is one of the possible output values because the output data are
linked to the q-pilot input and the construction is conditioned on the fixed
q-pilot value.

## 4. CTheta derivation

Let

    a := |log q| > 0,   b := -|log Θ| in R.

A1 gives a>0. A2 gives b in R and -|log q| in R. A3 gives
`-a in R_{<= b}`, i.e. `-a <= b`. Hence

    b >= -a.                                                   (4.1)

By definition of CTheta, for real CTheta,

    b <= CTheta * a.                                           (4.2)

Combining (4.2) and (4.1):

    CTheta * a >= b >= -a  =>  CTheta >= -1                   (4.3)

(since a>0). This is exactly the source's `CTheta>=-1` comparison after the
inequality `-|log Θ|>=-|log q|`.

Note the sign: the source's negative notation is intentional; `-|log Θ|` is
a negative real (or +∞ a priori), and the inequality says the Θ-pilot
negative log-volume is not more negative than the q-pilot one.

## 5. Exact source gaps / what is not black-box

A1-A3 are source properties, but the source proof leaves the following
internal gaps. They are not assumptions inserted in this file.

(G1) Positivity `|log q|>0` is stated as 'easily computed' from the
q-parameters; Cor3.12 does not expand the computation or give the exact
normalized sum. The sign is definitional (zero-locus divisor at bad v,
negative log-volume), but a numerical derivation of |log q| is not written.

(G2) Finiteness `-|log Θ| in R`: the proof says this follows easily from the
compactness of `1,°U_{j,vQ}` plus the definition of log-volume, and that
after assuming negativity one gets finiteness. The compactness verification
and the precise hull/log-volume argument are not expanded in Cor3.12; they
rely on Remark 3.9.5/Theorem 3.11.

(G3) The determinant/log-volume comparison is not an explicit determinant
norm equality. The source says to take a suitable positive tensor power of
the determinant and apply the suitably normalized log-volume; no explicit
constant/equality is computed. The comparison is at the level of normalized
log-volume functionals.

(G4) The inclusion `-|log q| in R_{<= -|log Θ|}` is semantic/tautological
from IPL/SHE/APT, not a numerical theorem or explicit region transport.
The source itself says the construction is conditioned on the fixed
q-pilot value; there is no explicit map from the q-pilot object to the
Θ-pilot hull.

(G5) Ind1/Ind2/Ind3: the q-pilot is not subject to them, while the Θ-pilot
output set is. The source takes the hull of possible images; it does not
resolve the indeterminacies into an explicit region comparison.

(G6) APT is a construction algorithm defined by source properties
(Remark 3.11.1(iv)), not a set-theoretic algorithm computing regions.

No additional black-box is introduced here: the gaps are exactly where the
source invokes Theorem 3.11, Proposition 3.9, Remark 3.9.5, and the
construction algorithm instead of expanding an explicit computation.
