## 1. Overall verdict

The proof is a source-faithful paraphrase of IUT III Corollary 3.12, but it is
NOT an unconditional natural-language proof in the sense claimed. It
paraphrases the source assertions; it does not derive the critical
closed-loop/AND/membership step independently.

The two substantive defects are:

(D1) The proof conflates the multiradial indeterminacies (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3)
     with the Ob9-1 formal quotient indeterminacies. These are different
     objects in the source. The phrase "(Ind1)-(Ind3)/Ob9-1" is an
     identification that is not in IUT III.

(D2) The proof treats the closed loop as exhibiting the q-pilot object
     directly as one of the possible output data. The source says the closed
     loop constructs a prime-strip simultaneously equipped with the Theta- and
     q-intertwinings, and that the q-intertwining/log-volume is a special case
     of the Theta-intertwining up to indeterminacies. It does not say the
     q-pilot object itself is one of the possible output images. The final
     membership is Cor 3.12(xi-e)-(xi-f), not a consequence of the preceding
     steps.

Minor defects:

(D3) Ind2 is described as "tensor-factor copy/isomorphism actions"; the actual
     source definition is independent copies of Ism (resp. order-2
     automorphisms) on each direct summand of the j+1 tensor factors. The
     paraphrase is too loose for an exact proof.

(D4) Finiteness of h is attributed to xi-d, but the finiteness argument is at
     lines 10234-10237 and is asserted from compactness of 1,circ U_{j,vQ},
     not derived.

The final inequality follows formally IF xi-e/xi-f membership is accepted.
Therefore the proof is acceptable as a source-conditional exposition, but
REJECTED as an "unconditional proof with no extra hypotheses."

## 2. Per-step verdicts

### Setup (Section 1) — ACCEPT

The setup uses initial Theta-data, the associated Theta-plus-minus-ell-NF Hodge
theaters, the log-theta-lattice, q-pilot and Theta-pilot objects, and the
packet+procession normalization of Proposition 3.9. These are source objects
(Def3.8, Prop3.9). No problem at the setup level.

### Multiradial algorithm invocation (Section 2) — ACCEPT with qualifications

Theorem 3.11 does give the multiradial representation algorithm and the output
indeterminacies. Remark 3.11.1 does give IPL, SHE, APT/HIS. The proof's
summary is broadly source-faithful.

However:

- Ind1 is described only as "procession automorphisms". Source Ind1 is the
  indeterminacy induced by the automorphisms of the procession of
  D^dagger-prime-strips (lines 9105-9107), which includes the D-prime-strip
  automorphisms, not only label permutations. The summary is incomplete.

- Ind2 is described as "tensor-factor copy/isomorphism actions". Source Ind2 is
  the indeterminacy induced by independent copies of Ism (nonarchimedean) or
  copies of the order-2 automorphisms (archimedean) on each direct summand of
  the j+1 factors (lines 9108-9119). The phrase "tensor-factor copy" does not
  capture this definition. REJECT this description as exact source; accept only
  as a rough paraphrase.

- Ind3 is described as inclusions at nonarchimedean places and surjections at
  archimedean places. This matches lines 9282-9291. ACCEPT.

### IPL/SHE/APT (Section 2) — ACCEPT as source properties

IPL and SHE are Remark 3.11.1(iii), lines 9524-9579; APT/HIS is Remark
3.11.1(iv), lines 9593-9668. The proof correctly states them as properties of
the multiradial algorithm, not as extra hypotheses.

But note: IPL links the output data to the input prime-strip by full
poly-isomorphisms of F^turnstile-times-mu-prime-strips. It does NOT by itself
say that the q-pilot object is an output image. The proof uses IPL later to
suggest such a membership; that inference is not IPL alone.

### Holomorphic hull and log-volume (Section 3) — ACCEPT with finiteness caveat

The hull description from Remark 3.9.5(i)/(Ob1)-(Ob2), the rank>1 arithmetic
vector bundle localizations, and the determinant comparison via (Ob3)-(Ob4) are
source-faithful.

The finiteness statement is problematic:

- the proof says "By xi-d, the hull H is a finite region after the log-volume
  computation; in particular h is finite."
- the source's finiteness argument is at lines 10234-10237: one concludes from
  the compactness of the 1,circ U_{j,vQ} and the definition of the log-volume
  that -|log(Theta)| is finite.
- xi-d assumes the finiteness; it does not prove it.

QUALIFY: finiteness is a source assertion from compactness, not derived in the
proof. This is a hidden assumption if the proof is called unconditional.

### Fixed q-pilot value and SHE (Section 4) — ACCEPT with a sharp caveat

The q-pilot is fixed and not subject to Ind1-3; its log-volume is -|log(q)|
(lines 10166-10172). SHE and the xi-e interpretation are source statements.
ACCEPT.

Sharp caveat: the sentence "By the multiradial construction, the q-pilot value
-|log q| is linked, via isomorphisms of F^turnstile-prime-strips, to the
possible output data of the Theta-pilot" only says **linked**, not **equal to
one of the output log-volumes**. The identification of -|log(q)| as an actual
possible output log-volume is exactly xi-f, not established here.

### Closed loop and formal quotient (Section 5) — REJECT at the critical step

The chain description (q-pilot -> LGP-link -> sQ1/sQ2 -> sQ3/sQ4 -> q-pilot)
matches Remark 3.9.5(ix), lines 8383-8405. ACCEPT as source chain.

The compatibility of sQ3/sQ4 with F^units-times-mu data matches cQ3/cQ4.
ACCEPT as source.

The formal indeterminacies statement matches Ob9-1. ACCEPT as source.

REJECT the following:

1. "The formal indeterminacies (tensor-power-twist, upper-semi-compatibility,
   log-link iterates) are treated by Ob9-1: they have no effect..." — correct
   for Ob9-1, but these are NOT the (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3) of the multiradial
   representation. Ob9-1 lists (Ob4-3), (Ob5), (Ob8); it does not list Ind1,
   Ind2, Ind3. The proof's later phrase "(Ind1)-(Ind3)/Ob9-1" identifies two
   distinct indeterminacy systems. This identification is not in the source.

2. "Thus the construction ... constitutes a construction, up to Ind1/2/3, of
   the pilot-object log-volume of the input data, namely -|log q|." The source
   says the closed loop constructs the F^turnstile-times-mu-prime-strip
   simultaneously equipped with the Theta-intertwining up to indeterminacies
   and the q-intertwining (lines 8417-8424). It does not say the q-pilot
   log-volume is itself a possible output log-volume of the Theta-pilot. That
   is xi-e/xi-f.

3. "The closed loop exhibits the q-pilot object as one of the possible output
   data of the multiradial algorithm." This is an overread. The closed loop
   exhibits a prime-strip with both intertwinings; "q-intertwining holds" is
   not the same as "q-pilot object is an output possible image." The source's
   logical form is (q-itw) => (q-itw) AND (Theta-itw/indets) => (Theta-itw/
   indets) (Remark 3.12.2(fitw)), not q-pilot in output set.

The final membership in xi-f is a source assertion that the construction
includes the fixed input log-volume among the possible output log-volumes. It
is not derived by the preceding chain in this proof.

### Inequality (Section 6) — ACCEPT conditionally

### Unconditionality (Section 8) — REJECT

The claim "no additional hypothesis on C was imposed" is not supported:

- the finiteness of h relies on compactness of 1,circ U_{j,vQ}, asserted not

- the q-pilot membership in R_{<=h} is xi-e/xi-f, a source assertion;

These are not additional hypotheses on the initial Theta-data; they are
unproved source-level assertions. The proof is therefore source-conditional,
not an independent unconditional proof.

## 4. Exact gaps

(G1) Ind2 source definition is not the proof's "tensor-factor
copy/isomorphism"; it is independent Ism/order-2 actions on direct summands.
This is a descriptive error, not necessarily a mathematical falsehood, but an
exact proof cannot use the paraphrase.

(G2) The proof conflates two indeterminacy systems: (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3) of the
multiradial representation and the Ob9-1 formal quotient (Ob4-3),(Ob5),(Ob8).
No source identifies them. The final "approximation is precisely the
indeterminacy quotient (Ind1)-(Ind3)/Ob9-1" is unsupported.

(G3) The critical membership -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} is xi-e/xi-f.
The proof restates it as a consequence of the closed loop; it does not derive
it. The closed loop gives simultaneous intertwinings, not q-pilot membership in
the output possible-image/log-volume set.

(G4) Finiteness of h uses compactness of the 1,circ U_{j,vQ}, asserted not
proved.

(G5) No counterexample: there is no known source-valid C where the proof's
steps hold but the conclusion fails. The objection is proof structure and
source misattribution, not falsity of Cor3.12.

## 5. Recommendation

Accept the proof as a **source-conditional exposition** of Cor3.12:

    assuming Theorem3.11 + Remark3.11.1, Prop3.9, Ob9-1, cQ3/cQ4,
    Remark3.9.5(ix) closed loop, and Cor3.12(xi-e)-(xi-f), the conclusion holds.

Reject it as an **unconditional natural-language proof**: the Ind/Ob9
conflation, the overread of the closed loop as q-pilot output membership, and
the finiteness/compactness assertion are exact gaps.
