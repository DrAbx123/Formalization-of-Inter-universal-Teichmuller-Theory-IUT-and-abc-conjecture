## 3. Exact obstruction to G2

### 3.1 Domain/type mismatch

S_q^LS is described as a region in the log-shell container VC(LS) / VC(I)
coming from the 1-column log-Kummer representation. The q-pilot diagonal
S_q={q_v O_v} is a region in the large volume container of local fields
(product of q_v O_v). T_1 is asserted as a map on the large container VC(I).
The source does not provide:
  - an inclusion S_q^LS ⊂ domain(T_1);
  - a map from the small/log-shell container to the large container;
  - a proof that T_1(S_q^LS) is the q-pilot diagonal region rather than merely
    a comparable region after determinant/log-volume.

The C1-LOGSHELL route itself notes the previous dead route's type mismatch:
S_q={q_v O_v} in the large container vs eta^anab_S needing S in the
small/log-shell container. G2 simply assumes the bridge T_1 that the previous
route lacked.

### 3.2 No source global T_1

The LGP action is local at each v and is defined on tensor packets of
log-shells via the splitting monoid; its global assembly is a construction
algorithm with indeterminacies, not a set-theoretic measurable bijection
T_1:VC(I)->VC(I). APT explicitly says the construction is not a set-theoretic
transport (Remark3.11.1(iv)); Ind1/Ind2 act on the construction, and Ind3 is
only upper semi-compatible. A global T_1 with a well-defined action on every
measurable subset is an extra hypothesis.

### 3.3 No class equality in Rss

Even if a local LGP translate is defined, Rss is the quotient of measurable
subsets by EQUAL VOLUME, not by LGP equivalence. To prove [T_1(S)]=[S_q] one
must prove
    vol(T_1(S_q^LS)) = vol(S_q)
and that T_1(S_q^LS) is adelic measurable and represents the q-pilot diagonal,
not merely a log-volume comparable region. The source does not compute either
the LGP scaling of the Haar measure on S_q^LS or the class of the resulting
region. The determinant/log-volume comparison of xi-d is not this equality.

### 3.4 Figure3.1 does not supply it

Figure3.1 records the splitting monoids q^{j^2} acting on tensor packets. It
does not identify the q-pilot diagonal S_q with a translate of the log-Kummer
q-pilot region S_q^LS, nor does it assert a global T_1. The labels/actions in
the figure are local/procession data, not a global volume-class map.

## 4. What would be needed for G2

A source-valid proof of G2 would require all of:

  (G2-1) a definition of the log-shell q-pilot region S_q^LS as an adelic
         measurable subset in the domain of the LGP action;
  (G2-2) a global T_1 induced by the local LGP actions, compatible with the
         log-Kummer correspondence and Ind1/Ind2, with Ind3 controlled;
  (G2-3) an identification T_1(S_q^LS)={q_v O_v} (or at least its class in
         Rss), not merely a comparable volume;
  (G2-4) a computation/proof that vol(T_1(S_q^LS))=vol(S_q) (and nonzero).
None of G2-1..G2-4 is in Theorem3.11, Figure3.1, or xi-d/e. G2 is therefore a
new hypothesis, not a consequence.

A conditional version is legitimate:

    Assume G2. Then [T_1(S_q^LS)]=[S_q], and with Lemma A/eta^anab one obtains
    the eta equality; but C1 remains conditional on G2.

G2 is not a technicality that can be read off from the source; it is the same
LGP/anabelian compatibility wall in another form.

## 5. Is G2 false?

No source-valid counterexample is constructed. The audit rejects G2 as
unproved/extra, not as mathematically false. There may exist a source-valid
choice of S_q^LS and an LGP action for which the class equality holds; proving
it is exactly the missing compatibility. The problem is that the source text
does not provide the global T_1/class equality, and the C1-LOGSHELL proof uses
it as a black-box step.

The rejection is therefore:
  - G2 is NOT a theorem of Theorem3.11/Figure3.1/xi-d-e;
  - G2 is NOT definitional (it involves a nontrivial global LGP action and a
    volume-class equality);
  - G2 is an unproved compatibility hypothesis on which the C1-LOGSHELL route
    depends.

## 7. Verdict

REJECT G2 as stated:
  - no source global T_1:VC(I)->VC(I);
  - no domain/type identification of S_q^LS with the q-pilot diagonal;
  - no source class equality [T_1(S_q^LS)]=[S_q] in Rss;

  - G2 is an extra LGP/log-Kummer compatibility hypothesis.
