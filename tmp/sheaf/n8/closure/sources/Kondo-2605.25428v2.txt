In the present paper, we show that, for a prime number p p and a nontrivial finite Galois extension k k of ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} , under the additional assumption that − 1 ∈ k \sqrt{-1}\in k if p = 2 p=2 , the continuous p p -adic representation of the absolute Galois group G k G_{k} determined by a Lubin-Tate formal group over the ring of integers of k k has the following property: for almost all outer automorphisms of G k G_{k} , its composite with a representative of the outer automorphism is not Hodge-Tate. More precisely, the set of outer automorphisms for which the resulting representation is Hodge-Tate is contained in a closed subgroup of Out ⁡ ( G k ) \operatorname{Out}(G_{k}) of Haar measure zero. In particular, this representation is not Aut-intrinsically Hodge-Tate [in the sense of Hoshi]. In the case where p p is odd, this settles the odd-degree cases left open in the previous works of Hoshi and the author and, together with the known even-degree case, completes the picture for finite Galois extensions of ℚ p \mathbb{Q}_{p} . This exhibits a sharp contrast, from the viewpoint of anabelian geometry, between the p p -adic cyclotomic character and other p p -adic Lubin-Tate characters.
