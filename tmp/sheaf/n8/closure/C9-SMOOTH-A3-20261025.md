## 0. Verdict

(E-A3)   -|log q| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|},
         hence -|log q| <= -|log(Theta)|, hence C_Theta >= -1.

The source asserts (E-A3) explicitly and says it "then follows formally" (lines 10712-10715). The parenthetical "perhaps only up to some sort of approximation" (lines 10709-10710) modifies the construction/identification of the pilot-object log-volume, but the source still asserts exact membership in the set of possible output log-volumes.

An exact proof can be reconstructed from three lemmas:
(L1) full-poly-isomorphism invariance of the normalized pilot log-volume;
(L2) SHE-to-membership: the fixed q-pilot value is a possible output log-volume;
(L3) hull/log-volume finiteness and the upper-bound characterization of R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}.
Given L1-L3 and the source's SHE definition, (E-A3) follows exactly; no epsilon is needed.

If one instead reads the parenthetical literally as an approximation of the fixed value, then no explicit epsilon with a proved sign/bound exists in the source: the approximation error is unspecified and its sign is unknown. In that reading A3 remains an exact gap (G1 below), and no arbitrary epsilon may be chosen. Thus the smooth route is exact membership; the error route is not source-supported.

## 2. L1: full-poly-isomorphism invariance

Lemma L1. Let S and S' be F^⊩×μ-prime-strips linked by a full poly-isomorphism. Then their procession-normalized pilot log-volumes are equal:
    L(S)=L(S').
In particular, if the F^⊩×μ portion of an output data D is linked to S_q by a full poly-isomorphism, then L(D)=L_q.

Proof/source support. The log-volume is constructed in Proposition 3.9:
- packet-normalization: multiplication by e adds 1; procession-normalization: average over capsules (lines 6760-6778);
- global log-volume is invariant under multiplication by units and under permutations of A (lines 6836-6843);
- mono-analytic compatibility: log-volumes are compatible with the natural poly-isomorphisms of Proposition 3.2(i) (lines 6779-6815);
- global log-volume has the arithmetic-line-bundle degree interpretation (lines 6844-6856).
A full poly-isomorphism of F^⊩×μ-prime-strips is a natural poly-isomorphism of the underlying prime-strip data: it permutes the local factors/A and acts by unit/scalar isomorphisms on the log-shells. Packet-normalization is unit-invariant; procession-normalization is an average over the permuted capsules and is permutation-invariant; the global degree interpretation is invariant under the induced line-bundle isomorphism. Hence L(S)=L(S').

Exact source gap: the source does not state "full poly-isomorphism invariance" as a separate displayed lemma; it states compatibility with natural poly-isomorphisms (Proposition 3.9(ii)) and invariance under permutations of A (Proposition 3.9(i)). L1 is the natural-language unpacking of these statements. A formal proof must verify that every full poly-isomorphism of F^⊩×μ-prime-strips is one of the natural poly-isomorphisms covered by Proposition 3.9(ii); this is the precise gap in L1.

## 3. L2: SHE-to-membership

Lemma L2. Let A be the Theorem 3.11 algorithm with input S_q fixed/coric, and let its output data be linked to S_q by a full poly-isomorphism (IPL) and expressed relative to the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure under the fixed input pilot log-volume L_q (SHE). Then L_q is one of the possible pilot-object output log-volumes:
    L_q in R_poss.

Proof/source support.
1. Input data is solely the F^⊩×μ-prime-strip S_q up to isomorphism; the q-pilot prime-strip is coric/fixed throughout the algorithm (Remark 3.11.1(ii), lines 9479-9516).
2. The algorithm's output data is linked/related to the input data prime-strip via full poly-isomorphisms of F^⊩×μ-prime-strips induced by the algorithm (IPL, Remark 3.11.1(iii), lines 9524-9539). In particular, the F^⊩-prime-strip portion of the output is linked to S_q.
3. The algorithm applied to S_q yields output data D_q containing a representation of the q-pilot object at (1,0) on 1,circUQ (xi-c, lines 10618-10639); this is exactly the "collection of possibilities of output data ... linked/related ... to the representation ... of the q-pilot object at (1,0)" in (xi-c).
4. By L1, the pilot log-volume of this output representation equals L(S_q)=L_q.
5. Applying the holomorphic hull and the procession-normalized log-volume to the possible output data yields R_poss. By (xi-e), SHE amounts precisely to the construction being valid/executable/well-defined under the condition that the pilot-object log-volume associated to the input data be the fixed value L_q (lines 10688-10695). Hence the fixed value L_q is one of the possible output log-volumes, i.e. L_q in R_poss.

Exact source gap: step 5 is the source's semantic interpretation of SHE; it is not a set-theoretic derivation. The source says the construction "constitutes ... a construction ... of the pilot-object log-volume" (xi-f, lines 10705-10711), and the parenthetical allows "perhaps only up to some sort of approximation." The exact membership L_q in R_poss requires reading this as exact construction of the value; if read as approximation, L2 is the gap G1 below, and an error term would be needed.

## 4. L3: hull/log-volume finiteness and upper bound

Lemma L3. Under the proof assumptions of Theorem B:
(a) L_Theta is finite (hence in R), and R_{<= L_Theta} is defined;
(b) every possible pilot-object output log-volume lambda in R_poss satisfies lambda <= L_Theta, i.e. R_poss subset R_{<= L_Theta}.

Proof/source support.
(a) The compactness of 1,circU_j,vQ for j in |F_l|, vQ in VQ, together with the definition of log-volume, implies -|log(Theta)| is finite; the proof uses this at lines 10234-10242, after assuming -|log(Theta)|<0 WLOG. The hull is formed from compact regions; the procession-normalized average over j is finite.
(b) 1,circU is the holomorphic hull of the union of the possible images 1,circU; the log-volume is monotone under inclusion (Proposition 3.9 / Remark 3.9.5). The hull contains every possible image, so the log-volume of each possible image/possible output pilot log-volume is at most the hull volume L_Theta. This is the defining upper-bound role of R_{<= L_Theta} (xi-d lines 10641-10681).

Exact source gap: the source states finiteness and the upper-bound interpretation but does not display the monotonicity/inclusion proof in Corollary 3.12; it refers to Proposition 3.9/Remark 3.9.5. A formal proof must establish that every possible output log-volume is the log-volume of a region contained in the hull; this is the precise gap in L3.

## 5. Theorem A3 exact

Theorem A3 (source-conditional). Assume L1, L2, L3 and the source data of Theorem 3.11/Remark 3.9.5/Proposition 3.9. Then
    L_q in R_{<= L_Theta}.                                           (5.1)
Since R_{<= L_Theta} = {lambda in R | lambda <= L_Theta}, (5.1) gives
    L_q <= L_Theta, i.e. -|log q| <= -|log(Theta)|.                  (5.2)
For any real C_Theta with L_Theta <= C_Theta |log q|, and |log q|>0,
    C_Theta >= -1.                                                  (5.3)

Proof. L2 gives L_q in R_poss; L3(b) gives R_poss subset R_{<= L_Theta}; hence (5.1). The rest is algebra and the definition of R. QED.

This is exact membership, not an approximation. The only "approximation" language in the source is the parenthetical in xi-f; it is not needed for the value membership once L1/L2 are accepted.

## 6. Error-term alternative and exact gap

If one does not accept L2's exact membership and insists on the parenthetical approximation, the natural formulation is:
    L_q + epsilon in R_poss,    epsilon in R,
where epsilon is the (unknown) approximation error of the constructed pilot-object log-volume. Then:
- No source bound on |epsilon| is given;
- No source sign of epsilon is given;
- The inequality L_q <= L_Theta would require epsilon >= L_q - L_Theta or an appropriate one-sided bound, which is not available.
Therefore an explicit epsilon with proved sign/bound cannot be extracted from the source. Defining epsilon by the difference and declaring it small would be arbitrary. The only non-arbitrary route is exact membership L2; otherwise A3 remains a gap.

This is the exact gap G1: the source's "perhaps only up to some sort of approximation" is not quantified. A formal proof must either
(a) prove L2 exactly (SHE constructs the fixed value, not merely an approximation), using the exact/full-poly-isomorphism invariance L1; or
(b) define an approximation relation and derive a one-sided error bound from the indeterminacies (Ind1)-(Ind3); the source does not supply such a bound in Corollary 3.12.

## 7. Full-poly-isomorphism invariance proof details

The proof of L1 can be made explicit at the level of the source definitions:
- Full poly-isomorphism of F^⊩×μ-prime-strips: a collection of isomorphisms of the local F^⊩×μ factors, indexed by the prime-strip data, compatible with the global product formula and the procession/capsule structure.
- Packet-normalization: multiplication by e adds 1 (line 6769); this is preserved by unit/scalar isomorphisms of the log-shells.
- Procession-normalization: average over capsules (lines 6776-6778); a full poly-isomorphism permutes the capsule factors/A, and the average is invariant under permutation.
- Global log-volume: additive over places, invariant under permutations of A and multiplication by units (lines 6836-6843); compatible with natural poly-isomorphisms (lines 6779-6815).
Thus L(S)=L(S') for full-poly-isomorphic S,S'. The only formal gap is the identification of "full poly-isomorphism" with the "natural poly-isomorphisms" of Proposition 3.2(i)/3.9(ii), which the source treats as immediate.
