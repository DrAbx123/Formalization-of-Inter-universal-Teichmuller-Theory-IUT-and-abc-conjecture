## 3. Tate q and qhat

The standard Tate j-expansion is

    j(q)=q^{-1}+744+196884q+21493760q^2+...
    q(x)=x+744x^2+750420x^3+872769632x^4+...   (x=1/j).

Substituting j0=1/p^6 (x=p^6):

    q = p^6 + 744 p^12 + 750420 p^18 + O(p^24)
      != p^6.

I verified the first inverse coefficients independently (744, 750420,
872769632). Thus:

- task-183's exact q=p^6 is FALSE for E_j;
- v_p(q)=6 and q/p^6 = 1+744p^6+O(p^12) is in 1+p^6 Z_p.

The qhat argument:

    q = p^6 * u_q,   u_q in 1+p^6 Z_p.
    qhat := q^{1/82}.

Since p=12301 does not divide 82, the map x->x^82 is bijective on the pro-p
group 1+p^6 Z_p (and on 1+p Z_p). Hence u_q has a unique 82nd root
v in 1+p^6 Z_p, and

    qhat = (p^6)^{1/82} * v = p^{6/82} v = p^{3/41} v.

With pi^41=p in the local field, p^{3/41}=pi^3, so

    qhat = pi^3 v,   v in 1+p^6 Z_p subset 1+p Z_p.
    qhat^82 = pi^{246} v^{82} = p^6 u_q = q.
    v_p(qhat)=3.

Thus qhat exists in the local field that contains pi, has order 3, and is not
exactly pi^3 unless v=1. In general v is nontrivial (q/p^6 starts with
1+744p^6). The exact equality qhat=pi^3 is FALSE for E_j.

Classification: q expansion, q/p^6, qhat existence/order 3 = ACTUAL;
exact qhat=pi^3 = FALSE/INVALID.
