## 1. Verdict

C1 is NOT derivable from A1-A3.

The exact obstruction is:

    APT/SHE/HIS are qualitative compatibility/existence properties of a
    construction algorithm. They do not provide an evaluation map from the
    algorithm to a specific output element, and they explicitly allow the
    output to be a collection of possible regions subject to Ind1/Ind2/Ind3.
    The source's xi-f gives only a formal/approximate membership of Q in the
    set R_{<= h}, not the existence of an exact output D0 with
    logVolume(D0)=Q.

C1 is therefore an additional existence/attainment assumption, not a theorem
of A1-A3.

No source-valid counterexample with A1-A3 true and C1 false is constructed; the
obstruction is the absence of an exact output/attainment principle.

## 2. What APT says

Remark3.11.1(iv), lines 9601-9613:

    (Algorithmic parallel transport (APT)) This parallel transport mechanism
    does not consist of a simple instance of transport of some set-theoretic
    region ... Rather, it consists of a construction algorithm that is
    simultaneously valid/executable/well-defined with respect to the
    arithmetic holomorphic structures in the domain and codomain of the
    Theta-link.

The same passage says the algorithm may yield, as output, **various possible
regions**, and that these possible regions cannot necessarily be directly
compared with structures in the codomain; comparison typically requires
further techniques (Remark3.9.5(vii)).

Consequences for C1:

- The algorithm does not define a function "input -> unique output D0".
- Its output is a collection of possible regions, not one element.
- The output may be subject to further indeterminacies/comparison techniques.
- No evaluation at a canonical input gives a distinguished D0 whose
  log-volume is Q.

APT gives the existence of a construction algorithm; it does not give an
output attainment theorem.

## 3. What HIS says

Remark3.11.1(iv), lines 9617-9634:

    (Hidden internal structures (HIS)) One may regard this construction
    algorithm as a construction algorithm for producing "some sort of output
    data" satisfying various properties associated to "some sort of input
    data" and forget that this construction algorithm has anything to do with
    theta functions or theta values.

Thus HIS deliberately forgets the theta functions/values, i.e. the very
quantities that could determine a numerical log-volume. What remains is a
qualitative construction of "some sort of output data."

Consequences for C1:

- HIS removes the numerical theta data from the qualitative algorithm.
- It does not assert that the output data has a specific log-volume Q.
- A qualitative construction of "some sort of output" cannot imply exact
  attainment logVolume(D0)=Q unless an independent numerical/attainment
  theorem is supplied.

## 4. What SHE says

Remark3.11.1(iii), lines 9562-9579:

    (SHE) The construction of this output data, as well as the output data
    itself, is expressed in terms that are simultaneously valid/executable/
    well-defined relative to both the arithmetic holomorphic structure that
    gives rise to the Theta-pilot object in the domain and the arithmetic
    holomorphic structure that gives rise to the input data prime-strip in the
    codomain.

SHE is a **compatibility/expressibility** property, not an existence or
attainment statement. It says the construction can be expressed relative to
the fixed input structure; it does not say that the fixed input value Q is one
of the output values, let alone that there is a canonical output D0 with
logVolume Q.

The later xi-e interpretation of SHE (lines 10682-10704) says expressibility
under the condition that the pilot-object log-volume be Q. That is a
conditional expressibility, not an existence of an output at Q.

## 5. Ind1/Ind2/Ind3 obstruction

The output of Theorem3.11 is subject to:

(Ind1) automorphisms of the procession of D^dagger-prime-strips (lines
9105-9107);
(Ind2) independent Ism/order-2 actions on direct summands of the j+1 tensor
factors (lines 9108-9119);
(Ind3) upper semi-compatibility: inclusions at nonarchimedean places and
surjections at archimedean places (lines 9282-9291).

These are not a group acting on a fixed set of outputs; they are
indeterminacies of the multiradial representation. An output is only defined
up to these indeterminacies. The formal quotient of Ob9-1 (lines 8145-8161)
says the tensor-power-twist, upper-semi-compatibility, and log-link
indeterminacies have no effect on **comparability** of the determinant/hull
objects; it does not provide a canonical representative, nor does it imply
that every real value in the quotient is attained by an output element.

In particular, even if Q is a possible output log-volume in the quotient, the
existence of an actual output D0 with logVolume(D0)=Q is not automatic. The
quotient can identify/approximate values without producing a representative.

## 6. What xi-f does and does not give

xi-f, lines 10705-10715:

    the construction of the subset R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} of possible
    pilot-object log-volumes of output data is subject to the condition that
    this construction of output data possibilities constitutes, in
    particular, a construction [perhaps only up to some sort of
    "approximation", as a result of various indeterminacies] of the
    pilot-object log-volume of the input data ... namely -|log q|.
    The inclusion -|log q| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}, hence also the inequality
    -|log q| <= -|log Theta| ... then follows formally.

Key points:

- xi-f explicitly says "perhaps only up to some sort of approximation, as a
  result of various indeterminacies."
- It asserts the formal inclusion Q in R_{<=h}, where R_{<=h} is a set of
  possible output log-volumes; it does not assert a distinguished output D0
  with logVolume Q.
- The inclusion is a source assertion; it is not derived from APT/SHE/HIS
  alone.
- Even reading the inclusion literally as "Q is a possible output log-volume,"
  it gives existence of **some** output data with log-volume Q only if R is
  defined as the set of log-volumes of actual output data and if every element
  of R is attained. That attainment is exactly C1, not a consequence of xi-f's
  phrasing.

Thus xi-f is weaker than C1 in the following sense: xi-f gives a formal
membership in a set of possible log-volumes (up to indeterminacies and
approximation); C1 demands an actual output element with exact logVolume Q.

## 7. Exact obstruction theorem

Theorem 7.1 (C1 obstruction). A1-A3 (Theorem3.11/Remark3.11.1, Proposition3.9,
Remark3.9.5) do not imply C1.

Proof. A1 gives a construction algorithm whose output is a collection of
possible regions, defined up to Ind1/Ind2/Ind3, and explicitly not a simple
set-theoretic transport (APT). HIS forgets the theta data that would determine
a numerical log-volume. SHE gives simultaneous expressibility, not existence
of an output at a fixed value. A2 gives log-volumes of regions, not an
attainment theorem. A3 gives hulls/determinants/formal quotient; Ob9-1 gives
comparability, not canonical representatives. None of these supplies:

    an evaluation map from the algorithm to an output D0,
    or an attainment theorem "every possible output log-volume is realized by
    an actual output element."

Therefore C1 is not derivable. The source's xi-f adds a formal inclusion
Q in R_{<=h} with "perhaps only up to some sort of approximation"; that is a
different, weaker statement. QED.

Corollary 7.2. C1 can be obtained only by adding one of:

(E1) an evaluation/attainment principle: the algorithm has a canonically
     selected output D0 and logVolume(D0)=Q;
(E2) a strengthened xi-f: Q is the log-volume of an actual output element
     (not merely a possible/approximate value in R_{<=h});
(E3) a direct construction of D0 from the q-pilot line bundle and the hull
     determinant, with exact equality of log-volumes.

None of E1-E3 is in A1-A3.

## 8. What would be needed for a positive C1

A genuine proof would require:

1. an explicit evaluation map from the algorithm to output data in the fixed
   1-column, including the choice of representative modulo Ind1/Ind2/Ind3;
2. proof that the q-pilot value Q is attained by that representative, not only
   a formal limit/approximation in R_{<=h};
3. compatibility of the determinant/log-volume normalization with that
   representative (A2/A3 details).

The source's APT/SHE/HIS do not provide 1-3. The exact obstruction is the
absence of an attainment/representative theorem.

## 9. Conclusion

C1 (existence of an output D0 with logVolume Q) is NOT derivable from
A1-A3/APT/SHE/HIS. The exact obstruction is:

- APT: output is a collection of possible regions, not a unique set-theoretic
  output;
- HIS: theta/numerical data are forgotten, leaving only qualitative output;
- SHE: simultaneous expressibility, not existence at a fixed value;
- Ind1/Ind2/Ind3: output only defined up to indeterminacies;
- xi-f: formal/approximate membership Q in R_{<=h}, not an exact output
  element.

C1 requires an additional attainment/representative principle. No extra
assumption was added; no mixed/E3 profile, arbitrary output, or Lean edit was
used.
