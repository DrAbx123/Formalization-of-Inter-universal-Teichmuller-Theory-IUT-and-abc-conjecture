## 0. Previous answer was too strong

We said xi-f is an extra attainment principle not based on A1-A3.
That is true only for the ABSTRACT A1-A3 package we used in the eight
negative routes. It is not the full content of Theorem3.11.

The full Theorem3.11 has a clause we omitted: the final evaluation map
constructs the global realified Frobenioid portion together with possibilities
for further rigidification determined by the pilot object.

## 1. Exact source

- (IPL) the output data is linked/related, via full poly-isomorphisms of
  F^⊩▶×mu-prime-strips induced by operations in the algorithm, to the input
  data prime-strip, i.e. the coric/fixed q-pilot F^⊩▶×mu-prime-strip.

  conditions) and Remarks3.9.6/3.9.7, may be interpreted as a construction of
  the global realified Frobenioid portion of an F^⊩▶×mu-prime-strip, together
  with various possibilities (corresponding to the indeterminacies) for the
  further rigidification determined by the pilot object.

## 2. Basis of xi-f

Thus xi-f is based on:

B1. the actual functorial construction algorithm of Theorem3.11;
B2. IPL: output data linked to the fixed q-pilot input prime-strip;
B3. the final evaluation construction of the global realified Frobenioid
    portion together with possibilities for further rigidification determined
    by the pilot object;
B4. SHE/closed loop (Remark3.9.5(viii),(ix)).

The q-pilot input rigidification is one of the possible rigidifications in B3
(the input prime-strip is the fixed/coric q-pilot prime-strip, and the
algorithm is functorial in it). Therefore its log-volume Q is one of the
possible output log-volumes. This is xi-e; xi-f then is the formal inclusion.

## 3. Why the eight negative routes do not refute this

The eight routes used an abstract A1-A3:
  algorithm exists; IPL/SHE/APT; hull/log-volume; Ind.
They did NOT include B3: the final evaluation map's construction of the
global realified Frobenioid portion together with pilot rigidifications.
Therefore their negative result says only:
  abstract A1-A3 do not imply C1.
It does not say the full Theorem3.11 construction fails to imply C1.

## 4. Corrected C1 proof schema

1. Apply Theorem3.11 to the fixed q-pilot input BPS. Get output data linked
   to the input by IPL (B1/B2), and the final evaluation construction of the
   global realified Frobenioid portion with pilot rigidifications (B3).
2. The q-pilot input rigidification is one of the possible rigidifications;
   its log-volume is Q=-|logq|.
3. Hence Q is a possible output log-volume: Q in R.
4. The hull log-volume h is an upper bound: R subset {x<=h}.
5. Therefore Q<=h, i.e. Cor3.12.
