## 1. Fixed uniform target and row

Fix F_mod=Q, a fixed complete C with a single selected bad rational prime p
and the uniform all-bad profile rho=1. At a fixed procession level r and
q-exponent s, the B=I row is

    L_B = p^{s-r+1} J^{tensor r},   J=m=pi O_K.                 (1.1)

The B=O_K row L_A is contained in L_B (task-298 R_A subset R_B). Let R_B be
the hull of the row family L_A/L_B over the allowed levels/powers, and let
R_B+arch be its product with the archimedean factor.

NPC (uniform): every source-valid finite bad possible image of this fixed C
lies in R_B; hence the finite hull is contained in R_B and

    h_C <= max(H_A,H_B)+h_inf.                                 (1.2)

## 2. Invariance of L_B under capsule poly-automorphisms

Lemma 2.1. A D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism acts on J=m=pi O_K through an
O_K^times-unit/factor-preserving action: it maps J to J. In particular it maps
J^{tensor r} to itself.

Proof. The D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism is an actual isomorphism of the local
field copies preserving the unit group O_K^times and the log/uniformizer data.
The log of a unit lies in J, and multiplication by a unit preserves J. Under
the log/unit compatibility, the induced action on J is a Z_p-linear isomorphism
preserving J. (Equivalently, use the recorded H_A-lite property alpha_+(J)=J
for actual source automorphisms.) QED.

Lemma 2.2. Every capsule-full poly-automorphism at a fixed procession level
acts on J^{tensor r} by a tensor-factor permutation and per-factor J-preserving
isomorphisms. Therefore it preserves L_B.

Proof. By IUT I Definition 4.10 / capsule-full poly-morphism, an automorphism
of a fixed capsule is a permutation of the capsule index set plus a
capsule-full poly-isomorphism at each index. The latter are D^dag-prime-strip
isomorphisms, each preserving J by Lemma 2.1. Tensor-factor permutations
permute the r copies of J. The scalar p^{s-r+1} is fixed because the level r
and exponent s are not changed by a fixed-length procession automorphism. QED.

Theorem 2.3 (procession invariance). The full procession/capsule
poly-automorphism group at fixed level, factor permutations, and D^dag
isomorphisms preserve L_B and hence its row volume

    nu_B/log p=-s+(r-1)-r/e.                                  (2.1)

Consequently these actions create no finite bad possible-image class outside
L_B.

## 3. Log-link/Theta-link iterates

- Same-path log-link iterations of length h>=2 have empty domain (TAME
  Section 1: first log image is in m, nonunit; next input must be a unit).
- A new unit path is a first log-link with image in J, already the J-factor of
  L_B.
- Teichmuller/root-unit inputs have log0 and do not change the volume class.
- Theta-pilot q^{j^2} powers are indexed by the fixed level j and exponent
  s_j; they are the allowed row family L_B(j), not a new s-independent class.
  A fixed target does not contain arbitrary Theta-link iterates as possible
  images; those would be a different theater/link object, not H_C of the
  fixed C.

Thus the log/Theta actions do not add a finite bad class beyond the row
family. This uses the standard log-domain facts and the fixed-target scoping;
no arbitrary Theta-link iteration is inserted.

## 4. NPC theorem

Theorem 4.1 (uniform NPC from processions). For the fixed uniform target:

(1) every finite bad possible image under the procession/capsule
    poly-automorphism group lies in L_B (and L_A subset L_B);
(2) good finite places have normalized volume0 and do not add a finite bad
    class;
(3) the archimedean component is the separate factor h_inf;
(4) hence the finite target hull is contained in R_B and

    h_C <= max(H_A,H_B)+h_inf.                                 (4.1)

If an actual complete C with TC-B/volume monotonicity is fixed, U follows; for
b=273 this gives the conditional counterexample

    F>=D_B-A_proc=8821.538890...>0.                            (4.2)

Proof. (1) is Theorem 2.3. (2) is the good-place reference O_E with volume0.
(3) is the archimedean product. (4) follows by monotonicity of normalized
volume and the row values. The b=273 arithmetic is the standard conditional
no-go. QED.

## 5. Scope/caveat

The theorem is for the fixed uniform single-selected-bad-prime target. It does
not use mixed profiles, other local types at additional bad primes, aggregate
multi-prime rows, or arbitrary Theta-link iterates; those would be a different
target/profile (E3) or outside the fixed C.

If the fixed target itself contains additional bad primes with other local
types, then L_B of the selected p does not cover them; NPC for that larger
target needs the row classification of those places. This is not used in the
b=3/b=273 single-bad-prime target.

## 6. Conclusion

At each procession level the capsule-full poly-automorphism is a capsule-index
permutation plus D^dag-prime-strip isomorphisms; on J=pi O_K these preserve J
by unit/log compatibility. Tensor-factor permutations permute the J factors,
and p^{s-r+1} is level-fixed. Therefore

    L_B=p^{s-r+1}J^{tensor r}

is invariant under the full procession/capsule action. No finite bad possible
image outside L_B is created by these actions; good places are zero and arch is
the h_inf factor. Hence the finite uniform target hull is contained in R_B and

    h_C<=max(H_A,H_B)+h_inf,

so NPC/U holds for the fixed uniform single-bad-prime target, and the b=273
conditional counterexample has F>=8821.538890....
