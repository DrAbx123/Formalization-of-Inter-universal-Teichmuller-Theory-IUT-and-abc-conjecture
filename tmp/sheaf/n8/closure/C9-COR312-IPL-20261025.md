## 1. What the q-pilot prime strip is

IUT III, lines 9509-9513:

    the q-pilot F-prime-strip, equipped with the rigidification determined by
    the arithmetic holomorphic structure constituted by this vertical line of
    Theta-Hodge theaters, is coric, i.e., remains invariant / may be regarded as
    being held fixed throughout the execution of the algorithm.

This is the **input** F^{value x mu}-prime-strip of the multiradial algorithm:
it is fixed/coric and rigidified by the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic
structure. It is not an output object.

The q-pilot **object** is different from the q-pilot **prime-strip**:

- the q-pilot object is the arithmetic divisor determined by the zero locus of
  the q_v at v in V_bad; its log-volume is -|log q| (IUT III lines 43913ff,
  quoted in the introduction);
- the q-pilot F-prime-strip is the input prime-strip whose rigidification is
  determined by the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure (lines
  9509-9513);
- the output data of Theorem 3.11 is linked to that input prime-strip via
  full poly-isomorphisms of F^{value x mu}-prime-strips.

## 6. Exact theorem extracted

F^{value x mu}-prime-strip of the 1-column, the multiradial algorithm output
possibilities are linked to that input prime-strip by full poly-isomorphisms of
F^{value x mu}-prime-strips induced by the algorithm's Kummer/evaluation

(full poly-)isomorphisms of F^{value}-prime-strips; the F^{unit x mu}-prime-
strip portion is subject to (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3). After holomorphic hull and
normalized log-volume, the resulting possible pilot-object log-volumes are
linked via F^{value}-prime-strip isomorphisms to the q-pilot log-volume
-|log q|, subject to SHE.

## 7. Exact gaps and non-conclusions

IPL does not give:

- a canonical object identified with the q-pilot prime-strip; it gives a
  collection of possibilities linked by full poly-isomorphisms;
- a canonical section or equality of F^{unit x mu}-prime-strips; that side is
  subject to Ind1/2/3;
- a numerical value of -|log q| or -|log Theta|;
- a derivation of the inequality from IPL alone. The inclusion
  -|log q| in R_{<= -|log Theta|} is a statement about the constructed
  output log-volumes; it depends on the q-pilot object/prime-strip
  identification and SHE, not on a black-box equality;
- an explicit canonical q-pilot prime strip for a C9 b=3/b=273 target without
  the BPS/Theta/0-column data. The q-pilot F^{value x mu}-prime-strip is an
  input datum, not a function of the local E_j/q data.

The source is a link/relationship theorem, not a numerical target inequality
extractor. The output is a collection of possibilities; the log-volume
comparison is obtained only after hull, determinant/localization, normalized
log-volume, and SHE, as in Steps (xi-d)-(xi-f).
