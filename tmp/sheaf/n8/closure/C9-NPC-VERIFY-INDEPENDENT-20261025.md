## 1. What IUT I Def4.10/Def4.1 actually give

Definition 4.1(i): a D-prime-strip

```
†D = {†D_v}_{v∈V}
```

assigns to v∈V_non a **category** †D_v with an equivalence †D_v ∼ D_v (the tempered/Galois covering category), and to v∈V_arc an Aut-holomorphic orbispace. At this level a D-prime-strip is **a collection of categories up to equivalence**, not a chosen local field with a chosen uniformizer π or integral structure O_K.

Definition 4.10: a capsule-full poly-morphism uses arbitrary isomorphisms of constituents; an Ind1 automorphism at level j is a permutation of the capsule index set plus **isomorphisms of D^dag-prime-strips** in each constituent. Proposition 4.11 describes the procession of D-prime-strips and label indeterminacy; it does not assert an action on a specific J=πO_K.

Therefore an isomorphism of D-prime-strips gives an equivalence of the underlying Galois/tempered categories, hence an **outer automorphism of G_K** (via π1 reconstruction), not a chosen field automorphism and not a canonical map of a fixed uniformizer/ideal J. The equality

```
D-prime-strip isom ⇒ J → J
```

does not follow from Def4.1/Def4.10/Prop4.11 alone. It requires an extra log-shell/absolute-anabelian functoriality/donor theorem.

## 2. The missing JP gap

Define the needed statement:

```
(JP) Every D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism occurring in full Ind1 acts on the local factor
     K_v by an automorphism of the Z_p-lattice J=πO_K (not necessarily by O_K^×).
```

C9-IND1-GLOBAL supplies JP only in conditional/partial forms:

- H=Φ^{-1}(Sp_{2g}(Z_p)) is a subgroup of Out(G_K); H_A-lite (task-196) says the corrected donor H preserves J and I, but that is for the symplectic subgroup H, not arbitrary full Ind1 capsule-full poly-automorphisms;
- full Ind1 is explicitly **larger** than H: it includes arbitrary capsule-full poly-automorphisms of D^dag-prime-strips, arbitrary constituent isomorphisms, and independent choices at every level (C9-IND1 §3);
- H_A-full is unproved (task-203), and the full Out/Ind1 action on O_K/J is not established. The W⊂J/E24 issue showed that even the additive integral lift is not automatic.

Thus JP is not a source theorem in the frozen corpus. It is an extra naturality/log-shell compatibility input, not a consequence of Def4.10.

## 3. Conditional L_B invariance (valid under JP)

Assume JP. Let `φ_i∈Aut_{Z_p}(J)` be the action in factor i, and σ a permutation. Then

```
(⊗_i φ_i) J^{⊗r} = J^{⊗r};   p^{s-r+1} fixed;
Φ(L_B)=Φ(p^{s-r+1}J^{⊗r})=p^{s-r+1}J^{⊗r}=L_B.
```

Also, if L_A⊂L_B (task-291), `Φ(L_A)⊂Φ(L_B)=L_B`. Therefore under JP:

- L_B is invariant;
- every full Ind1 image of the row lattice family lies in R_B;
- no finite bad row image escapes R_B.

Note: JP does **not** imply the action on J is by O_K^×. The H symplectic transvection on J_W is a non-scalar Z_p-lattice automorphism, not a unit; unit-only claim is false. But any Aut_{Z_p}(J) suffices for L_B invariance. This part of transport's argument is correct **conditional on JP**.

## 4. What remains for U/NPC

Even under JP, two additional inputs are needed for U_uniform:

```
(G1-row) every finite bad possible image is generated by the row lattices L_A/L_B
         (not only the TAME actual subfamily; TAME §6 explicitly disclaims a whole-C upper bound);
(G2) good finite volume≤0; (G3) arch component h_inf;
(G-other) no other local type/Theta/log/Ind3 positive-volume possible image.
```

JP only controls the action on J; it does not prove that all possible images come from the row subfamily. TAME §6 classifies the fixed all-bad subfamily, not the full C log/Θ/∞ output family. Therefore U_uniform/NPC is **not** established even conditionally on JP; the remaining exact gap is (G1-row)/(G-other). Under JP + row exhaustion + good/arch, the conditional theorem gives `h_C≤H_B+h_inf` and b=273 `F≥H_B...` = 8821.538.

## 5. Counterexample status

No explicit uniform source-valid `E_extra` outside R_B is constructed. A counterexample would need either:

```
(a) an actual full Ind1 factor isomorphism whose induced action on J is not a Z_p-lattice
    automorphism of J (JP fails); or
(b) an actual non-row positive-volume possible image (G1 fails).
```

Neither is available: Def4.10 gives abstract category equivalences, but no source theorem computes their induced J action; and TAME §6 does not enumerate other branches. The absence of a counterexample is not a proof of JP/U. This is the exact obstruction.

## 6. Independent verdict on transport's NPC proof

Transport's C9-IND1-J-ACTION proves:

- Def4.10 structure: permutations + constituent isomorphisms (correct);
- conditional L_B invariance from JP (correct conditional algebra);
- unit-only O_K^× claim false (correct).

But it **assumes/asserts** “D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism preserves O_K/J and induces Aut_{Z_p}(J)” as source-structural. Independent reading of Def4.1/Def4.10/Prop4.11 does not supply this: D-prime-strips are categories up to equivalence; the induced data is an outer G_K automorphism. JP is an extra missing naturality/log-shell theorem. Therefore transport's NPC/U conclusion is **conditional on JP**, and its “no uniform counterexample” remains a gap statement, not a proof of NPC.
