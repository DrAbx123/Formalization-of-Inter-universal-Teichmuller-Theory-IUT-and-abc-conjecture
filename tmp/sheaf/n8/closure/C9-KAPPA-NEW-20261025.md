## 1. Verdict

The new angle “use the JW relation under F, the trace pairing, and U/W
integrality to compute kappa” does **not** determine kappa. It yields an exact
linear relation

    y1 = (F(x0) - t F(x0'))/p^s,                                  (1.1)

but both F(x0) and F(x0') are outside the y-basis and are not computed by the
frozen source. The trace relation

    Tr(y1) = (Tr F(x0) - t Tr F(x0'))/p^s                           (1.2)

is likewise one equation with two unknown trace values. No integrality of the
y-basis or of F(x0),F(x0') is supplied.

Therefore the kappa-B theorem remains conditional: kappa<=0 gives B=O_K,
1<=kappa<=40 gives B=I, but the actual kappa is source-undetermined. Models A
and B realize kappa=0/B=O_K and kappa=40/B=I with the same structural data.

## 2. The JW relation under F

Kondo 2025 Theorem D gives JW generators sigma,tau,x_0,...,x_d and the odd
relation

    sigma x_0 sigma^{-1}
      = (x_0')^t x_1^{p^s} [x_1,x_1'][x_2,x_3]...[x_{d-1},x_d],   (2.1)

with x_0' in closure<tau,x_0>, x_1' in closure<sigma,tau,x_1>. The map
F:G_K->k_+ is the composite of abelianization, inverse local reciprocity,
uniformizer projection to O_K^times, and log.

Apply F. Since F is a homomorphism into the additive group:

    F(sigma x_0 sigma^{-1})=F(x_0),
    F((x_0')^t)=t F(x_0'),
    F(x_1^{p^s})=p^s y_1,
    all commutators map to 0.                                     (2.2)

Hence

    F(x_0)=t F(x_0')+p^s y_1.                                    (2.3)

This is the relation in (1.1). It is exact.

## 3. Why (2.3) does not compute kappa

Let

    y_0=F(x_0),  y_0'=F(x_0').                                    (3.1)

Both x_0,x_0' are in the pro-p/wild-inertia part, so y_0,y_0' in J=m. The
relation gives

    y_1=(y_0-t y_0')/p^s.                                        (3.2)

Kappa requires v_pi(y1) and v_p(Tr(y1)). From (3.2):

    v_pi(y_1)=v_pi(y_0-t y_0')-41s,                               (3.3)
    Tr(y_1)=(Tr(y_0)-t Tr(y_0'))/p^s.                             (3.4)

Since y_0,y_0' in J, one only gets v_pi(y_0-t y_0')>=1, hence
v_pi(y1)>=1-41s, which is compatible with any value of v_pi(y1) after
cancellation. The trace values Tr(y_0),Tr(y_0') are unknown; they are not
determined by the y-basis and are not constrained by the source to have a
specific valuation. The trace pairing gives W=kerTr, but y_0,y_0' are F-values
of x_0,x_0', not linear combinations of y_i with known coefficients.

No source theorem computes y_0,y_0' or their traces. Thus (3.3)-(3.4) do not
determine kappa.

## 4. Trace pairing does not fix it

The field trace pairing beta(a,b)=Tr(ab) is symmetric nondegenerate. On
W=kerTr it satisfies W^perp=Q_p1 (task-224), while the Kondo fixed line
U=<y1> is not W^perp in general. The trace functional gives

    Tr(1)=41,  Tr(y1) in pZ_p nonzero,                            (4.1)

but not the value or its p-valuation. The relation (3.4) adds only the
unknown y_0,y_0'. The anti-diagonal trace Gram used in some conditional kappa
statements is an extra normalization, not a Kondo theorem. It cannot be used
to fix v_p(Tr(y1)).

## 5. Integrality U/W does not fix it

Kondo gives a Q_p-basis y_1,...,y_e with y_i in J and W=Q_p-span(y_2,...,y_e).
It does not give a Z_p-basis of J or O_K. The JW symbols x_0,...,x_d are d+1=42
generators, while the y-basis has d=41 vectors; x_0 is an extra direction and
does not supply the missing integral relation. Even if a Z_p-basis of J existed,
J/pi J has F_p-dimension1, so at most one basis vector has v_pi=1; the rest
have v_pi>=2. Hence “all v_pi(y_i)=1” is impossible, and kappa is not fixed by
a hypothetical integral basis alone.

## 6. Two models with the same structural data

Model A: y1=p. Then v_pi(y1)=41, Tr(y1)=41p, v_p(Tr)=1,

    kappa=41*1-41=0,  B=O_K.                                     (6.1)

Model B: y1=p+pi. Then v_pi(y1)=1, Tr(y1)=41p, v_p(Tr)=1,

    kappa=41*1-1=40,  B=I.                                       (6.2)

Both admit the same Q_p-basis/W=kerTr/Sp structural data and satisfy the F/JW
relation with suitable y_0,y_0'. They show kappa=0 and kappa=40 are both
compatible with the source structure; no source selects one.

## 7. Exact obstruction

The actual kappa is equivalent to one of the following missing inputs:

- actual y1 and Tr(y1);
- v_pi(y1) and v_p(Tr(y1));
- the actual F-values F(x_0),F(x_0') and their traces;
- an integral change-of-basis from the y-basis to (p,pi,...,pi^40) plus the
  trace of y1.

None is supplied by Kondo, JW, local reciprocity, the trace pairing, or U/W
integrality in the frozen source. Therefore:

    kappa is source-undetermined;
    B=I is not proved;
    B=O_K is not proved.

This is an exact obstruction, not a counterexample to either branch.

## 8. Conditional kappa-B theorem (unchanged)

If the actual kappa were known:

    kappa<=0          => H_A-full and B=O_K;
    1<=kappa<=40      => B=I;
    kappa>=41        => branch not well-posed inside I.

This is a branch theorem only. By task-264, it has no P6 sign implication:
kappa<=0/B=O_K does not make P6 false; 1..40/B=I does not by itself prove P6.
