## 6. Transfer to F=Q(i,E[6],F_sem)

Let G be the image of G_Q in GL2(F41), and H the image of G_F. Since F/Q is
Galois, H is normal in G and G/H is a quotient of Gal(F/Q), so

    [G:H] divides [F:Q].                                   (6.1)

The file's transfer assumes G=GL2; that assumption is invalid. However the
argument can be repaired using only SL2 subset G:

Suppose SL2 is not contained in H. Since SL2 subset G, H is contained in a
maximal subgroup M of G with SL2 not subset M. Dickson gives maximal subgroups
of GL2 not containing SL2; because SL2 subset G but not M, G is not contained
in M, so G M = GL2 (for M maximal in GL2), and

    [G:G cap M]=[GL2:M].

If H subset G cap M, then [G:H] is a multiple of [G:G cap M]=[GL2:M]. But all
these indices contain 5 or 7, while [F:Q] is assumed 2,3-smooth; then [G:H]
would have a prime 5 or 7, contradicting [G:H] | [F:Q]. Hence H contains SL2.

So the corrected conditional theorem is:

    SL2(F41) subset G (proved by the two classes)
    + [F:Q] is 2,3-smooth
    => SL2(F41) subset H=image(G_F).

This does not require G=GL2 or determinant fullness. The file's proof as
written uses the invalid G=GL2 step, but the conclusion can be retained after
this repair, conditional on [F:Q].
