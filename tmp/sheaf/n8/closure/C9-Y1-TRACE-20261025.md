    W=ker Tr,  Tr(y1) != 0,  y_i=F(x_i) in J.                  (1.1)

Write the actual field identity as

    1 = u0 + w0,  u0 in U, w0 in W,

    w0 = 1 - c y1.                                             (1.2)

Choose B_O=(1,pi,...,pi^40) and let

    C = ([y1]_{B_O}, [y2]_{B_O}, ..., [y_d]_{B_O}) in GL_41(Q_p)   (1.3)

be the change-of-basis matrix from y-coordinates to O_K-coordinates.

    a(alpha)=alpha_+(1)=u0+A w0,                                (1.4)
    H_A-full_H <=> u0+A w0 in O_K for every A in Sp_{40}(Z_p).   (1.5)
