## 2. What SHE exactly says

Remark 3.11.1(iii), lines 9562-9579:

    (Simultaneous holomorphic expressibility (SHE)) The construction of this
    output data, as well as the output data itself, is expressed in terms
    that are simultaneously valid/executable/well-defined relative to both
    the arithmetic holomorphic structure that gives rise to the Theta-pilot
    object in the domain of the Theta-link and the arithmetic holomorphic
    structure that gives rise to the input data prime-strip in the codomain
    of the Theta-link.

The source adds that SHE corresponds to the chain of subquotients in
Remark 3.9.5(viii),(ix) forming a closed loop.

Thus SHE has two components:

(S1) construction operations are simultaneously well-defined relative to both
     arithmetic holomorphic structures;
(S2) the output data itself is expressed in terms of structures from both
     sides.

The fixed one-column claim is obtained by restricting (S1), (S2) to the
codomain side, i.e. the 1-column.

## 3. Proposition: SHE gives fixed one-column expression

Proposition 3.1. Assume the output construction and output data of Theorem
3.11 satisfy SHE, and let

    0,circ U  =~  1,circ U

be the permutation-symmetry isomorphism of the final portion of Theorem
3.11(i). Then, after choosing the 1-column representative of this isomorphism,
every output possibility is a function of structures in the arithmetic
holomorphic 1-column:

    local rings O_{K_v} labeled 1,circ,
    log-shells / tensor packets 1,circ U,
    the log-Kummer correspondence in the 1-column.

Proof. By SHE(S2), the output data is expressed in terms that are
simultaneously valid relative to the domain and the codomain arithmetic
holomorphic structures. In particular it is expressed as a function of the
codomain structure. The permutation-symmetry isomorphism places the output in
1,circ U; the codomain structure is the arithmetic holomorphic 1-column.
Restricting a simultaneously valid expression to the codomain side gives a
1-column expression. No additional geometric input is used. QED.

Remark 3.2. This proposition is a formal consequence of SHE; it is not yet the
full Corollary 3.12 comparison. It does not yet give the holomorphic hull, the
determinant, or the normalized log-volume.

## 5. The A1-A3 schema in detail

### A1. q-pilot input/representation

Source lines 10166-10172, 10571-10581, 10812-10826. The q-pilot object at
(1,0) is represented relative to the 1-column by its q-parameters at v in

Exact source content: the q-pilot log-volume is defined as the procession-

multiradial representation, NOT subject to (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3). It is
computed from the q-parameters of E_F; |log(q)|>0.

### A2. determinant/log-volume comparison

Source lines 10641-10681. The multiradial output in 1,circ U consists of
holomorphic hulls of possible images, and after hull enlargement is expressed
via localizations of arithmetic vector bundles over local rings O_{K_v} of
rank >1. The q-pilot is a line bundle. To compare them, the source takes a
positive tensor power of the determinant of the vector bundle localizations,
then applies the j-normalized log-volume. This produces the two real numbers
-|log(Theta)| (output region) and -|log(q)| (input q-pilot region).

The determinant step is necessary because the output is not a line bundle; the
rank >1 vector bundle localizations must be converted to a line-bundle-like
quantity before a log-volume comparison with the q-pilot line bundle is
meaningful.

### A3. fixed q-pilot value => inclusion

Source lines 10682-10715. The fixed q-pilot value -|log(q)| is not an extra
choice: it is the pilot-object log-volume associated to the input data
prime-strip. SHE says the construction is executable under the condition that
this value is fixed. Hence the construction of possible output log-volumes
includes the construction of -|log(q)| as one possible value:

    -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}.

By definition of R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}, this is exactly

    -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|,

which is the Corollary 3.12 inequality (with C_Theta >= -1).

The source states that this final inclusion/inequality follows formally
(lines 10712-10715).

Theorem 6.1 (conditional fixed one-column comparison). Assume:

     j-normalized log-volume comparison, its output log-volume is
     -|log(Theta)|;

(A3) the q-pilot log-volume -|log(q)| is fixed as the input pilot value.

Then the output is expressible in the fixed 1-column structure and

    -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|},
    -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|.

Proof. A1 fixes the input representation. A2 gives the output region and its
log-volume. A3 plus the Corollary 3.12(xi-e)-(xi-f) argument gives the
inclusion of the fixed input value among the possible output log-volumes.
