## 3. Dickson exclusion: retained

By Dickson's classification, a proper subgroup of GL2(F41) not containing

- Borel (index 42=2*3*7);
- split Cartan normalizer (index 861=3*7*41);
- nonsplit Cartan normalizer (index 820=2^2*5*41);
- exceptional 2.A4/2.S4/2.A5 (indices 114800, 57400, 22960; all contain 5

Exclusions with the corrected classes:

2. l=127 has split class of projective order 5; 5 does not divide 84, so it

Therefore G is not contained in any maximal subgroup missing SL2; hence

    SL2(F_41) subset G.                                      (3.1)

## 4. Determinant correction: no G=GL2

The determinant of Frobenius at l is `d_l=l mod41`.

    l=37: 37^2=16, 37^4=10, 37^5=1,  ord_41(37)=5;
    l=127: 4^2=16, 4^4=10, 4^5=-1, 4^10=1,  ord_41(4)=10.

The two displayed determinant classes generate a subgroup of F41^× of order
lcm(5,10)=10. The old claim `ord(37)=40` and `det image full` was FALSE;
therefore the old conclusion

    G = GL2(F41),  |G|=2,755,200

is NOT proved. The correct bounds are

    68,880 = |SL2(F_41)| <= |G| <= |GL2(F_41)| = 2,755,200.    (4.1)

G contains SL2 and is transitive on nonzero vectors. Its determinant image
contains the order-10 subgroup generated by 37 and 4; the full determinant

## 5. Repaired transfer to F

Let G=image(G_Q), H=image(G_F). Since F/Q is Galois, H is normal in G and
G/H is a quotient of Gal(F/Q), so

    [G:H] divides d=[F:Q].                                  (5.1)

The old transfer used the invalid `G=GL2`. The repair uses only
`SL2(F41) subset G`:

Suppose H does not contain SL2(F41). Then

    K := H cap SL2(F41)

is a proper normal subgroup of SL2(F41). Since p=41>=5, PSL2(F41) is simple,
so the proper normal subgroups of SL2(F41) are 1, {+-I}, and SL2(F41).
Thus K is 1 or {+-I}. The quotient

    SL2(F41)/K  embeds in G/H

(via SL2 -> G/H), so

    [G:H] is divisible by |SL2(F41):K| = 68,880 or 34,440.

Both 68,880 and 34,440 contain the prime 5 (indeed 5,7,41); hence [G:H] has
a prime factor 5 or 7. But [G:H] divides the 2,3-smooth degree d=[F:Q]
(E_j completion: E[6] degree divides 288=2^5*3^2, i adds 2, F_sem adds only
2,3). Contradiction. Therefore

    SL2(F_41) subset H = image(G_F).                         (5.2)

This repair does not use G=GL2 or determinant fullness; it uses only
`SL2 subset G`, normality of H, simplicity of PSL2(41), and the 2,3-smooth
degree input.

If [F:Q] contains a prime 5 or 7, the transfer (5.2) is not justified and
the image over F must be computed directly.
