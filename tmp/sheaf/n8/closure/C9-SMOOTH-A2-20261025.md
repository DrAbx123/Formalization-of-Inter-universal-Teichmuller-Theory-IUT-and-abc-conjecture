## 1. Exact statement

Let:

- H = 1,circ U be the holomorphic hull of the output possible-image family,
  assumed bounded and of finite log-volume;
- E_H be the arithmetic vector bundle of rank r > 1 over the local rings
  O_{K_v} determined by H (Ob1-Ob2);
- det(E_H) be its determinant arithmetic line bundle;
- det^otimes M be the operation of Ob3: a weighted positive tensor power of
  the determinant, normalized by the inverse determinant of O(-) so that
  localizations are trivial for M sufficiently divisible;
- L_H := det^otimes M(E_H) tensor det^otimes M(O(-))^{-1};
- mu_log(H) be the normalized log-volume of H;
- deg be the arithmetic degree normalized according to Proposition 3.9(iii).

Then the exact identity is

    deg(L_H) / M = deg(det(E_H)) = mu_log(H) = -|log(Theta)|.      (1.1)

In particular, the O(-) adjustment has zero arithmetic degree, the factor M
cancels, and the identity is independent of the choice of the sufficiently
divisible integer M.

The q-pilot side is a rank-one arithmetic line bundle L_q with

    deg(L_q) = -|log(q)|.                                          (1.2)

Thus -|log(Theta)| and -|log(q)| are arithmetic degrees in the same normalized
scale, and

    R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} := {lambda in R | lambda <= -|log(Theta)|}  (1.3)

is well-defined. If A3 supplies -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}, then
-|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|.

## 2. Exact lemmas

### Lemma 2.1: degree of determinant = log-volume

Proposition 3.9(iii), lines 6844-6856: for an object J of the global realified
Frobenioid determined by local integral structures, the global log-volume
mu_log(J) is equal to the degree of the arithmetic line bundle determined by J,
relative to a suitable normalization. Ob3-3, lines 7846-7855, repeats that the
arithmetic degree of the determinant line bundle may be interpreted, by working
with suitable normalization factors, as the log-volume of the original
arithmetic vector bundle.

Therefore, for the vector bundle E_H determined by the hull H,

    deg(det(E_H)) = mu_log(H) = -|log(Theta)|.                     (2.1)

The normalization is fixed once and for all by Proposition 3.9(i): the local
holomorphic integral structures O(-) have log-volume zero. This removes the
additive normalization ambiguity in (2.1).

### Lemma 2.2: determinant power multiplies degree by M

By Ob3-2, lines 7820-7845, det^otimes M is the positive tensor power of the
determinant operation with the defining property that, on an arithmetic line
bundle, it sends the line bundle to its M-th tensor power:

    det^otimes M(L) = L^{otimes M}.                                (2.2)

For a vector bundle E_H, det^otimes M(E_H) is by definition the M-th tensor
power of det(E_H):

    det^otimes M(E_H) = det(E_H)^{otimes M}.                       (2.3)

The arithmetic degree is additive under tensor products and multiplicative
under positive tensor powers, so

    deg(det^otimes M(E_H)) = M deg(det(E_H)) = M mu_log(H).        (2.4)

### Lemma 2.3: O(-) adjustment has zero degree

The O(-) adjustment of Ob3-1-2, lines 7811-7819, tensors the determinant with
the inverse of the determinant of the structure sheaf O(-), in order to make
the log-volume normalization consistent with Proposition 3.9(i).

By Proposition 3.9(i), lines 6738-6746, the log-volume of each local
holomorphic integral structure O(-) is zero. The determinant of O(-) is the
rank-one structure line bundle, and its arithmetic degree is zero in the same
normalization:

    deg(det(O(-))) = 0.                                           (2.5)

Hence, for every M,

    deg(det^otimes M(O(-))) = M deg(det(O(-))) = 0,                (2.6)
    deg(det^otimes M(O(-))^{-1}) = 0.                              (2.7)

### Lemma 2.4: exact cancellation

Combining (2.4), (2.6), (2.7):

    L_H = det^otimes M(E_H) tensor det^otimes M(O(-))^{-1},        (2.8)
    deg(L_H) = M mu_log(H) + 0 = M mu_log(H),                      (2.9)
    deg(L_H)/M = mu_log(H) = -|log(Theta)|.                        (2.10)

This is the exact determinant/log-volume identity. The factor M is not an
arbitrary normalization: it cancels between the determinant power and the
normalized degree.

## 3. q-pilot side

The q-pilot object is an arithmetic line bundle L_q (a rank-one arithmetic
line bundle, not a vector bundle). Its log-volume is defined to be

    -|log(q)|,  |log(q)| > 0,                                     (3.1)

by Corollary 3.12 statement, lines 10166-10180. By Proposition 3.9(iii), this
log-volume is the arithmetic degree of L_q in the same normalization:

    deg(L_q) = -|log(q)|.                                          (3.2)

Thus (2.10) and (3.2) place -|log(Theta)| and -|log(q)| in the same real
normalized degree scale.
