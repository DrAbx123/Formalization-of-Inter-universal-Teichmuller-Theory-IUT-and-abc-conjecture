## 0. Source statement

Theorem B / Corollary 3.12 (IUT III lines 1155-1184):
Let -|log(Theta)| in R union {+infinity} be the procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume (average over j in F_l^x) of the holomorphic hull of the union of the possible images of a Theta-pilot object, relative to the Kummer isomorphisms, in the multiradial representation subject to (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3). Let -|log(q)| in R be the procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume of the image of a q-pilot object in the multiradial representation, not subject to (Ind1)-(Ind3). Then -|log(Theta)| in R and
    -|log(Theta)| >= -|log(q)|,                                       (0.1)
i.e. C_Theta >= -1 for any real C_Theta such that
    -|log(Theta)| <= C_Theta * |log(q)|.                            (0.2)

The proof assumes WLOG -|log(Theta)| < 0, since if -|log(Theta)| >= 0 then (0.1) is immediate because -|log(q)| < 0 (lines 10181-10185). The real content is therefore the case -|log(Theta)|<0.

(xi-c) Summary: the algorithm yields output data in 1,circU subset 1,circUQ linked to the representation of the q-pilot at (1,0) on 1,circUQ via F-prime-strip isomorphisms, and expressible entirely relative to the arithmetic holomorphic structure of the 1-column (SHE). Enlarging the collection by the holomorphic hull 1,circU superset 1,circU gives output data expressed not in terms of regions in tensor products of local fields but in terms of localizations of arithmetic vector bundles over local rings (isomorphs of O_Kv) on the 1-column. This is necessary for comparability with the q-pilot line bundle at (1,0).

## 2. Step (xi-d): determinant/tensor log-volume comparison

The output data are localizations of arithmetic vector bundles of rank > 1; the q-pilot is an arithmetic line bundle. Therefore direct comparison is impossible. The source applies:

(Ob3) determinant: form the determinant of the arithmetic vector bundle (hull) to get an arithmetic line bundle in the local/global Frobenioids of the codomain F-prime-strip, comparable to the q-pilot line bundle.
(Ob3-1) weighted tensor powers: because log-volume is a weighted sum, use suitable positive tensor powers of the determinants of the direct summands, with weights N_v (Remark 3.1.1); tensor with the inverse determinant of the structure sheaf O(-) to normalize for ramification.
(Ob3-2) det^{tensor M}: the operation det^{tensor M}(-) is the M-th tensor power of the arithmetic line bundle, with M a positive integer (the N_E of Remark 3.1.1); M may be chosen sufficiently divisible so localizations at all v are trivial.
(Ob3-3) determinants and log-volumes: the arithmetic degree of the determinant line bundle, with suitable normalization, is the log-volume of the original arithmetic vector bundle. Hence for log-volume computations the intermediate det^{tensor M} step may be omitted.
(Ob4) positive tensor powers: det^{tensor M} produces objects comparable to the M-th tensor power of the q-pilot; this is harmless because the Frobenioid structure is unchanged (naive Frobenius functor of degree M; Ob4-1/Ob4-2/Ob4-3).

Applying the procession-normalized log-volume (average over j in F_l^x) to the two regions:
- the region 1,circU (hull of possible images), whose log-volume is -|log(Theta)|;
- the region arising from the representation of the q-pilot at (1,0) on 1,circUQ, whose log-volume is -|log(q)|;

yields completely comparable objects in
    R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} := { lambda in R | lambda <= -|log(Theta)| } subset R,
    -|log(q)| in R.                                                    (2.1)

The source also emphasizes that the log-Kummer correspondence in the 1-column is crucial in (xi-d), to rectify the vertical shift between the unit portion of 1,0F^{...} and the log-shells giving the tensor packets of 1,circU (lines 10671-10681). This is the (Ob7)/(Ob8)/(sQ4) input.

Exact source gap in (xi-d): the determinant/weights/ramification adjustment (Ob3-1)-(Ob3-2) is not displayed as an explicit formula; the source says "we leave the routine details to the reader" (line 7819). The proof of (0.1) does not need the explicit determinant because of (Ob3-3), but a formal proof of the comparison requires the normalization/M/N_v data.

## 3. Step (xi-e): relation of possible log-volumes to the input pilot

The relationship between the pilot log-volume -|log(q)| and the input F-prime-strip is precisely the one prescribed by the arithmetic holomorphic structure of the 1-column, via the representation of the input on 1,circU. By (SHE), "expressibility relative to the 1-column" means:
    expressibility via operations valid/executable/well-defined under the condition
    that the pilot-object log-volume associated to the input F-prime-strip
    equals the fixed value -|log(q)|.                                 (3.1)
Hence the algorithm followed by holomorphic hull and log-volume yields a collection of possible log-volumes of pilot-object output data
    R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} subset R
linked via F-prime-strip isomorphisms (IPL) to the pilot-object log-volume -|log(q)| of the input (SHE) (lines 10696-10704).

This is the key semantic step: the output possibilities are not arbitrary; their construction uses only operations compatible with the fixed input pilot log-volume -|log(q)|. The source supports it by the combination of (IPL) and (SHE) from Theorem 3.11 / Remark 3.11.1, not by a numerical formula.

Source gap: (3.1) is a qualitative characterization of SHE; the exact formal equivalence between "expressible relative to 1-column" and "valid under fixed -|log(q)|" is asserted by the source (lines 10688-10695), not derived in Corollary 3.12. A formal proof needs the precise SHE definition from Theorem 3.11/Remark 3.11.1.

## 4. Step (xi-f): q-pilot inclusion and C_Theta >= -1

From (xi-e), the construction of the subset R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} of possible pilot-object log-volumes is subject to the condition that it constitutes a construction, perhaps only up to approximation/indeterminacies, of the pilot-object log-volume of the input, namely -|log(q)|. Therefore
    -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}.                                 (4.1)
Since R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} = {lambda | lambda <= -|log(Theta)|}, (4.1) gives
    -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|.                                       (4.2)
Thus -|log(Theta)| >= -|log(q)|. If C_Theta is any real number with
    -|log(Theta)| <= C_Theta * |log(q)|                             (4.3)
and |log(q)|>0, dividing (4.2) by |log(q)| gives
    C_Theta >= -1.                                                    (4.4)

The source explicitly says (4.1), hence (4.2) and C_Theta>=-1, "then follows formally" (lines 10712-10715). This is a short algebraic step; the mathematical content is entirely in (xi-d)/(xi-e).

Source gap: (4.1) is the inclusion of the input q-pilot log-volume in the set of possible output log-volumes. It is not a set-theoretic identity; it relies on the multiradial representation/IPL/SHE and the vertical log-Kummer rectification. The source does not give an independent numerical computation of -|log(Theta)| or of the error term; only the qualitative inclusion/inequality.

## 5. Step (xi-g): summary and scope

(xi-g) summarizes: the multiradial algorithm followed by holomorphic hull and log-volume yields two tautologically equivalent ways to compute the q-pilot log-volume at (1,0), as in Fig. 3.8: via the multiradial representation at the 0-column, Kummer-detachment/log-Kummer, and the Theta x mu LGP-link, compared with the 1-column holomorphic hull/log-volume. The two ways are:
- 0-column: Theta-pilot at (0,0), F-prime and F-dagger x mu portions, Kummer-detachment compatible with the log-theta link;
- 1-column: q-pilot at (1,0), holomorphic hull and log-volume.
The equality/comparison is tautological because both compute the same q-pilot log-volume; this is the source of the inequality.

(xi-h) warns that the argument depends essentially on the theory of [EtTh] and does not admit an evident generalization to N-th tensor powers of the Theta-pilot for N>=2; the log-volume of an N-th tensor power must be N times the original, but the sharper inequalities would be false in [IUTchIV], Remark 2.3.2(ii). This is not used in the proof of C_Theta>=-1, but it fixes the scope: the argument is for the first tensor power/pilot, not arbitrary N.

## 6. Exact proof skeleton

Assume Theorem 3.11 (multiradial representation with (Ind1)-(Ind3), (IPL), (SHE), (APT)), Theorem 1.5 bi-coricity, Proposition 3.9 log-volume, and Remark 3.9.5 (Ob1)-(Ob9). Then:

1. Take the single LGP-link (0,0)->(1,0); consider the q-pilot at (1,0) and its representation on 1,circUQ.
2. The multiradial algorithm yields possible output data 1,circU with (IPL)+(SHE), linked to the q-pilot via F-prime-strip isomorphisms.
3. Enlarge to the holomorphic hull 1,circU; express as localizations of arithmetic vector bundles on the 1-column.
4. Apply det^{tensor M} to compare with the q-pilot line bundle. By (Ob3-3), for log-volume the determinant can be omitted; the normalized log-volume of the hull is -|log(Theta)|, that of the q-pilot image is -|log(q)|.
5. The possible output log-volumes form R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}; by (SHE), their construction is valid under the fixed q-pilot input log-volume -|log(q)|.
6. Hence -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}; so -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|; so C_Theta>=-1.
7. If -|log(Theta)|>=0, the inequality is immediate; otherwise the above applies.

This is the exact source proof. It is not a black box at the level of Corollary 3.12: it is a reduction to Theorem 3.11/Remark 3.9.5/Proposition 3.9, with the determinant/log-volume comparison explicitly cited.

## 8.5 A1-A3 source schema: tautological construction-computation

Following the task-374 APT insight, the exact source schema should be stated as three moves. It is a construction-computation comparison, not an explicit construction of a complete C.

(A1) q-pilot input/representation. The q-pilot object at (1,0) is represented on 1,circUQ via the Kummer isomorphisms and log-shells of the 1-column; its procession-normalized log-volume is the fixed real number -|log(q)|, and this representation is NOT subject to (Ind1), (Ind2), (Ind3). The multiradial algorithm of Theorem 3.11 gives possible output data in 1,circU with (IPL): F-prime-strip isomorphisms linking the output data to the input F-prime-strip, and with (SHE): expressibility relative to the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure. Source: lines 10166-10180, 10582-10626.

(A2) determinant/log-volume comparison. The possible output data, after holomorphic hull, are localizations of arithmetic vector bundles of rank>1; the q-pilot is an arithmetic line bundle. Applying det^{tensor M} (Remark 3.9.5, (Ob3), with the weighted positive tensor powers and O(-) adjustment of (Ob3-1)/(Ob3-2), and with (Ob3-3) allowing the determinant to be omitted for log-volume) makes them comparable. Applying the procession-normalized log-volume to the hull region gives -|log(Theta)| and to the q-pilot image gives -|log(q)|; both land in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}. The log-Kummer correspondence in the 1-column rectifies the vertical shift between the unit portion and the log-shell tensor packets. Source: lines 10641-10681, 7735-7868.

(A3) fixed q-pilot value => inclusion. By (SHE), the construction of the possible output data is valid under the condition that the pilot-object log-volume of the input is the fixed value -|log(q)|. Hence the construction yields -|log(q)| as one of the possible output log-volumes in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}. Therefore
    -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|},
so -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|, and C_Theta >= -1 for any C_Theta with -|log(Theta)| <= C_Theta |log(q)|.
Source: lines 10682-10715.

Schema:
    A1 + A2 + A3  =>  Corollary 3.12 / Theorem B.
No explicit complete C is constructed or needed: the argument compares two representations of the same q-pilot log-volume (the 0-column Theta-pilot side and the 1-column q-pilot/hull/log-volume side), which is why the source calls the two computations tautologically equivalent (xi-g, Fig. 3.8). The schema is universal over the admissible C/Theta-data to which Theorems 3.11 and 3.9.5 apply; an explicit C would only instantiate the terms, not change the inequality.

Exact gaps in A1-A3:

## 9. Conclusion

Corollary 3.12/Theorem B is proved in the source by Steps (xi-a)-(xi-g) as follows: the multiradial representation produces possible output data linked to the q-pilot; the holomorphic hull and det^{tensor M}/log-volume comparison produce the set R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}; SHE ensures the construction is valid under the fixed input q-pilot log-volume -|log(q)|; hence -|log(q)| lies in this set and C_Theta>=-1. The determinant step is cited from Remark 3.9.5(Ob3) (and can be omitted for log-volume by Ob3-3); the key semantic step is the SHE/IPL inclusion (xi-e)/(xi-f). The source gaps are the multiradial representation theorem, the hull/Ob data, the exact SHE definition, the vertical log-Kummer rectification, and the determinant normalization, not an unknown numerical estimate.
