D1. Initial Θ-data (IUT I Def3.1): F/F, X_F,l, C_K, V, V^bad_mod, ε; the

D2. Θ-pilot object and q-pilot object (IUT III Def3.8(i)): the Θ-pilot object
is the object of the relevant global realified Frobenioid associated to the
Θ-link; the q-pilot object is its q-side image. The Θ-pilot object is the
object whose possible images are hulled; the q-pilot object is the comparison
object on the 1-column.

D3. Multiradial representation (Theorem 3.11(i)): for a fixed Θ±ellNF-Hodge
theater and its log-theta-lattice, the algorithm produces a collection of
possible output data in the tensor packets of log-shells
  ^n,◦U_{j,v_Q} ⊆ ^n,◦U^Q_{j,v_Q}=I_Q(S^±_{j+1};^n,◦D^⊣_{v_Q}),
indexed by j∈|F_l|=F_l^× and v_Q∈V_Q. The holomorphic hull ^n,◦U of the
union of possible images is taken relative to the arithmetic holomorphic
structure labeled n,◦. Its negative log-volume is −|log(Θ)|.

D4. Indeterminacies (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3) (Theorem 3.11(i),(ii)):
  (Ind1): étale-picture permutation symmetries (permutations of the labels
          j∈F_l^× / capsule index sets);
  (Ind2): Aut_{F^⊣×μ}(−)-indeterminacies acting on the domain/codomain of
          the Θ×μ-LGP-link (horizontal arrows);
  (Ind3): indeterminacies from the log-Kummer correspondences (vertical
          arrows), of upper semi-compatible type.
  −|log(Θ)| is taken subject to all three; −|log(q)| is not subject to them.
  Ind1/Ind2 invariance and Ind3 "inequality from above" are essential to the
  final direction.

D5. Procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume (Remark 3.1.1(ii)-(iv);
Prop3.9(i),(ii)): the average over j∈F_l^× of the mono-analytic log-volume of
the tensor packet; it is invariant under (Ind1),(Ind2) and converts (Ind3) into
an inequality from above, compatible with log-links (Prop3.9(iv), Theorem
3.11(ii) final portion).

D6. Holomorphic hull and log-volume (Remark 3.9.5(i),(vii)-(x)): the hull of
the possible-image regions in the tensor packet of log-shells; its log-volume
is computed after passing to localizations of arithmetic vector bundles
(rank>1) and taking a suitable positive tensor power of the determinant
(Step xi-d). The q-pilot object is an arithmetic line bundle; the determinant
normalization is what makes the two log-volumes comparable.

D7. IPL/SHE/APT (Remark 3.11.1(iii),(iv); corpus lines 9524-9635):
  IPL (Input prime-strip link): output data is linked/related, via full
  poly-isomorphisms of F^⊩▶×μ-prime-strips induced by algorithm operations, to
  the input data prime-strip (the coric/fixed q-pilot F^⊩▶×μ-prime-strip with
  its rigidifying arithmetic holomorphic structure).
  SHE (Simultaneous holomorphic expressibility): construction of output data
  and output data itself are expressed simultaneously in terms valid relative
  to both the domain arithmetic holomorphic structure (Θ-pilot side) and the
  codomain arithmetic holomorphic structure (q-pilot/input side).
  APT (Algorithmic parallel transport): the transport is not a set-theoretic
  map of regions; it is a construction algorithm simultaneously valid in both
  arithmetic holomorphic structures. APT is what allows the qualitative
  logical reading of Step (xi).
  HIS (Hidden internal structures): one may forget theta functions/values and
  treat the algorithm as an abstract input-to-output construction satisfying
  IPL/SHE; Cor3.12 uses only these qualitative properties, not explicit theta
  values.

## 4. Proof skeleton: Steps (i)-(x) (reduction)

S0. If −|log(Θ)|≥0, then −|log(Θ)|≥0>−|log(q)| because |log(q)|>0; the
    Cor3.12 inequality is immediate. Hence assume −|log(Θ)|<0; compactness of
    the ^1,◦U_{j,v_Q} plus the log-volume definition makes −|log(Θ)| finite
    (Cor3.12 proof lines 10181-10186, 10234-10242).

S1 (Step i). Concentrate on a single arrow of the log-theta-lattice
    0,0HT → 1,0HT, consisting of the full poly-isomorphism of
    F^⊩▶×μ-prime-strips
      0,0F^⊩▶×μ_LGP ~→ 1,0F^⊩▶×μ_Δ.
    It splits into a unit portion (F^⊣×μ-prime-strips) and a value-group
    portion (F^⊩▶-prime-strips). The value-group portion maps Θ-pilot objects
    to q-pilot objects.

S2 (Step ii). The units in F^⊣×μ are subject to (Ind1),(Ind2); the cyclotomes
    in the Kummer theory of the étale theta function/κ-coric functions — the
    value-group portion — are insulated from these by cyclotomic rigidity
    (mono-theta environments; [EtTh]; [IUTchI] Example 5.1(v), Def5.2(vi),
    (viii); Theorem3.11(iii)(c),(d)). Hence the value-group portion can be
    compared without (Ind1),(Ind2) ambiguity.

S3 (Step iii). Relate unit and value-group portions of the SAME link
    simultaneously. Using different links for unit and value group is
    impracticable because of non-commutativity of the log-theta-lattice; this
    is why a single link is used.

S4 (Step iv). The 0-column Θ-pilot object must be computed in terms of the
    1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure. Units are vertically
    once-shifted; the solution is to work with vertically coric structures
    (indexed n,◦) invariant under vertical shifts, related to Frobenius-like
    structures by the log-Kummer correspondences; this costs (Ind3) upper
    semi-compatibility.

S5 (Step v). Relate 0-column and 1-column units via the unit portion of the
    LGP-link and bi-coricity of units (Theorem1.5(iii),(iv)); the mono-analytic
    log-shell interpretation (Theorem1.5(iv)) gives multiradial mono-analytic
    containers for the local/global value-group structures of the Θ-pilot
    object. The local units, splitting monoids, and global Frobenioids indexed
    by (0,m) are related to their vertically coric (0,◦) versions via the
    log-Kummer correspondences (Theorem3.11(ii)(a),(b),(c)).

S6 (Step vi). The log-Kummer correspondences (ii)(b),(c) come from splittings
    (up to roots of unity) into unit/value-group portions via Galois evaluation
    (Remarks2.2.2(iii), 2.3.2). Their Kummer theory depends on [IUTchII] §3
    and [EtTh] mono-theta environments; discrete rigidity avoids hat Z-divisors
    incompatible with cyclotomic rigidity; conjugate synchronization from the
    F_l^{⋊±}-symmetry is compatible with the log-link. The MLF-Galois-pair
    cyclotomic rigidity at good nonarchimedean places and the number-field
    cyclotomic rigidity via Q_{>0}∩hat Z^×={1} are also recorded, with mutual
    compatibility of the three approaches (Fig3.7).

S7 (Step vii). The bi-coricity of Theorem1.5(iii),(iv) depends on conjugate
    synchronization from F_l^{⋊±}, compatible with vertical coricity/Kummer
    and with distinguishing distinct labels j∈F_l^×. F_l^× symmetry is
    incompatible with this synchronization and is kept separate; it descends
    to F_mod at the level of absolute Galois groups. Both symmetries are
    compatible with the 0-column vertical coricity/Kummer isomorphisms
    (Theorem3.11(ii)(b),(c)).

S8 (Step viii). The F_l^{⋊±} symmetry requires global ±-synchronizations of
    local ±-indeterminacies; local tempered fundamental groups at v∈V_bad do
    not globalize, so these give pro-finite conjugacy indeterminacies in the
    vertically coric LGP-monoid construction, resolved by [IUTchI]§2.

S9 (Step ix). Copies of F_mod (global realified Frobenioids F^⊛_MOD,
    F^⊛_mod and realifications) translate between the multiplicative structures
    related by the LGP-link and the additive mono-analytic log-shell
    containers. F^⊛_MOD is better suited to the LGP-link; the local portion of
    F^⊛_mod, subject to (Ind3) upper semi-compatibility, is better suited to
    explicit global arithmetic-degree/log-volume estimates.

S10 (Step x). The theory of Kummer-detachment (Remarks1.5.4, 2.1.1, 3.10.1)
    relates the Frobenius-like structures at (0,0) to the multiradial
    representation of Theorem3.11(i)(a),(b),(c), at the cost of:
      (Ind1) permutation symmetries of the étale picture;
      (Ind2) Aut_{F^⊣×μ}(−) indeterminacies of the horizontal LGP arrows;
      (Ind3) log-Kummer correspondence indeterminacies of the vertical arrows.
    Procession-normalized log-volumes give a coarse space invariant under
    (Ind1),(Ind2), convert (Ind3) into an inequality from above, and are
    compatible with log-links/Kummer isomorphisms. The average over j∈F_l^×
    comes from the F_l^× symmetry; tensor products identify multiplication by
    Z at different labels (affecting log-volumes).

## 5. Proof skeleton: Step (xi) and final inequality

S11 (Step xi-a). A q-pilot object at (1,0) is expressed in terms of the
    holomorphic log-shells at (1,0). The LGP-link from (0,0) to (1,0) is read
    as a gluing isomorphism relating the arithmetic holomorphic structure at
    (1,0) to that at (0,0): the Θ-pilot object at (0,0) corresponds to the
    q-pilot object at (1,0).

S12 (Step xi-b). The multiradial algorithm of Theorem3.11 gives a collection
    of possible output data
      (^0,◦U^Q ⊇) ^0,◦U ~→ ^1,◦U (⊆ ^1,◦U^Q)
    satisfying IPL and SHE (Remark3.11.1(iii)-(v)); the displayed isomorphism
    comes from the permutation symmetries of Theorem3.11(i) final portion.
    IPL: the F^⊩▶-portion of the link to the input (F^⊩▶×μ-)prime-strip consists
    of full poly-isomorphisms of F^⊩▶-prime-strips; the F^⊣×μ portion is more
    complicated because of (Ind1),(Ind2),(Ind3).

S13 (Step xi-c). The algorithm yields output data in ^1,◦U linked via IPL to
    the 1-column representation of the q-pilot object, and expressible
    entirely relative to the 1-column arithmetic holomorphic structure (SHE).
    Concretely, the 1-column structure is the ring structure labeled 1,◦;
    enlarging to the holomorphic hull ^1,◦U expresses output data in terms of
    localizations of arithmetic vector bundles over local rings O_{K_v}
    (rank>1), not regions in tensor products of local fields.

S14 (Step xi-d). The q-pilot object is an arithmetic line bundle; the output
    vector bundles are rank>1. Therefore one takes a suitable positive tensor
    power of the determinant of the localizations, and applies the normalized
    log-volume to the regions ^1,◦U and the q-pilot region in ^1,◦U^Q, to get
    comparable objects:
      R_{≤−|log(Θ)|} := {λ∈R | λ≤−|log(Θ)|} ⊆ R,   −|log(q)|∈R.
    Here −|log(Θ)| is the log-volume of ^1,◦U, and −|log(q)| is the log-volume
    of the q-pilot region in ^1,◦U^Q. The 1-column log-Kummer correspondence
    rectifies the vertical shift/mismatch between the unit portion of
    ^1,0F^⊩▶×μ_Δ and the log-shells constituting ^1,◦U. Global arithmetic
    degrees of global realified Frobenioid objects are interpreted as
    log-volumes (Prop3.9(iii); Remarks3.9.2,3.9.6,1.5.2(iii),3.10.1(iv)).

S15 (Step xi-e). The relationship between −|log(q)| and the input data
    (F^⊩▶×μ-)prime-strip is precisely the one prescribed by the 1-column
    arithmetic holomorphic structure. SHE "amounts precisely" to
    expressibility via operations valid even when the pilot-object log-volume
    associated to the input data is fixed to −|log(q)|∈R. Therefore the
    algorithm yields a collection of possible pilot-object log-volumes
      R_{≤−|log(Θ)|}⊆R
    linked via IPL/F^⊩▶-isomorphisms to the input pilot-object log-volume
    −|log(q)|, and expressed via SHE relative to the 1-column structure.

S16 (Step xi-f). The construction of R_{≤−|log(Θ)|} is subject to the
    condition that it constitutes a construction — perhaps only up to
    approximation due to indeterminacies — of the pilot-object log-volume of
    the input data, namely −|log(q)|. Hence
      −|log(q)| ∈ R_{≤−|log(Θ)|},
    and therefore
      −|log(q)| ≤ −|log(Θ)|,
    i.e. C_Θ≥−1 for every C_Θ with −|log(Θ)|≤C_Θ|log(q)|. This is the final
    inequality.

S17 (Step xi-g). Summary: the multiradial algorithm + holomorphic hull +
    log-volume gives two tautologically equivalent computations of the
    q-pilot log-volume: at the 0-column (Θ-pilot, Kummer-detached, up to
    indeterminacies) and at the 1-column (q-pilot, native). The comparison is
    via the LGP-link, communication of the two arithmetic holomorphic
    structures, holomorphic hull/log-volume, and permutation symmetry of the
    étale picture.

S18 (Step xi-h). The argument depends essentially on [EtTh] and does not
    admit the evident generalization to N-th tensor powers of Θ-pilot objects,
    N≥2; the log-volume of an N-th tensor power must be computed by
    multiplying the original log-volume by N. The sharper N-th-power analogue
    is not obtained by a routine generalization; in IUT IV those sharper
    inequalities are in fact false (IUT IV Remark2.3.2(ii)). This is a
    consistency check on the first-power argument, not part of the inequality.

S19 (Step xii). Global realified Frobenioids are essential: local Frobenioids
    only preserve isomorphism classes of objects, so without the global
    Frobenioids one loses control of whether a local object corresponds to an
    open submodule or a p^N-multiple, rendering precise log-volume computation
    meaningless. This justifies the use of F^⊛_MOD/F^⊛_mod in Step (xi).

## 6. Why the final inequality follows

Formal core:

  (1) |log(q)|>0, hence −|log(q)|<0.
  (2) If −|log(Θ)|≥0, the inequality is immediate.
  (3) Otherwise −|log(Θ)|<0 finite; Steps (i)-(x) construct the coarse
      procession-normalized log-volume invariant under (Ind1),(Ind2) and
      upper-bounded by (Ind3).
  (4) Step (xi-b)-(xi-d) produce the output region ^1,◦U and its log-volume
      −|log(Θ)|, together with a comparable q-pilot region of log-volume
      −|log(q)|, linked by IPL and expressed by SHE.
  (5) Step (xi-e)-(xi-f) state that the construction includes the input
      pilot-object log-volume −|log(q)| among the possible output log-volumes,
      so −|log(q)|∈R_{≤−|log(Θ)|}.
  (6) Therefore −|log(q)|≤−|log(Θ)|, i.e. C_Θ≥−1.

No equality is asserted; the inequality direction is fixed by the inclusion
R_{≤−|log(Θ)|} and by |log(q)|>0.
