## 0. Verdict

**JP 不是 source theorem。** IUT I Def4.1/Def4.10/Prop4.11 给的是 D-prime-strip 范畴的等价/outer G_K automorphism，不直接给 J=πO_K 上的 chosen action。JP（每个 full Ind1 constituent isomorphism 诱导 J→J 的 Z_p-lattice automorphism）是额外自然性/绝对 anabelian/log-shell 兼容输入。U/NPC 依赖 JP；无 JP 则 proof fails，但也没有 explicit uniform counterexample。

## 1. What the source defines

IUT I Definition 4.1(i):

```
D-prime-strip †D={†D_v};  at v∈V_non, †D_v is a category with an
equivalence †D_v ∼ D_v (tempered/Galois covering category);
at v∈V_arc, an Aut-holomorphic orbispace.
```

Thus a D-prime-strip is a collection of categories up to equivalence, not a chosen local field with a chosen uniformizer/integral ideal. Definition 4.10: capsule-full poly-morphism = index injection + arbitrary isomorphisms of constituents; automorphism uses permutation + constituent isomorphisms. Proposition 4.11 describes the procession and labels; it does not assert an action on J.

Consequently a D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism induces an isomorphism of the underlying Galois/tempered categories, hence an **outer automorphism of G_K**; it does not canonically determine a map J=πO_K→J. Any such map requires extra choices/reconstruction.

## 2. Does it preserve J?

At least three separate statements must be distinguished:

```
(JP1) the isomorphism induces an outer automorphism α∈Out(G_K);
(JP2) α preserves J=m=πO_K and I=p^{-1}J;
(JP3) the induced map on J is a Z_p-lattice automorphism (Aut_Zp(J)), possibly non-scalar;
(JP4) the action is only by O_K^× and tensor-factor permutations.
```

Def4.1/4.10/Prop4.11 give JP1 (category equivalence/outer reconstruction) but **not JP2/JP3**. H_A-lite (task-196) gives JP2 only for the corrected donor subgroup H=Φ^{-1}(Sp), not for arbitrary full Ind1 capsule-full poly-automorphisms; H_A-full/full Out is unproved (task-203). JP4 is false even for H: the symplectic transvection on W is a non-scalar Z_p-lattice automorphism, not multiplication by O_K^×. JP3 is the weakest sufficient statement for L_B invariance; it is not source-proved for full Ind1.

Therefore the correct audit conclusion:

```
JP1: source-level;   JP2/JP3: NOT PROVED for full Ind1;   JP4: FALSE.
```

## 3. Consequence for L_B

If JP3 holds, then

```
(⊗φ_i)J^{⊗r}=J^{⊗r},  Φ(L_B)=L_B,  Φ(L_A)⊂L_B
```

and the row subfamily cannot leave R_B. Without JP3, a constituent isomorphism could send J to a different lattice; L_B would map outside R_B. No such actual α/region is constructed, so this is a gap, not a counterexample. The full Ind1 automorphism group contains arbitrary D^dag-prime-strip isomorphisms by Def4.10; no source theorem restricts their J action to JP3.

## 4. What would prove JP

A source theorem of one of the following forms:

```
(A) every D^dag-prime-strip isomorphism in full Ind1 induces an action on the
    chosen local log-shell J by Aut_Zp(J) (naturality of log-shell);
(B) Out(G_K) itself preserves J (H_A-full), and full Ind1 is known to act through Out;
(C) a direct D^dag→D comparison making J functorial under capsule-full poly-isomorphisms.
```

None is in the frozen source. (B) is especially not available: H_A-full/B=O_K/B=I remain undecided, and task-203 showed the W⊂J/additive integrality step is invalid.
