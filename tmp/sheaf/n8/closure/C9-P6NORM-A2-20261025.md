## 1. What A2 is

A2 is the comparison step of Corollary 3.12:

    multiradial output region 1,circ U
      -> holomorphic hull 1,circ U
      -> localizations of arithmetic vector bundles of rank > 1
      -> positive tensor power of the determinant det^otimes M(-)
      -> arithmetic line bundle
      -> normalized log-volume / arithmetic degree
      -> real number -|log(Theta)|;

and the parallel q-pilot side

    q-pilot arithmetic line bundle
      -> arithmetic degree / log-volume
      -> real number -|log(q)|.

A2 is the assertion that these two real numbers are comparable, so that the
subset

    R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} := {lambda in R | lambda <= -|log(Theta)|} subset R

and the fixed value -|log(q)| make sense in the same scale.

## 2. Source construction

### 2.1 Hull to arithmetic vector bundle

Remark 3.9.5(Ob1)-(Ob2), lines 7758-7787:

- a possible image is a region P in a tensor packet of log-shells;
- the hull phi(P)=lambda·O is stabilized by the natural O_K^times-action
  (unlike an arbitrary region P);
- hence phi(P) defines the local portion of a global arithmetic vector bundle
  relative to the ring structure labeled by the relevant index, typically the
  zero label 0.

In Corollary 3.12(xi-c), lines 10629-10639, the hull is enlarged further and
expressed in terms of localizations of arithmetic vector bundles over local
rings O_{K_v} labeled 1,circ. These vector bundles have rank > 1.

This is the first reason A2 is needed: the output is not a line bundle, so it
cannot be directly compared with the q-pilot arithmetic line bundle.

### 2.2 Determinant and O(-) adjustment

Remark 3.9.5(Ob3), lines 7788-7855:

(Ob3-1-1) For consistency with the weighted log-volume of Remark 3.1.1, one
takes suitable positive tensor powers of the determinant line bundles of the
various direct summands of the tensor packet of log-shells. The exponents are
the weights (products of N_v).

(Ob3-1-2) For consistency with the normalization of the log-volume by the
structure sheaf O(-) of Proposition 3.9(i), one tensors the determinant of
(Ob3-1-1) with the inverse of the determinant of O(-). The source describes
this as an adjustment for ramification and says:

    We leave the routine details to the reader.

This is the first explicit source gap in A2.

(Ob3-2) The determinant may be a positive tensor power, denoted det^otimes M;
M is the positive integer such that det^otimes M maps an arithmetic line
bundle to its M-th tensor power. By taking M sufficiently divisible, one may
assume the localization at every v in V of any arithmetic line bundle output by
det^otimes M is trivial.

(Ob3-3) The arithmetic degree of the resulting arithmetic line bundle may be
interpreted, by working with suitable normalization factors, as the log-volume
of the original arithmetic vector bundle. In particular, the intermediate
det^otimes M step may be omitted when one is only interested in log-volumes.

### 2.3 Positive tensor powers and q-pilot comparison

Remark 3.9.5(Ob4), lines 7856-7921:

- applying det^otimes M yields objects comparable to the local/global
  Frobenioids determined by the M-th tensor power of the q-pilot object;
- the non-tensor-power and tensor-power Frobenioids are related by the naive
  Frobenius functor of degree M (Ob4-1);
- extracting M-th power roots gives the non-tensor-power Frobenioids up to
  tensor-power twists (Ob4-2);
- the tensor-power-twist indeterminacy does not affect log-volume estimates
  (Ob4-3).

Thus, after dividing by M (or using the normalized weights), the determinant
output is comparable with the original q-pilot line bundle.

### 2.4 Independence of possible images and hull approximants

Remark 3.9.5(Ob5)-(Ob6), lines 7922-7960:

- the choice of a specific possible region is internal to the algorithm; for
  external comparison one works with the hull phi(P_B) of the bounded family of
  possibilities P_B;
- the abstract set-theoretic upper-semi-compatibility formalism of (v) may be
  applied to phi(P_B), det^otimes M(phi(P_B)), and mu_log(phi(P_B))
  compatibly;
- since one is ultimately interested in log-volumes, one works with hull
  approximants in Phi(P) or Xi(P), i.e. with mu_log(H_Phi(P)) or

### 2.5 Log-Kummer and vertical-shift compatibility

Remark 3.9.5(Ob7)-(Ob9), lines 7961-8172:

- Ob7: hulls/determinants must be compatible with the log-Kummer
  correspondences;
- Ob8: the output is a priori vertically shifted; the log-Kummer

## 3. Conditional A2 theorem

The source does not write the explicit determinant/degree identity; it gives
the operations and the normalization principles. The following is the
self-contained conditional expansion.

Theorem 3.1 (A2 determinant/log-volume comparison). Assume:

(D1) the output hull H = 1,circ U is a bounded direct product region,
     stabilized by O_K^times, and defines an arithmetic vector bundle E_H of
     finite rank > 1 over the local rings O_{K_v} labeled 1,circ;

(D2) the operation det^otimes M of (Ob3) exists, including the O(-) inverse
     adjustment of (Ob3-1-2), and for M sufficiently divisible all
     localizations of the resulting arithmetic line bundle L_H are trivial;

(D3) the arithmetic degree of L_H is normalized so that

         deg(L_H) / M = mu_log(H) = -|log(Theta)|;                (3.1)

(D4) the q-pilot arithmetic line bundle L_q has arithmetic degree /
     log-volume -|log(q)| in the same normalization;

(D5) the tensor-power-twist indeterminacy of (Ob4-3) and the upper-semi-
     compatibility indeterminacy of (Ob5) do not change the normalized
     log-volumes.

Then:

(a) L_H = det^otimes M(E_H) tensor (det^otimes M(O(-)))^{-1} is an
    arithmetic line bundle of rank 1, comparable with L_q;
(b) its normalized arithmetic degree is the real number -|log(Theta)|;
(c) the q-pilot value -|log(q)| is a real number in the same scale;
(d) the set R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} is well-defined, and if A3 supplies
    -|log(q)| in R_{<= -|log(Theta)|}, then

         -|log(q)| <= -|log(Theta)|.                           (3.2)

Proof. D1 and Ob2 give the arithmetic vector bundle E_H. D2 and Ob3-1 give
the determinant line bundle, with the O(-) adjustment making its log-volume
normalization match Proposition 3.9(i). Ob3-3 identifies the arithmetic degree
with the log-volume of E_H; D3 writes this normalized value as -|log(Theta)|.
D4 gives the q-pilot value. D5 removes the tensor-power-twist and upper-semi-
compatibility indeterminacies from the log-volume comparison. Ob4 gives
comparability of the det^otimes M output with the M-th tensor power of the
q-pilot, and dividing by M (equivalently, the normalized weights) gives (a)-(c).
The set R_{<= -|log(Theta)|} is then a subset of R; A3 gives the membership and
(3.2). QED.

Remark 3.2. The theorem is conditional on D1-D5. D1-D2 are source operations;
D3-D5 are the normalization/comparability statements that the source asserts
but does not expand into explicit formulas.

## 4. Exact source gaps

(G1) O(-) adjustment. Ob3-1-2 (line 7811-7819) says the determinant must be
     tensored with the inverse determinant of the structure sheaf O(-), but
     explicitly says "We leave the routine details to the reader." The
     explicit determinant/degree identity including this adjustment is not
     written.

(G2) The integer M. Ob3-2 (lines 7820-7845) says M is a uniquely determined
     positive integer and can be taken sufficiently divisible, but does not
     compute M from the weights/ramification. The identity deg/M = mu_log is
     therefore not an explicit numerical identity in the source.

(G3) Normalization factors. Ob3-3 (lines 7846-7855) says the arithmetic degree
     may be interpreted as log-volume "by working with suitable normalization
     factors", but the factors are not specified. This is exactly the
     normalization ambiguity in A2.

(G4) Tensor-power twists. Ob4-2/Ob4-3 (lines 7891-7921) introduce
     tensor-power-twist indeterminacies and assert they have no substantive
     effect on log-volume estimates. The compatibility is asserted, not
     proved line by line.

(G5) Upper-semi-compatibility. Ob5 (lines 7922-7943) uses the abstract
     upper-semi-compatibility formalism to identify the possible-image
     indeterminacy; the compatibility of det^otimes M and mu_log with this
     formalism is asserted.

(G6) Hull approximants. Ob6 (lines 7944-7960) notes that mu_log(H_Phi(P)) may
     be strictly larger than mu_log(P) and equals mu_log(phi(P)) in general.
     Thus the log-volume of the hull is not the log-volume of an arbitrary
     possible region; the comparison uses the hull approximant. This is a
     genuine inequality, not an equality.

(G7) Vertical shift. Ob8 (lines 8097-8127) and Corollary 3.12(xi-d) use the
     log-Kummer correspondence to adjust the vertical shift between the unit
     portion at 1,0 and the log-shells constituting 1,circ U. The adjusted
     objects are subject to arbitrary log-link iterates; Ob9 asserts these do
     not affect the log-volume comparison via realified semi-simplifications.
     The explicit adjustment is not written in Corollary 3.12.

## 6. Conclusion

A2 is the determinant/log-volume bridge:

    rank > 1 arithmetic vector bundle localizations
      -> hull-stabilized bundle
      -> det^otimes M with O(-)^{-1} adjustment
      -> arithmetic line bundle
      -> arithmetic degree = normalized log-volume
      -> -|log(Theta)|;

the q-pilot line bundle gives -|log(q)| in the same normalized scale. The
source provides the operations and the normalization principles, but the
explicit determinant/degree identity, the O(-) adjustment, the integer M, and
the invariance under tensor-power twists/upper-semi-compatibility are not
written out. These are exact gaps G1-G7, not black boxes.
