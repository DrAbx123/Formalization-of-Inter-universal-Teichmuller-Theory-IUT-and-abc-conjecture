## 5. Theorem 4: row inclusion R_A subset R_B (task-291)

Theorem 5.1. For the C9 all-bad r-tuple, with

    L_A=sum_i p^s pi_i D_r (B=O_K),  L_B=p^{s-r+1}J^{tensor r} (B=I),
    J=pi O_K, D_r=O_K^{tensor r},

one has L_A subset L_B; r=1 equality, r>=2 strict. Consequently
R_A=Hull(L_A) subset R_B=Hull(L_B).

Proof. For the i-th generator e_i=p^s pi_i, multiply the B=I generator
b=p^{s-r+1}pi_1...pi_r by prod_{j!=i}(p/pi_j) in D_r:
  b*prod(p/pi_j)=p^{s-r+1}pi_i p^{r-1}=p^s pi_i=e_i.
Since p/pi_j in O_{K_j}, the multiplier is integral, so e_i in L_B. The same
for every i, hence L_A subset L_B. For r=1 both lattices are p^s pi_1 O, equal;
for r>=2 b has pi-valuation one in every factor while L_A generators have it
in one factor, so strict. QED.

Normalized heights:
  nu_A/log p=-s-1/e, nu_B/log p=-s+(r-1)-r/e,
  nu_B-nu_A=(r-1)(1-1/e)>0 for r>=2.

## 7. Theorem 6: BPS root/label ambiguity (task-292)

  theta(U_j)/theta(U_0)=q^{-j^2/2},  g_{r,v,j}=zeta_{r,v} a^{j^2}, a^{82}=q.
Replacing a by zeta_82 a (nontrivial 82nd root) multiplies g_{r,v,j} by
zeta_82^{j^2}; the same curve/q/qhat and local I_546/b=273 data are unchanged.
Both choices satisfy the IUT II value-profile/zero-label axioms. The BPS label
also depends on evaluation/zero-label splitting and gluing; these can be chosen
compatibly in two ways. No E546 function selects one. QED sketch.

  (O1) E546 supplies the curve/local q, not the global mono-theta environment,
       theta/Gaussian monoids, evaluation points, Kummer classes;
  (O2) theta value has root/cyclotome and zero-label choices not fixed by E546;

## 9. Next attack: the single most promising source theorem

Target theorem T_U (volume-level target saturation upper). Prove

    (U)  h_C <= max(H_A,H_B)+h_inf = H_B+h_inf

for the actual target hull, at normalized-volume level and independent of BPS
root/label choices. After task-291, it is enough to prove the volume-level

h_C<=H_B+h_inf.

Why this is the single most promising next source theorem:

  - task-291 has already proved R_A subset R_B, so the two-branch upper
    envelope reduces to the B=I row hull. The only remaining upper-side gap is
    the target possible-image hull being generated by that row hull + arch
    (S2/S3/S4).
  - If U is proved, task-293 gives a decisive split: b=273 becomes a
    conditional P6 counterexample with margin >=8821.54 in both branches; b=3
    gets the exact h_inf threshold -S_3, with P6 in the B=I/region corridor and
    counterexamples below the threshold. This is much stronger than any
    B-branch-only result.
  - task-292's BPS ambiguity is label/root-level; the theta root choices are
    roots of unity and do not obviously change normalized hull volumes. A
    volume-level U theorem can bypass the missing canonical BPS label. Proving
    BPS-volume invariance is the natural first lemma of T_U, not an extra
    arbitrary assumption.
  - Without U, the b=273 upper side is open; C1 alone gives only F<=D_A/B-h_inf
    (task-290/293). No other all-hands result supplies this inequality.

Supporting sublemma T_U0 (BPS/root volume invariance): the admissible theta
root/cyclotome choices act by roots of unity (or zero normalized log-volume),
so the normalized log-volume of the target hull is independent of the BPS
root/label choice. If T_U0 fails, the BPS ambiguity itself changes hull volume
and U must be replaced by a range; that failure is the exact alternative
obstruction.

Fallback: if U is false, the b=273 upper side requires an upper bound on every
extra target region; task-276 shows no canonical extra of size >=8808, so the
fallback is the b=273 exclusion problem, not a P6 proof.

Second priority remains T_B (actual B=I/kappa) for the b=3 B=I corridor and
T_arch (h_inf>=-S_3); but T_U is the single highest-leverage missing theorem:
it decides the upper side for both b=3 and b=273 without needing to choose a
BPS label or an actual B branch.
