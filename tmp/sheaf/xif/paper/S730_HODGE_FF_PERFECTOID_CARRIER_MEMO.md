## 0. Verdict

**Explicit rejection for both gaps.**  None of the three theories supplies a paper-faithful carrier.

* For q[2u]: the paper root is an element of O^triangle_{K_v} (nonzero integral elements of the local field K_v), determined up to mu_{2l}.  Its existence is an arithmetic divisibility condition, not a root-existence-in-a-big-field condition.  A 2l-th root in a Z-valued valuation ring forces 2l | ord(q).  The repository qVFamily has Nat.Coprime(order, l), so it cannot be the paper q[1u].  B_dR also has value group Z and therefore cannot contain the root of qVFamily either; B_cris/B_st are not valued fields; perfectoid tilting changes the value group to Q and the characteristic, i.e. it solves the problem by changing the paper object.
* For the Theta-pilot hull log: the missing object is not a field carrier.  The paper hull is the least lambda-O region containing the possible image (an intersection of candidates), and the paper log-volume is the procession average over the l* labels.  The current rootThetaHull is the closure of the union of the per-label regions, which for nonnegative coefficients is the largest region (the j = 1 region), not the paper hull.  The L-extension product Haar is a different carrier (field L, product max-ball) and is auxiliary only.  None of p-adic Hodge theory, the FF curve, or tilting changes this.

The paper-faithful route is instead:

  A. construct the actual paper q_v with a 2l-th root in O^triangle_{K_v} (equivalently 2l | ord(q_v)); then the coefficient c = ord(q_v)/(2l) is an integer and the existing ArithmeticTuplePureFractionalScalingData / normalized-Haar machinery applies exactly;
  or
  B. if the source root cannot be constructed, use the paper's formal divisor-monoid / log-theta-lattice carrier with a real exponent c, not a Haar measure on the completion.

The root stack/mu_{2l} is paper-faithful only as the mu_{2l}-torsor of roots and its isometric invariance (compiled in S730).  It cannot create a root whose valuation is not divisible by 2l.

## 2. The decisive valuation obstruction

### 2.1 Mathematical statement

Let K be a mixed-characteristic local field with ring of integers O_K and discrete valuation ord.  If q in O_K is nonzero and r in K satisfies r^n = q, then n | ord(q).  Indeed ord(r^n) = n ord(r) = ord(q), and ord(r) is an integer.

Equivalently: a 2l-th root of q in K_v exists only if 2l | ord(q).  A root stack / mu_{2l}-gerbe over K_v does not change the value group; it only records the mu_{2l} ambiguity among roots that already exist.

For the paper q_v, the root is required to lie in O^triangle_{K_v}, the nonzero integral elements.  Therefore the paper's own assertion forces 2l | ord(q_v).

### 2.2 Source-level tension that must be resolved before choosing q

IUT I Definition 3.1(c) says l is prime to the orders (local heights) of the q-parameters.  IUT I Example 3.2(iv) says q[1u] admits a 2l-th root in O^triangle_{K_v}.  If "order" is the valuation/local height, then:

    q[1u] in O^triangle_{K_v}, q[2u]^{2l} = q[1u]
      => ord(q[1u]) = 2l · ord(q[2u])
      => 2l | ord(q[1u])
      => l | ord(q[1u]),

which contradicts Definition 3.1(c).  There are only two possibilities:

1. the paper's "order" is not the valuation (then the repository's qVFamily.order, formalized as the valuation exponent, is the wrong invariant for clause (c)); or
2. the paper's clauses (b)/(c)/(d) and the root assertion cannot all hold with the same q.

This memo does not adjudicate the paper's convention.  It records the consequence for Lean: **do not construct a paper-faithful q[2u] until the lead fixes which invariant "order" is.**  The current repository formalizes order as the valuation exponent (SourceDefinition31D4TateQIdentification; S342), so under the current source model the paper root is impossible for qVFamily.  This is not a missing Kummer/Hensel input and no p-adic Hodge/FF/tilt construction can repair it.

## 3. Audit of the three proposed theories

### 3.1 p-adic Hodge theory (B_dR, B_cris, B_st, B_HT, A_inf)

**Concrete objects and standard facts.**

* B_dR^+ is a complete DVR with residue field C_p and uniformizer t; B_dR is its fraction field.  Its valuation v has value group Z, normalized by v(t) = 1.
* B_cris and B_st are Q_p-algebras with Frobenius; B_cris^+ is a subring of B_dR^+, not a valuation ring.  B_HT = direct sum over n in Z of C_p(n) is a graded ring, not a field.  A_inf is not a field.
* The de Rham / crystalline / semistable period rings are design for p-adic Galois representations and p-power ramification, not for a tame 2l-Kummer root (l is prime to p by Definition 3.1(c)).

**Root criterion in B_dR.**  A 2l-th root of q in B_dR exists only if 2l | v(q).  For the repository qVFamily, gcd(ord(q), l) = 1, and v(q) is a multiple of ord(q) in the normalization of B_dR (the exact multiple depends on the embedding of K_v into B_dR^+); hence 2l does not divide v(q), so B_dR contains no root of qVFamily.  For the paper q_v, if 2l | ord(q_v), then q_v is already a 2l-th power in K_v, so B_dR adds nothing.

**Log-volume.**  The paper's -|log(Theta)| is a mono-analytic log-volume on a log-shell in a finite-dimensional K_v-tensor block, with the procession normalization.  B_dR / B_cris / B_st carry no canonical Haar measure, no log-shell lattice, and no procession labels compatible with this functional.  The de Rham period is not the paper's log-volume; identifying them would be a surrogate and would change the object.

**Lean feasibility.**  Mathlib has no B_dR, B_cris, B_st, A_inf, or their topology.  Formalizing B_dR alone is a multi-year project (perfectoid/condensed foundations, Fontaine's rings, the theta map).  Even if formalized, it would not give the paper log-volume.  The existing actual carrier LocalIntegralMonoid (IUT II, Frobenioid/LocalIntegralMonoid.lean) is the nonzero integral monoid of the algebraic closure of Q_p, not of K_v; it is a genuine O^triangle-type carrier for roots up to mu, but it is not the paper K_v and it has no paper log-volume.

**Verdict: reject as paper-faithful carrier.**  It may be cited only for the no-go fact "Z-valued valuation rings cannot contain a root whose valuation is not divisible by 2l."

### 3.2 Fargues–Fontaine curve

**Concrete objects and standard facts.**

* For a perfectoid field F (e.g. C_p), the Fargues–Fontaine curve X_F is a regular noetherian scheme of dimension 1; its closed points correspond to untilts of F up to Frobenius; the untilt data are q-pairs (F^#, phi).
* Line bundles on X_F are classified by an integer degree: O(n), n in Z.  The curve is the geometric home of p-adic Hodge theory and the p-adic Simpson correspondence.

**Why it cannot be the paper carrier.**

* The "q" in a q-pair is an untilt/Frobenius parameter, not the Tate q-parameter q_v of the elliptic curve E_v.  There is no theorem identifying the FF q with the IUT q_v; constructing such an identification would be a surrogate.
* The paper's q[2u] is a 2l-th root in O^triangle_{K_v}, with a Z-valued valuation.  The FF curve has no such element; it has line bundles and untilts.
* There is no mu_{2l}-root-stack over X_F in the paper's sense, no lambda-O hull, no log-shell, and no procession-normalized mono-analytic log-volume.  The degree of a line bundle is not the paper log-volume.
* l is prime to p, so the 2l-Kummer class is tame; the FF curve is a p-adic/Frobenius tool, not a tame Kummer tool.

**Lean feasibility.**  Mathlib has no Fargues–Fontaine curve, no perfectoid spaces, no diamonds, no untilts.  Formalizing the curve and the untilt classification is far beyond the project; it would still not produce the paper log-volume.

**Verdict: reject as paper-faithful carrier.**  It is a different object in a different regime.

### 3.3 Perfectoid tilting

**Concrete objects and standard facts.**

* Tilting gives an equivalence between perfectoid K-algebras and perfectoid K^flat-algebras, preserving Galois categories; for C_p the tilt C_p^flat is a perfectoid field of characteristic p with value group Q.
* A 2l-th root of the tilt of q exists in C_p^flat because its value group is Q and the field is algebraically closed (and 2l is invertible in characteristic p).  But this root lives in characteristic p, not in K_v.

**Why it cannot be the paper carrier.**

* The tilt is not a ring homomorphism to K_v; there is no map C_p^flat -> K_v taking the tilt of q to q.  The root is not an element of O^triangle_{K_v}.
* Tilting does not preserve the p-adic Haar measure / log-volume.  The paper's mono-analytic log-volume is characteristic 0 and lives on a K_v-tensor block; the tilt has no compatible measure.
* 2l is prime to p, so the 2l-Kummer theory is tame.  Perfectoid tilting is designed for p-power ramification; it is the wrong tool.  The tilt "solves" the root by changing the value group from Z to Q, which is exactly the surrogate the task forbids.

**Lean feasibility.**  Mathlib has no perfectoid spaces or tilting equivalence.  Formalizing them is a major project and would not give the paper log-volume.

**Verdict: reject as paper-faithful carrier.**  It changes the field, the characteristic, and the value group.

### 3.4 Root stack / mu_{2l}: what is paper-faithful

The paper itself says q[2u] is determined up to a mu_{2l}-multiple.  The paper-faithful content is therefore a mu_{2l}-torsor of roots, not a single root and not a function monoid.

S730PaperRootCarrierActual.lean constructs, for a normed field K and a unit q:

    structure RootTorsor (n : Nat) (q : K^*) where
      root : K^*
      root_pow : root^n = q

    def muN (n : Nat) (q : K^*) : Subgroup (K^*) := {zeta | zeta^n = 1}
    def muAction (zeta : muN n q) (r : RootTorsor n q) : RootTorsor n q

and proves:

* root_ratio_mem_mu : any two roots differ by an element of muN;
* exists_mu_mul_eq : the action is transitive;
* mu_norm_eq_one : every mu_n element has norm one;
* root_norm_independent : the norm of a root is independent of the chosen root;
* root_pow_norm_independent : the norm of root^(j^2) is independent of the chosen root.

This is the precise sense in which the root stack/mu_{2l} is paper-faithful: it captures the indeterminacy, and the hull radius/log-volume is invariant under the mu_{2l} action because mu_{2l} acts by isometries.  It does **not** create a root when 2l does not divide the valuation; a nontrivial torsor exists in the algebraic closure, but its elements are not in K_v.

The S392 RootStackSectionMonoidActual is **not** paper-faithful: its carrier is RootObj -> Multiplicative Z, a function monoid with a delta injection, and its qSection_toCompletion is the constant map to q.  Do not extend it as the paper carrier.

## 4. The Theta-pilot hull log: additional gaps that no Hodge/FF/tilt carrier fixes

### 4.1 The paper hull is the least lambda-O region, not the closure of the union

IUT III Remark 3.9.5 and LiteralIQHolomorphicHullActual.lean (lines 11–14, 92–97) define the holomorphic hull of a possible-image set P as the intersection of all lambda-O regions (nonzero lambda) containing P — the least such region.

The current RootHullContainmentInequalityActual.rootThetaHull is

    closure (union over j of rootThetaRegion j),
    rootThetaRegion j = F(j^2 c) * O_hol.

For c >= 0 and j^2 >= 1, the fractional lattices are antitone: F(j^2 c) <= F(c), and the j = 1 region is F(c) O_hol.  Hence the union is exactly F(c) O_hol (the largest region), and the closure is the largest region.  The paper hull (intersection) is instead the smallest region containing all labels, morally F((l*)^2 c) O_hol.  These are opposite extremes.  Therefore:

* the current rootThetaHull is not the paper holomorphic hull;
* the current paperRootThetaHullLog / perBThetaHullLog (nonpositive, close to 0) is not the paper -|log(Theta)|;
* S726's proof that paperPerBThetaHullLog <= 0 and that it cannot equal the positive S663 average is correct for the current object, but it does not prove a paper-level falsehood; it shows that the current hull is the wrong region.

The L-extension image hull (PaperRootExtensionThetaHullAuditActual.rootExtensionProductImageHull) is the coordinatewise max-norm product ball.  It is a least product ball containing the image, but it is not the per-label lambda-O intersection, and it lives in the product of the L_b, not in K_v.

### 4.2 The paper log is a procession average over l* labels

IUT II Remark 4.7.3 and IUT III Corollary 3.12 average over j in F_l^x, with l* = (l-1)/2 classes and weight 1/l*.  S694ThetaAverageNormalizationActual formalizes this:

    PaperThetaLabel l = Fin (lStar l.value)
    paperThetaLabelWeight l = (lStar l.value)^{-1}
    paperThetaLabelAverage f = (1/l*) * sum_{i : PaperThetaLabel} f i.

The S657/S663/S729 rootThetaMagnitudeProcession uses SignedLabel l.value (cardinality l) with weight 1/l, not l* with weight 1/l*.  These are different averages; for l = 29, the difference is not negligible.  The paper-faithful average is S694's, not the uniform SignedLabel average.

### 4.3 Consequence for the exact gap

The exact gap "paper Theta-pilot hull log" decomposes into four independent obligations:

1. root/coefficient: actual q[2u] in O^triangle_{K_v} (2l | ord), or a formal divisor-monoid exponent;
2. hull operation: the least lambda-O region containing the possible image (intersection), per label;
3. denomination: the procession average over the l* labels (S694), not SignedLabel;
4. log-volume: an actual Haar/normalized log-volume on the correct hull, with the paper's degree normalization.

p-adic Hodge theory, the FF curve, and perfectoid tilting address none of 1–4.  The root stack addresses only the mu_{2l} indeterminacy inside 1.

## 5. Paper-faithful routes

### Route A (preferred): actual paper q_v with a 2l-th root in K_v

Source theorem to construct (not to assume):

    PaperQRootInField (q_v) : exists r : K_v^*, r^(2l) = q_v and r in O^triangle_{K_v}.

The paper's route is the 2-torsion assumption plus K = F(E[l]): for the Tate curve, E[2] rational gives q^{1/2} in F_v, E[l] rational over K gives q^{1/l} in K_v, and l odd combines them to q^{1/2l} in K_v.  In Lean, the exact source theorem is a Tate-uniformization statement; the current SourceTateComparisonWitness only supplies a q with order coprime to l, so it cannot be the paper q_v.  This is the real source gap.

Given PaperQRootInField, the coefficient c = ord(q_v)/(2l) is an integer.  Then:

* the local generator r has norm p^{-c} (normalized completion norm);
* ArithmeticTuplePureFractionalScalingData at c is constructed by arithmeticTuplePureFractionalScalingData_ofGenerator with generator r, commonExponent 1, integerExponent c, localUnit r / p^c (norm one);
* arithmeticTupleNormalizedLogVolume_fractionalLattice_mul_holomorphic gives mu_log(F(c) O_hol) = -c log p;
* for each label j, the least lambda-O hull of q[2u]^{j^2} is F(j^2 c) O_hol, with log -j^2 c log p;
* the paper procession average is (1/l*) sum_{j=1}^{l*} (-j^2 c log p) (sign depending on the -|log| convention);
* the comparison with the q-pilot uses the same root normalization (see the separate q-pilot convention issue below).

Lean plan (next file, S731):
* structure PaperQRootInField with root, root_pow, root_integral;
* theorem paperRootScalingData_of_fieldRoot : ArithmeticTuplePureFractionalScalingData at c;
* theorem paperPerLabelHullLog_eq : log(F(j^2 c) O_hol) = -j^2 c log p;
* theorem paperProcessionAverage_eq : the l* average formula;
* theorem paperHullLog_eq_average : the paper hull log equals the procession average.

Difficulty: the source theorem (Tate uniformization / E[2l] rational) is the hard part; the hull/log algebra is routine with the existing API.

### Route B (fallback): formal divisor-monoid / log-theta lattice

If the source q cannot be shown to have a 2l-th root in K_v, the paper's own object is still a **divisor-monoid section**:

    log_Phi(q[2u]) in Phi_Cv, a constant section of the divisor monoid.

The paper's log-volume is a monoid functional on this section, not a Haar measure on a subset of the completion.  A paper-faithful Lean carrier would be a structure with:

* a real exponent vector c (not necessarily integral);
* a log-volume functional logVol on exponent vectors;
* logVol(c) = -(sum c) log p (the monoid/degree normalization, IUT IV Prop 1.4(i));
* a mu_{2l}-invariance proof (the exponent is unchanged by the root choice);
* a per-label hull operation and the l* average.

This is a formal carrier, not a completion Haar.  It must be clearly separated from arithmeticTupleNormalizedLogVolume; do not define logVol by the formula and call it a theorem without a source identification.  The source identification is exactly the paper's definition of the mono-analytic log-volume on the divisor monoid.

Route B is acceptable only if the lead accepts the divisor-monoid semantics as the paper carrier.  It is not a substitute for the actual paper root if the paper's O^triangle root is required.

### Route C (reject): what not to do

* Do not use B_dR, B_cris, B_st, A_inf, the FF curve, or the perfectoid tilt as the paper carrier.
* Do not use the L-extension product Haar as the paper Theta hull; it is a value-level auxiliary on a different field.
* Do not use the completion root of qVFamily; S342 proves it is impossible, and no root stack/gerbe fixes the valuation.
* Do not use S392's function monoid as O^triangle_{K_v}.
* Do not use the closure of the union as the paper holomorphic hull.
* Do not use the SignedLabel uniform average as the paper l* procession average.
* Do not replace the paper hull log by the unit ball log 0 or by a Dirac counting measure.
* Do not change the target inequality or add a correction term.
