## 2. General obstruction: the paper quantity is not a mirror invariant

There are five independent mismatches. They explain the verdicts of sections
3-8 and are not repaired by choosing a better mirror-symmetry theorem.

(O1) Category mismatch. The paper hull is a compact open subset of a
finite-dimensional Q_p-vector space / tensor block. The log-volume is a Haar
measure normalized by the unit lattice. SYZ, HMS, tropical Lagrangians, and
theta-function mirror constructions live on complex/symplectic Calabi-Yau or
log Calabi-Yau varieties (or their non-archimedean analytifications). There is
no actual family, no holomorphic volume form, no symplectic form, and no
Lagrangian in the paper data.

(O2) Measure mismatch. Even for the Tate curve, where the local geometry is
genuinely present, the IUT measure is the group Haar measure on the completion
units (or on K_v), not the Chambert-Loir canonical measure or the skeleton
Lebesgue measure on the curve's Berkovich analytification. For a ball
{ |x| <= p^(-c) } the Haar log-volume is -c log p, while the canonical/
skeleton measure of the corresponding analytic subset has a different
normalization (additive/Lebesgue, not exponential in c). These are different
measures and different normalizations; no theorem in the cited literature
identifies them on the IUT data.

(O3) Carrier mismatch. The actual root carrier is the finite extension
rootNormedExtension; the Berkovich space of a finite extension field is a
single point. The root stack/gerbe language models the mu_{2l} inertia, but
the paper O^tri also carries the log-shell/positivity submonoid and the
qSection reflection (S680), which a root stack does not supply.

(O4) Normalization mismatch. The paper comparison is a procession-normalized
average (PaperSignedHull.hull_logVolume_eq_average), not the raw measure of a
set. A mirror/skeleton/period construction produces a measure or a period; it
does not produce the procession average. The identification of the two is
precisely the missing paper normalization obligation.

(O5) Period vs. volume. A period is an integral of a differential form over a
cycle (or a p-adic period such as the Tate period log q). The paper Theta
quantity is the logarithm of a Haar measure of a lattice hull, i.e. a volume.
Volumes can be periods in special situations, but here the measure is Haar on
a Q_p-vector space and the normalized log is a linear form in the lattice
exponents; it is already the finite formula of section 1.1, not an integral of
a holomorphic form over a cycle.

A useful way to state the situation: the q-side is genuinely period-like (the
valuation of the Tate period; section 9), while the Theta side is a Haar
volume with a procession normalization. The paper inequality compares a
period-like quantity with a volume-like quantity. This is exactly why a
mirror-symmetry equivalence cannot simply transport it: the two sides are
different kinds of invariants.

## 3. Route A: non-archimedean SYZ / Berkovich skeleta

### 3.1 Concrete objects and theorems

- Maximally degenerate Calabi-Yau X over a non-archimedean field; essential
  skeleton Sk(X); retraction rho : X^an -> Sk(X).
- Nicaise-Xu-Yu, The non-archimedean SYZ fibration, Compositio Math. 155
  (2019) 953-972, arXiv:1802.00287: for maximally degenerate CY varieties the
  non-archimedean SYZ fibration is an affinoid torus fibration away from a
  codimension-two subset of the base, confirming a Kontsevich-Soibelman
  prediction; they describe the integral affine structure.
- Kontsevich-Soibelman, Homological mirror symmetry and torus fibrations,
  arXiv:math/0011041 (torus fibrations, wall-crossing, non-archimedean
  mirrors).
- Gubler, Tropical varieties for non-archimedean analytic spaces, Invent.
  Math. 169 (2007) 321-376, arXiv:math/0609383: Chambert-Loir canonical
  measures on a closed subvariety of a totally degenerate abelian variety
  induce piecewise Haar measures on the associated tropical variety.
- Chambert-Loir, Mesures et equidistribution sur les espaces de Berkovich,
  J. reine angew. Math. 595 (2006): canonical measures.
- Mikhalkin-Zharkov, Tropical curves, their Jacobians and theta functions,
  arXiv:math/0612267: tropical Jacobians, tropical theta function, tropical
  Riemann-Roch and theta divisor theorems.
- Tate curve: for E_q = K^x / q^Z with |q| < 1, the Berkovich
  analytification retracts to a skeleton circle Sigma = R / v(q) Z whose
  length is v(q) = -log|q| in the additive normalization (Baker-Payne-
  Rabinoff, On the structure of non-archimedean analytic curves, Contemp.
  Math. 605 (2013); Bosch-Lutkebohmert-Raynaud, Neron Models, Ch. 9).

### 3.2 What it could give

In the Tate-curve case the q-pilot log is literally the length of the SYZ base
circle: q-pilot log = ord(q) log p = v(q) = length(Sigma). The Theta labels
j in F_l^x index torsion translates of the skeleton; the mu_{2l} Kummer
indeterminacy is the 2l-torsion of the local multiplicative group; the hull
is a union of balls whose images are intervals/cubes. This produces a precise
dictionary: q-side = tropical period; Theta side = tropical volume.

### 3.3 Why it is not paper-faithful

1. Ambient object. The IUT hull lives in ArithmeticTensorTupleBlock (a tensor
   product of completions) with the fractional/holomorphic lattices. It is not
   a subset of X^an for a CY X; no such X is constructed in the paper or the
   repo. The coefficient field K_v has a one-point Berkovich space, and the
   finite-dimensional tensor block is not a maximally degenerate CY; the
   skeleton exists only for the curve E_q, a different space.
2. Measure. rootThetaHullLog is the degree-normalized Haar measure of
   arithmeticTupleNormalizedLogVolume, normalized by O_hol. The
   Chambert-Loir/Gubler measure is a measure on X^an, with canonical
   normalization; no theorem identifies the two, and the ball computation
   shows they are genuinely different (O2).
3. Procession. The paper quantity is the procession average
   PaperSignedHull.procession.average, not the ambient measure alone. The
   skeleton measure knows nothing about the finite label procession or the
   mu_{2l} Kummer equivalence.
4. Root extension. The paper root q[2u] lives in the finite extension
   rootNormedExtension (or in the root stack O^tri); the finite extension's
   Berkovich space is one point, so there is no skeleton on which to compare
   its norm.
5. No family/section. There is no actual maximally degenerate CY family over
   each K_v with the Theta-pilot as a section. The IUT Theta-pilot is a formal
   theta value in the Frobenioid, not a section of a CY.

### 3.4 What a positive bridge would have to prove

A theorem of the form:

> There is an actual family X -> Spec K_v with maximally degenerate reduction,
> an actual section/cycle gamma, a measure-preserving map from the paper hull
> to a skeleton subset, and an equality
> paperRootThetaHullLog = (1/D) * integral_{Sk(X)} ... including the
> 1/[K_v:(F_mod)_v] procession weights.

No such family, map, or equality exists in the repo or in the cited
literature. The skeleton route is rejected as a paper-faithful construction;
the Tate-curve dictionary is retained only as auxiliary (section 9).

## 4. Route B: SYZ special Lagrangians and central charge = period

### 4.1 Concrete objects and theorems

- SYZ conjecture: a Calabi-Yau X admits a special Lagrangian T^n fibration;
  the mirror is the dual torus fibration (Strominger-Yau-Zaslow, Mirror
  symmetry is T-duality, Nuclear Phys. B 479 (1996); arXiv:hep-th/9606040).
- Holomorphic volume form Omega; special Lagrangian L is calibrated by
  Re Omega; central charge Z(L) = integral_L Omega is a period; the volume of
  L equals |Z(L)| in the Calabi-Yau metric.
- For an elliptic curve the SYZ fibers are circles; the mirror is the dual
  elliptic curve; the period is 2 pi i tau (complex) or log q (p-adic Tate
  parameter).

### 4.2 What it could give

A genuine equality "volume of a special Lagrangian fiber = period" and, in the
Tate case, "q-pilot log = valuation of the period". This is the strongest
positive statement available for the q-side.

### 4.3 Why it is not paper-faithful

1. The paper has no Calabi-Yau metric, no Omega, no special Lagrangian, and no
   calibrated cycle; the Theta hull is a union of lattice balls, not a
   minimal/calibrated submanifold.
2. The paper target is an inequality of log-volumes with a specific
   procession normalization, not an equality of periods; even if a period
   identity held, it would not give the inequality direction or the
   1/[K_v:(F_mod)_v] weights.
3. T-duality exchanges the Kaehler parameter q with the complexified
   parameter (or the dual torus); the inequality is not mirror-invariant.
   Transporting a mirror inequality back would require an orientation and
   normalization theorem that the paper does not contain.
4. The Theta side is not a period: it is the Haar log-volume of a hull (O5).
   There is no cycle whose integral equals -|log Theta|.

Verdict: rejected. The q-side period picture is correct but is already the
paper's ord(q) log p; the Theta side remains a volume.

## 5. Route C: tropical Lagrangians, amoebas, tropical theta, Newton-Okounkov

### 5.1 Concrete objects and theorems

- Tropicalization trop : (K^x)^n -> R^n; amoeba Log(V) of a complex
  hypersurface; Mikhalkin's correspondence theorem and the tropical
  vertex/Newton-polytope correspondence; the complement components of the
  amoeba correspond to vertices of the Newton polytope
  (Forsberg-Passare-Tsikh; Mikhalkin, Enumerative tropical algebraic geometry
  in R^2, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 18 (2005)).
- Tropical theta functions and tropical Jacobians (Mikhalkin-Zharkov,
  arXiv:math/0612267): the tropical theta function is a convex piecewise
  linear function on a real torus; the tropical theta divisor theorem.
- Newton-Okounkov bodies: Vol(Delta(D)) = lim_n d! h^0(X, nD)/n^d
  (Kaveh-Khovanskii, Ann. of Math. 176 (2012); Lazarsfeld-Mustata, Ann. Sci.
  ENS 42 (2009)).
- Tropical Lagrangians: Lagrangian submanifolds of toric varieties whose
  moment-map image is a tropical polyhedral complex, with Floer-theoretic
  mirrors (Matessi, Lagrangian submanifolds from tropical hypersurfaces,
  arXiv:1804.01469; Abouzaid, Family Floer cohomology and mirror symmetry,
  ICM 2014).

### 5.2 What it could give

The valuation image of a lattice coset F(c) * O_hol is a cube/polyhedron in
the tropical base; the Haar mass is p^(-sum c_i), and the normalized log is
-(sum c_i) log p. This is exactly the "tropical volume" intuition. The union
over labels is a union of cubes (nested when the coefficients are
nonnegative), so the hull log is piecewise linear in the coefficient.

### 5.3 Why it is not paper-faithful

1. The computation is already done. The actual finite-lattice theorem
   arithmeticTupleNormalizedLogVolume_fractionalLattice_mul_holomorphic
   computes the log-volume exactly. The "tropical" content is not missing; it
   is the existing Lean theorem. The exact gap is not a tropical volume
   computation but the set equality paperRootThetaHull = F(c_paper) * O_hol
   plus the existence of the paper-coefficient generator. That generator is a
   2l-th root of q in the completion, excluded by S342.
2. No complex amoeba/Newton-Okounkov body enters. The paper hull is a p-adic
   lattice region, not the amoeba of a complex hypersurface and not the
   asymptotic Hilbert-function body of a line bundle. Newton-Okounkov volumes
   are asymptotic h^0 invariants; the paper log is a finite Haar log.
3. Tropical Lagrangian theorems do not imply p-adic lattice identities. They
   are about Lagrangians/Floer theory in toric symplectic manifolds; they do
   not construct the actual fractional lattice, its generator, or the
   procession normalization.
4. The root carrier is separate. The q[2u] root is a Kummer/root-stack object;
   a tropical Lagrangian body does not supply the root, the norm, or the
   qSection reflection.

Verdict: rejected as a replacement. Keep the existing lattice theorem and the
exact gap; do not restate the gap as a tropical/amoeba statement.

## 6. Route D: HMS, Fourier-Mukai, finite Heisenberg

### 6.1 Concrete objects and theorems

- Homological mirror symmetry for elliptic curves:
  D^b(Coh E) ~ Fuk(E^dual) (Polishchuk-Zaslow, Homological mirror symmetry
  for the elliptic curve, 1998; published in Symplectic Geometry and Mirror
  Symmetry, World Sci. 2001). The theta line bundle and the skyscraper sheaf
  are exchanged by the Fourier-Mukai transform.
- HMS for toric varieties: Abouzaid-Fukaya-Oh-Ohta-Ono (2010); Fukaya,
  Mirror symmetry of abelian varieties and multi-theta functions, J.
  Algebraic Geom. 11 (2002): theta functions as a basis of the mirror.
- Finite Heisenberg group at level 2l and the Weil representation; the labels
  j in F_l^x, the quadratic exponent j^2, and the mu_{2l} action are the
  finite shadow of the theta/Heisenberg structure.

### 6.2 What it could give

The label space and the quadratic exponent are exactly the finite
Heisenberg/theta data; HMS explains why the labels form a basis and why the
mu_{2l} action is the natural indeterminacy. For an elliptic curve, the theta
functions are the images of skyscrapers under Fourier-Mukai.

### 6.3 Why it is not paper-faithful

1. Categorical, not metric. HMS is an equivalence of derived/Fukaya
   categories. It does not preserve Haar measures, log-volumes, the
   1/finrank normalization, the unit reference O_hol, or the procession
   weights. A derived equivalence cannot prove an inequality in R.
2. Fourier-Mukai is not measure-preserving. Even in the exact elliptic curve
   model, the Fourier-Mukai transform exchanges a point with a theta line
   bundle; the Haar measure of a hull of points/lattices is not an invariant
   of the equivalence.
3. The finite model is already tested and negative. S689
   (S689_GAUSS_ORBIT_MEMO.md) computes the mu_{2l} orbit average of the theta
   values: for nonzero labels the finite Fourier/Gauss average vanishes; the
   Gauss sums have constant modulus sqrt(4l) and are phase-cancellation
   objects, not positive volume estimates. The label action is already
   modeled (S356/S392, Kummer action actuals); HMS adds nothing to the
   comparison.
4. No mirror partner for the paper object. The paper's Theta-pilot is not an
   object of D^b(Coh E) or of a Fukaya category; it is a formal theta value in
   the Frobenioid. There is no mirror functor defined on it.

Verdict: rejected; the finite Heisenberg/Weil structure is auxiliary and
already explored (with a negative result).

## 7. Route E: GHK/Gross-Siebert theta functions as periods

### 7.1 Concrete objects and theorems

- Gross-Hacking-Keel, Mirror symmetry for log Calabi-Yau surfaces I, Publ.
  IHES 122 (2015) 65-168, arXiv:1106.4977: for a log Calabi-Yau surface (Y, D),
  the mirror family is built from counts of rational curves; theta functions
  are canonical sections defined via broken lines/scattering diagrams.
- Gross-Siebert, From real affine geometry to complex geometry, Ann. of Math.
  174 (2011) 1301-1428, arXiv:math/0703822: the reconstruction and
  tropical/scattering description; theta functions are flat/canonical
  sections in the large complex structure limit.
- This is the closest genuine "theta = period" story: theta functions are
  canonical bases/periods of a mirror family, with tropical leading terms.

### 7.2 Why it is not paper-faithful

1. Different theta. The IUT theta is the classical theta function of the
   elliptic curve with the q^(j^2) (more precisely q[2u]^(j^2))
   normalization. GHK theta functions are canonical sections of a log CY
   surface's mirror family, defined by disk counts/broken lines. No theorem
   identifies the IUT theta value with a GHK theta function.
2. Different family. The paper has no log Calabi-Yau surface (Y, D), no
   scattering diagram, and no mirror family. The IUT local geometry at a bad
   place is a Tate curve, not a log CY surface with an anticanonical cycle.
3. Different normalization. GHK theta functions are not normalized by the
   procession 1/[K_v:(F_mod)_v] weights or by the mu_{2l} root ambiguity; the
   paper comparison would not be preserved.
4. No measure comparison. Even if a GHK theta function were identified with
   the IUT theta, the paper's hull log-volume is a Haar log of the log-shell
   hull, not the period/coefficient of the mirror family.

Verdict: rejected. The "theta = period" theorem is real but for a different
object; record the exact missing bridge (a log CY family whose period is the
paper theta value and whose canonical measure matches the procession-normalized
Haar), but do not pursue it as a paper-faithful route.

## 8. Route F: root stacks, gerbes, B-fields, and the q[2u] carrier

### 8.1 Concrete objects and theorems

- Root stack X_{D,2l} of a divisor/line bundle (Cadman, Using stacks to impose
  tangency conditions on curves, Amer. J. Math. 129 (2007);
  Abramovich-Graber-Vistoli, Gromov-Witten theory of Deligne-Mumford stacks,
  Amer. J. Math. 130 (2008)): the stack of 2l-th roots, with mu_{2l} inertia.
- B-field gerbes and D-branes (Kapustin, D-branes in a topologically
  nontrivial B-field, Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 4 (2000)); gerby mirror
  symmetry / torsion B-fields.
- The mu_{2l} Kummer ambiguity is a torsor of 2l-th roots; the root stack is
  its quotient/stackification.

### 8.2 What it could give

A clean model of the mu_{2l} inertia and of the q[2u] root as a twisted
sector/gerbe section. This is exactly the "root stack" side of the task.

### 8.3 Why it is not paper-faithful

1. O^tri is not an arbitrary root stack. S680
   (S680OtriUnitsFromLogShellSectionsActual.lean) records the actual
   requirements: OtriUnitsSections with qUnit, rootUnit,
   root_pow : rootUnit^(2l) = qUnit in
   Units (LinearBaseIdentityEndomorphism A), plus splitting,
   structuralSheafCondition, and qSection_reflects_qVFamily. A root stack
   supplies the inertia but not the log-shell/positivity submonoid, the
   characteristic splitting, or the qSection reflection.
2. The actual carrier already exists. rootNormedExtension /
   rootNormedElement is the actual finite-extension root with the power
   relation and the norm p^(-c_norm/(2l)); it is the honest carrier for the
   root in the current model. Replacing it by a gerbe would lose the norm and
   the qSection equations.
3. Candidate root-stack models are incomplete/toy. S355 quotients all roots
   to one point (degenerate); S356/S392 give an action groupoid and a
   function-space monoid, but not the paper O^tri multiplication or the
   q[1u]/q[2u] section identification (FIVE_PAPER_GAPS_STATUS.md, gap 1).
   S392's qSection_toCompletion is the constant function to qVFamily.q, which
   is a value-level model, not the paper section equality.
4. Gerbe mirror symmetry does not supply a comparison theorem. Even a gerby
   mirror would identify the gerbe with another gerbe; the paper's log-volume
   comparison needs the actual Haar/procession normalization, not a gerbe
   equivalence.

Verdict: rejected as a paper-faithful carrier. Keep rootNormedExtension; the
root stack/gerbe may remain an auxiliary model of the mu_{2l} torsor only.

## 9. The one honest positive statement: the Tate-curve/tropical dictionary

This section is the only place where the mirror-symmetry picture gives a true
statement. It is auxiliary, not a proof of the paper target.

### 9.1 q-side is a tropical period

For the local Tate curve E_q = K^x / q^Z with |q| < 1:

- the Berkovich skeleton is a circle Sigma = R / v(q) Z;
- its length is v(q) = -log|q| (additive normalization);
- with the p-adic normalization v(p) = log p, the length is ord(q) log p,
  which is exactly the paper qPilotClassicalLog per bad place;
- the Tate period is log q (the p-adic period of the uniformization); its
  valuation is v(q). Thus the q-pilot log is the valuation of the Tate
  period, i.e. a tropical period.

This is a precise, citable statement (Tate/Mumford uniformization;
Baker-Payne-Rabinoff for the skeleton length).

### 9.2 Theta side is a lattice/tropical volume, not a period

For a bad place b, a ball/fractional lattice region F(c) * O_hol in the
completion has (for the exact fractional-lattice scaling data)

  mu_log(F(c) * O_hol) = -(sum_i c_i) log p

by the actual theorem
arithmeticTupleNormalizedLogVolume_fractionalLattice_mul_holomorphic.
The right-hand side is linear in the tropical exponents c_i and log p. This
is the "tropical volume" of the region, but it is a Haar volume, not an
integral of a form over a cycle. The exponential relation
mu(ball) = p^(-c) mu(O_hol) shows that the Haar measure is not the
Lebesgue/skeleton measure: the latter is additive in c, the former is
exponential.

The actual root model uses c_root = c_norm/(2l) and the extension
rootNormedExtension; the paper coefficient c_paper = order/(2l) would need a
completion root, which is impossible (S342). This is the exact content of the
q[2u]/root-stack gap.

### 9.3 What the dictionary does and does not explain

It explains:

- why the q-side has the shape sum_v ord(q_v) log p_v (skeleton length);
- why the Theta side is a lattice/ball volume with quadratic label exponents
  j^2 c_root (torsion translates on the skeleton; theta labels);
- why the comparison is a comparison of two tropical volumes and why the
  existing Lean containment theorem qPilotClassicalLog_le_rootThetaHullLog is
  true (the qPilot region is nested inside the root hull).

It does not explain or supply:

- the exact PaperRootHullFormulaGap (paper coefficient hull equality);
- the paper q[2u] root carrier in O^tri;
- the procession normalization PaperSignedHull.hull_logVolume_eq_average;
- the paper-level Ind1/Ind2/Ind3 and Theta-link fields;
- the coefficient (l+1)/24 normalization issue (S711), which is an
  arithmetic/manuscript-consistency question independent of mirror symmetry.

A toy theorem one could state (research only, no Lean claim):

> In the rank-one split model, if c_root >= 0 and the label set is the paper
> set {+-1, ..., +-l*} (zero label excluded), the union_j F(j^2 c_root) * O_hol
> is the largest region F(c_root) * O_hol; its normalized log is
> -c_root log p. The paper coefficient c_paper = order/(2l) cannot be realized
> in the completion (S342); the extension root realizes c_norm/(2l) instead.

Caveat: the Lean SignedLabel type in RootHullContainmentInequalityActual also
contains 0. If the zero label is included, the union collapses to
F(0) * O_hol and the hull log is 0; this is the zero-label artifact recorded
in CURRENT_PATH_NOTES S24 (the paper Cor 3.12 average is over F_l^x and
excludes 0). The toy statement above is therefore about the paper label set,
not about the raw SignedLabel union.

This toy statement is essentially the existing Lean computation plus S342; it
is not a new bridge and should not be presented as the paper theorem.
