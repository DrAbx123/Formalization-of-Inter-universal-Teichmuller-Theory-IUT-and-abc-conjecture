# S601 tau/π breakthrough vs paper (deviation audit)

## What is actually constructed

* positiveUnit qOrder from surjectivity of an abstract normalized valuation V
  on coverField A.base.
* positiveQvPhiPreimage: divisor ULift.up qOrder, rationalFunction the unit,
  divisor_eq by positiveUnit_divisor_eval.
* QvPhiQOrderFields / qOrderEndomorphismOfFields: a
  LinearBaseIdentityEndomorphism with divisor qOrder and rationalFunction the unit.
* tau = Submonoid.powers qOrderEndomorphism.

These are actual Lean objects (S591/S575/S593/S601/S597/S598/S599).

## Where it does NOT yet match the paper

1. qOrder source.
   qOrder = (qVFamilyK T b).order.  qVFamilyK is the base change of the
   F-side Clause B qVFamily.  The paper q_v is the K_v-side q-parameter
   (q[1u]v[1u]); the identification qVFamilyK = paper q_v is NOT proved.
   The paper's root uses the K_v/extension q[2u], not this F-side candidate.

2. Phi.
   The model uses a constant ULift Nat divisor monoid with ramification
   pullback.  Paper Phi_Cv is the divisor monoid ord(O_L^tri)^pf of the
   p_v-adic Frobenioid C_v; Phi_Cv^vdash = N log_Phi(q_v) is a submonoid
   generated by the image of q_v.  The identification of the model Phi with
   paper Phi_Cv / Phi_Cv^vdash is NOT proved.

3. positiveUnit is not canonical q_v.
   positiveUnit is chosen by valuation surjectivity, not by the q_v image
   or by the special 2-torsion/evaluation normalization.  There is no proof
   that its divisor is log_Phi(q_v), only that its valuation exponent is qOrder.

4. tau = Submonoid.powers is not a characteristic splitting.
   CharacteristicSplitting requires O^x x tau ~= O^triangle,
   multiplication_bijective, units_commute, isotropicHull_mem_range.
   S603 shows divisor divisibility for arbitrary a is NOT proved; the unit
   decomposition gap remains.  So tau is a submonoid, not a paper
   characteristic splitting.

5. qHalf / special 2-torsion.
   The algebraic qHalf = theta^l is actual, but the paper's evaluation at the
   special 2-torsion point / constant multiple rigidity normalization is exact
   missing.  qHalf is not yet the paper q[2u] section.

## Verdict

The breakthrough is a real Lean construction of a qOrder element and a
powers submonoid, but it is NOT yet the paper characteristic splitting tau
and NOT yet paper q_v/Phi_Cv.  It is a candidate/model-level construction.

To become paper-level one must prove:
  qVFamilyK = paper K_v q_v;
  model Phi = paper Phi_Cv / Phi_Cv^vdash;
  positiveUnit / qOrder = image of q_v;
  unit decomposition and CharacteristicSplitting laws;
  special 2-torsion evaluation/normalization of qHalf.
