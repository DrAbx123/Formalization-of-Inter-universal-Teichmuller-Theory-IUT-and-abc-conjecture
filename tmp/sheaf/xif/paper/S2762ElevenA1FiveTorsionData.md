## 2. 5-torsion basis

Exact rational generator:

```text
P = (5,5) in E(Q), order 5, 5P = O   (exact rational arithmetic in the test).
```

A basis of E[5] over F5 is `(P, Q)` for any Q completing P to a basis of E[5].
The repository does NOT provide an explicit Q or its coordinates; it only gives
the rational generator P and the dimension count.  Choosing Q requires a
division-polynomial/5-torsion computation (not in the repo).

## 3. Frobenius matrices in that basis

Because P is rational, it is fixed by G_Q.  In the basis `(P, Q)` every
Frobenius at unramified q has the form

```text
rho_q = [[1, c_q], [0, q mod 5]]   in GL2(F5),
```

with determinant `q mod 5` (cyclotomic character) and trace

```text
tr(rho_q) = 1 + q mod 5.
```

This is exactly the congruence asserted in the Rust test for
q = 2,3,7,13,17,19,23,29,31.  The off-diagonal entry `c_q` depends on the choice
of Q; the repository does NOT record the matrices `rho_q` themselves, only
their traces.

## 4. Matrices generating SL2(F5): NONE

The mod-5 image is contained in the Borel subgroup of upper triangular
matrices, because the line `<P>` is a trivial subrepresentation.  Therefore:

```text
NO finite set of matrices in the image of rho generates SL2(F5);
the image cannot contain SL2(F5).
```

This is not a missing computation: 11a1 at p=5 has a rational 5-torsion point,
so `SerreStyleLargeImageStatement 11a1 5` is FALSE.  The target in S2760 is
therefore not merely unproved, it is false at l=5.

## 5. Consequence / next curve-l choice

To get an SL2(F5) image, do NOT use 11a1 at l=5.  Options:

- 11a1 at l=7: E(Q)[7] = 0 (torsion is Z/5), so the obstruction disappears;
  still needs a 7-torsion basis/Frobenius computation.
- Another non-CM curve with no rational 5-torsion (e.g. 37a1, 14a1) at l=5.
- Any CM curve is excluded.

The repository's 11a1/p=5 data is exact and useful for Selmer, but it cannot
supply SL2(F5) generators.

## 6. Verdict

- 5-torsion generator: exact `(5,5)`.
- Basis completion Q: NOT in repo; exact object gap.
- Frobenius matrices: only triangular form [[1,c_q],[0,q]] and traces 1+q mod5;
  no c_q/matrices stored.
- SL2(F5) generating set: EMPTY (image in Borel); l=5 target false.
- Recommend 11a1 at l=7 (or another non-CM no-rational-5-torsion curve) for
  Gap2; do not claim Serre large image for 11a1/l=5.
