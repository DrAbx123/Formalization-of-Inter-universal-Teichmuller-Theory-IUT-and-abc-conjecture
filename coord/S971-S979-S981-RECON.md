# S971-S979-S981-RECON — tensorRegionHull vs actualC3 region (A1125)

Status: read-only; no compile.

## 1. What S971 defines

S971TensorRegionImageActual:33-69:

- tensorThetaRegionImage T b :=
    qVFamToLValued (T b) '' paperRootThetaImageNZ (T b)
  (the paper lambda-O possible-image union, pushed to the L-valued tensor carrier).
- tensorThetaRegionIndUnion T b :=
    tensorThetaRegionImage T b union thetaImageIndUnion T b
  (region image plus actual Ind point images).
- tensorRegionHull D :=
    closure (tensorThetaRegionIndUnion T b)
  with the topology D.topologicalSpace supplied by FinalLTensorLogVolumeData.

Carrier: FinalLValuedCarrier T b, NOT C3LogShellTensorBlockL.
Region: paper theta image union + Ind images, NOT actualC3LogShellTensorRegionL
(which is tensorIntegralRegion of local integralLogShells).
So tensorRegionHull is NOT the hull of actualC3LogShellTensorRegion; it is a
closure of the paper possible-image union on a different carrier.

## 2. Containment directions

S971:46-53:
- qPilotPaperImage_subset_tensorRegionImage:
    qPilotPaperImage ⊆ tensorThetaRegionImage.
- qPilotPaperImage_subset_tensorRegionIndUnion:
    qPilotPaperImage ⊆ tensorThetaRegionIndUnion.

S971:61-68:
- qPilotPaperImage_subset_tensorRegionHull:
    qPilotPaperImage ⊆ tensorRegionHull D
  (subset_closure of the previous inclusion).

Direction: q-pilot image lies INSIDE the theta-region closure. This is the
q ⊆ theta direction (q-pilot is the j=1 theta image; paperRootThetaImageNZ is
the union over all j). It is NOT theta ⊆ q and NOT our tensorIntegerRegionL
subset actualC3LogShellTensorRegionL.

## 3. S979: normalized inequality

S962TensorPacketPaperInequalityActual:35-39:
  tensorNormalizedLogVolume D S :=
    log (D.measure S).toReal - log (D.measure D.unitRegion).toReal.
  qPilotTensorLog D := tensorNormalizedLogVolume D qPilotPaperImage.
  paperTensorHullLog D := tensorNormalizedLogVolume D paperTensorHull.

S979TensorRegionHullInequalityActual:53-66:
  tensorRegionHullLog D := tensorNormalizedLogVolume D (tensorRegionHull D).
  qPilotTensorLog_le_tensorRegionHullLog:
    qPilotTensorLog D <= tensorRegionHullLog D
  assuming
    0 < D.measure qPilotPaperImage
    and D.measure (tensorRegionHull D) < top.

This is exactly the sign of our target (q <= hull), but in the S956/S962
normalization: measure is relative to D.unitRegion, and carrier is
FinalLValuedCarrier. It is not the S2807 R = -haarLogVolume(actualC3Region).

## 4. S981: hull subset unitRegion

S981TensorRegionHullFiniteActual:41-53:
  S981TensorRegionHullSubsetUnitRegion D := tensorRegionHull D subset D.unitRegion.
  tensorRegionHull_measure_lt_top_of_subset:
    subset D.unitRegion -> D.measure (tensorRegionHull D) < top
  (because D.unit_lt_top : D.measure D.unitRegion < top).

Meaning: the hull lies in the positive-finite unit region, so its measure is
finite. This is a finiteness condition only; it is not an inclusion in
actualC3LogShellTensorRegionL, and it is not a normalization mu(O)=1.
D.unitRegion is an arbitrary field of FinalLTensorLogVolumeData with
unit_pos/unit_lt_top; no relation to the local O tensor region.

S981:56-62:
  qPilotTensorLog_le_tensorRegionHullLog_of_subset:
    positivity of qPilot image + hull subset unitRegion => the S979
    per-b inequality.

## 5. Can these three green files directly give our variant?

Target ingredients:
- qPilotTensorLog = -A = qPilot.lhs?
- tensorRegionHullLog = R = -haarLogVolume(actualC3Region)?
- carrier FinalLValuedCarrier = C3LogShellTensorBlockL?
- D.measure = actualC3LogShellTensorHaarL?
- D.unitRegion = tensorIntegerRegionL?

All are missing. What S971/S979/S981 actually give:
- an actual containment qPilotPaperImage subset tensorRegionHull on the paper
  carrier (good direction);
- a conditional normalized inequality qPilotTensorLog <= tensorRegionHullLog
  (good sign);
- a finite-measure criterion via hull subset unitRegion.

They do NOT provide:
- identification of tensorRegionHull with actualC3LogShellTensorRegionL/hull;
- identification of tensorNormalizedLogVolume with S2807's -R;
- identification of qPilotTensorLog with -A;
- normalization mu(tensor O)=1;
- the local O tensor region inclusion (that is Mzk1TensorRegionNormalizationActual).

## 7. Verdict

- tensorRegionHull is NOT the hull of actualC3LogShellTensorRegion; it is the
  closure of the paper theta image union on FinalLValuedCarrier.
- qPilotPaperImage subset tensorRegionHull is the q-inside-theta direction; it
  supports the same sign as our target but not our objects.
- tensorRegionHull subset unitRegion is only a finiteness condition, not an
  O-normalization and not the actualC3 region inclusion.
- The three green files cannot directly give our variant containment/
  normalization; they need carrier, qPilot, hull and measure bridges.
- The local O-inclusion and tensor monotonicity are in
  Mzk1TensorRegionNormalizationActual (A1124), not in S971/S979/S981.
