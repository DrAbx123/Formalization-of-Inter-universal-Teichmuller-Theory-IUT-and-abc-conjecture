## Proved

S2138H1Tower is an abstract tower of finite commutative H^1 groups with
transition maps.  S2138IterProj is the iterated transition.
S2138MittagLeffler is the range-stabilization condition for this tower.
S2138MittagLeffler_of_surjective proves: if every transition map is
surjective, then the tower satisfies Mittag-Leffler.
S2138ActualMuH1Tower adds the surjectivity field; its ML theorem follows
without further input.

This is the formal ML half.  It does not assert the actual arithmetic tower.
