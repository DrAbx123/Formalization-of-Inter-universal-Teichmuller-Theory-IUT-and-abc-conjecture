## 2. Why the field-case proofs do not apply, and what replaces them

S1746/S1770 prove G2 over a field by showing that every subalgebra of a
finite etale K-algebra is finite etale.  This is false over a Dedekind
coordinate ring: e.g. over k[t], the subalgebra k[t][s]/(s^2 - t^2) of
k[t] x k[t] (s |-> (t,-t)) is finite free but not finite etale (its
discriminant is 4t^2, not a unit).  R' is a Dedekind coordinate ring, so the
field-case lemma is not available.  S1771/S1785/S1811 prove G3 over a field
by decomposing finite etale K-algebras into products of finite separable
fields; over R' the connected components of a finite etale algebra are
Dedekind domains, not fields, so that decomposition also does not apply.

The replacements are genuine general-ring arguments:

### G2: Galois descent for invariant subalgebras

`FiniteEtaleFixedPointsGeneral.fixedPointSubalgebra_finiteEtale` (already in
the tree, in `LeanFormal/IUT/Foundations/AlgebraicGeometry/`) proves the
general-ring fixed-point theorem.  Its route is:

* split A by a finite faithfully flat etale cover T using Mathlib's
  `Algebra.IsFiniteSplit.exists_tensorProduct_of_etale` (Lenstra 5.10);
* over T, A becomes (T)^n and the induced action is a permutation action;
  the fixed subalgebra is the subalgebra of functions constant on orbits;
* the fixed-point construction commutes with the flat base change T/R;
* `Algebra.Etale.of_etale_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat` descends the
  finite-etale property back to R.

No `|G|`-invertibility is used.  This file then builds the invariant object,
the structural maps and the full limit cone over the action diagram, proving
the categorical quotient statement in the opposite category.

### G3: surjectivity, idempotent splitting, categorical transfer

* Epi -> surjective: for an epimorphism f : B -> A of finite etale
  R-algebras, the self-pushout A (x)_B A is finite etale over R (S1779's
  tensor cocone), hence a legal test object.  Epimorphy forces the two tensor
  inclusions to agree, which is exactly `Algebra.IsEpi B A`; since A is
  finite over B, Mathlib's
  `Algebra.isEpi_iff_surjective_algebraMap_of_finite` gives surjectivity.
* Surjective -> product projection: the kernel I is an idempotent ideal by
  formal etaleness of A/B.  It is finitely generated (A is finitely presented
  over R, B is finite over R, and a projective linear section of f over R
  writes I as the range of an R-linear endomorphism of B).  A finitely
  generated idempotent ideal is generated by an idempotent e, and the Chinese
  remainder decomposition gives B ~= A x B/(1-e) with f the first projection.
* Categorical transfer: the binary product fan with point B and projections
  f and the complement map is a limit in the full subcategory of finite etale
  algebras; its opposite is the binary coproduct cofan required by
  `S1779MonoMissing`.

## 4. Relationship to S1806

S1806 (`S1806FiniteEtaleRPrimeGaloisActual.lean`) contains a compiling G2
development and the same algebraic G3 core, but its file does **not** compile
as a whole: its final G3 declarations
(`epi_surjective_of_finiteEtale`, `productFan_isLimit`,
`monoInducesIsoOnDirectSummand_finiteEtaleAlg`) are referenced but absent,
so the G3 conclusion there contains `sorryAx` (unknown-constant placeholders).
S1830 does not import S1806; it is self-contained on S1779 plus the general
fixed-point theorem and Mathlib.  S1830 therefore supersedes S1806 for the
S1779 G2/G3 target (S1806 remains useful as an independent draft of G2).
