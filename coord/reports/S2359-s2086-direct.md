## Why S1742/S2161 do not close it

- `S1742StrongTransOfHom`: turns an actual scheme morphism into a strong
  transformation; it does not prove representable finite etaleness of an
  arbitrary cover-to-cover map.
- `S2161SchemeRepresentableMapBaseChange`: proves the scheme-map
  representability API for *scheme morphisms*; it does not cancel finite
  étaleness along a composite of stack morphisms.
- A cover map `g.map : B.source.Hom C.source` is not literally a scheme
  morphism; its finite étaleness must be shown representably at every test
  scheme/point. The available cover data gives `B.finiteEtale` and
  `C.finiteEtale` for the maps to the orbicurve, and S2086 reduces `g.map`
  to the stack cancellation above.
