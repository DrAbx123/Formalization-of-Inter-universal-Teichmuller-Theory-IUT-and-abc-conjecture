## Mathlib availability audit
- Mathlib has finite residue fields and ramification/inertia degrees for number-field ideals (Ideal.inertiaDeg, inertiaDegIn).
- Mathlib has no subgroup-level inertia/wild-inertia decomposition of an absolute Galois group of a local field, no Frobenius lift theorem for G_Qp, no procyclic inertia quotient, no pro-p wild inertia theorem, and no Demushkin presentation.
- The tree's S2116/S2117 comments already record exactly these absences.

## Minimal API (no inhabitant asserted)
To make the Demushkin route precise one would need:
1. `PadicAbsoluteGaloisInertia` : a subgroup I of LocalAbsoluteGalois p with the valuation/residue invariance property;
2. `PadicInertiaQuotientProcyclic` : LocalAbsoluteGalois p / I is procyclic, with a chosen Frobenius lift sigma whose image topologically generates the quotient;
3. `PadicWildInertiaProP` : the wild inertia subgroup is pro-p;
4. `PadicDemushkinGenerators` : elements sigma tau1 tau2 : LocalAbsoluteGalois p whose generated closed subgroup is top, plus the Demushkin relation (needs a free pro-p group API absent from the tree);
5. `padicTopFG_of_demushkin : PadicDemushkinGenerators -> PadicAbsoluteGaloisTopologicallyFinitelyGenerated`.
None of 1-4 is constructed; item 4 in particular requires a pro-p free group/Demushkin presentation not present in Mathlib.

## Exact unprovable points
- No formal definition of the inertia subgroup of G_Qp in the tree.
- No theorem that the inertia quotient is procyclic / generated by a Frobenius lift.
- No theorem that wild inertia is pro-p.
- No Demushkin presentation or free pro-p group API.
- Therefore no inhabitant of `PadicAbsoluteGaloisHasAtMostGenerators 3` or target.
The finiteness-of-extensions statements in S2117 are necessary but not sufficient (explicit counterexample type recorded there).
