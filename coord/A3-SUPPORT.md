## 1. Belyi map beta(z)=4z/(1+z)^2

Belyi map and iterates phi_n = beta^{circ n}.  beta'(z)=4(1-z)/(1+z)^3, so
|phi_n'(0)| = 4^n exactly.  The integral is
    I_r(phi_n) = (1/2pi) int_0^{2pi} log^+ |phi_n(r e^{i theta})| d theta
with r=0.99 and r=0.999, 20000 trapezoid points.

| n | |phi_n'(0)| | I_0.99 | I_0.999 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 | 1.376244 | 1.385294 |
| 2 | 16 | 4.8966e-12 | 4.8161e-17 |
| 3 | 64 | 1.5210e-18 | 7.7716e-20 |
| 4 | 256 | 1.6209e-18 | 3.4417e-19 |
| 5 | 1024 | 2.0206e-18 | 6.2172e-19 |
| 6 | 4096 | 2.2759e-18 | 9.6589e-19 |

Tower reading: the derivative grows by the exact factor 4 per iterate; the log^+-integral
becomes numerically negligible from n=2 onward (values at/below 1e-17 for r=0.999).  These
tiny values are near the double-precision noise level and should be re-run in mpmath before
being used as a sign; the n=1 value is robust.

## 2. Legendre period L(s)=sum_n C(2n,n)^2 s^n

- Claimed radius R_inf = 1/16 = 0.0625.
- Coefficients a_n = C(2n,n)^2 grow like 16^n; numerical ratios:
  n=100: a_101/a_100 = 15.841976;
  n=300: a_301/a_300 = 15.946711;
  last ratio error |ratio-16| = 0.053289.
This is consistent with R_inf=1/16; convergence is slow as expected (O(1/n)).

p-adic radius for u=a/c:
- For every one of the 80 triples in the CSV, at least one prime p | c has
  |u|_p = |a/c|_p >= 1, so the p-adic Legendre series at u is outside its convergence
  disc under the usual |.|_p criterion.
- Family (1,3^k-1,3^k): u=1/3^k, so |u|_3 = 3^k > 1; max |u|_p in the table is
  4.2391e28 at k=60 (p=3).
- Therefore "admissible_padic" = 0/80 in this first pass. This is a numerical obstruction,
  not a theorem; the correct p-adic normalization may differ and must be supplied by A3.

Definitions used (all documented above):
